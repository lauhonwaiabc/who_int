9.0.0.1 Mesenchymal and stromal tumours: Introduction

Although most tumours of the endocrine organs are of epithelial origin, a wide variety of mesenchymal tumours can also occur. Only the most commonly encountered tumours are discussed in this chapter. For entities not found, please refer to the fifth-edition Soft tissue and bone tumours volume of this series. For the more detailed classification and descriptions of fibromas and haemangiomas, please refer to the Skin tumours volume. Radiographic and cytological characteristics of the mesenchymal lesions may mimic epithelial tumours and therefore they should always be considered in the differential diagnosis. As seen in #25489Table 9.01 , the thyroid is the organ with the greatest variety of mesenchymal tumour types reported. Many such tumours have also been reported in the adrenal, pituitary, and pancreas, whereas few have been detected in the parathyroid glands.
