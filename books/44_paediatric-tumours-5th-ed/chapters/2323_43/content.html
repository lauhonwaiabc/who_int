2.3.2.3 Large B-cell lymphoma with IRF4 rearrangement

Definition

Large B-cell lymphoma with IRF4 rearrangement (LBCL-IRF4+) is a de novo large B-cell lymphoma with a follicular, follicular and diffuse, or purely diffuse growth pattern characterized by the strong expression of IRF4 (MUM1), and in most cases associated with an IRF4 translocation with an immunoglobulin gene partner (IGH, IGK, IGL).

ICD-O coding

9698/3 Large B-cell lymphoma with IRF4 rearrangement

ICD-11 coding

2A81.Y & XH6SU8 Other specified diffuse large B-cell lymphomas & Large B-cell lymphoma with IRF4 rearrangement

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Most cases of LBCL-IRF4+ arise the Waldeyer ring or cervical lymph nodes {22700993; 21487109}. Other involved sites are the gastrointestinal tract and (very rarely) other sites {31012208; 32239695; 31738823}.

Clinical features

The most common presentation is a localized tonsillar enlargement or an isolated lymphadenopathy without B symptoms {32239695; 31187530}.

Epidemiology

The incidence of LBCL-IRF4+ among large B-cell lymphomas decreases with age, and most patients are aged < 18 years {22238326}. Two studies show that 6–20% of mature B-cell lymphomas with diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL) or follicular lymphoma morphology represent LBCL-IRF4+ in the paediatric age group {32239695; 31012208}. Males and females are affected almost equally.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

The main pathogenetic events of this lymphoma are probably alterations of the BCL6 / IRF4 / Blimp1 (PRDM1) and NF-κB pathways {31738823}. The mutation landscape and gene expression of LBCL-IRF4+ differs from that of DLBCL-NOS and paediatric-type follicular lymphoma {23445872; 21487109; 31738823}.

Macroscopic appearance

These lymphomas produce a firm, fleshy, homogeneous mass.

Histopathology

The morphology of LBCL-IRF4+ can be diffuse, follicular and diffuse, or purely follicular, resembling the morphological spectrum of DLBCL and follicular lymphoma grade 3. The follicular component, however, differs slightly from a typical paediatric-type follicular lymphoma because the follicles are large and round, and they lack the serpiginous configuration frequently characterizing paediatric-type follicular lymphoma. Cytologically, it is a blastic neoplasm formed by sheets of typical centroblasts and (less frequently) medium-sized blastoid cells with small nucleoli. A starry-sky pattern is usually absent {32452165}.

Immunophenotype

The neoplastic cells have a mature B-cell phenotype with immunoreactivity for all pan–B-cell antigens (CD19, CD20, PAX5, and CD79a). BCL6 is invariably positive, whereas CD10 and BCL2 are found in approximately half of cases. CD5 is expressed in about 30% {21487109; 27616053}. The main characteristic of this lymphoma is the strong expression of IRF4 (MUM1); BLIMP1 is negative {21487109}. These neoplasms usually have a high proliferation index.

Cytology

The cytology is not clinically relevant. Subclassification of DLBCL by FNAB alone is not advised.

Diagnostic molecular pathology

The main feature of LBCL-IRF4+ is a rearrangement of IRF4, most frequently with an IGH locus, or rarely with IGK or an unknown partner {21487109; 31738823}. In rare cases, IRF4 rearrangement cannot be demonstrated by FISH analysis. However, usually an IGH rearrangement is detected in these cases and the diagnosis of LBCL-IRF4+ is accepted if the clinical presentation and immunophenotype are in line with the WHO definition {26980727}. BCL2 and MYC are virtually never rearranged, whereas in a subset of cases, chromosomal breaks affecting the BCL6 locus have been reported {21487109; 31738823}.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential:

  • Suggestive clinical features (paediatric age, head and neck region, localized disease)
  • Mature B-cell phenotype with expression of BCL6 and IRF4, blastic morphology, and follicular and/or diffuse growth pattern
  • IRF4 gene rearrangement (in the absence of demonstration of IRF4 gene rearrangements by FISH, strong IRF4 [MUM1] positivity by immunohistochemistry in combination with the typical pathological features is required)

Desirable:

  • Absence of BCL2 and MYC gene rearrangements

Staging

Staging is performed according to the Revised International Pediatric Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma Staging System (IPNHLSS) {25940716}.

Prognosis and prediction

The overall outcome of LBCL-IRF4+ is favourable in paediatric patients {32239695}. It is uncertain if local management (like in paediatric follicular lymphoma) can be considered a therapeutic option, but a few patients with tonsillar, entirely follicular disease that had been treated by tonsillectomy alone were disease-free and alive at the last reported follow-up {27616053}.
