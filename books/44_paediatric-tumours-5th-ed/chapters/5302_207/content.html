5.3.0.2 Round cell sarcoma with EWSR1::non-ETS fusions

Definition

Round cell sarcomas with EWSR1::non-ETS fusions are round and spindle cell sarcomas with EWSR1 or FUS fusions involving partners unrelated to the ETS gene family, excluding EWSR1-rearranged myoepitheliomas.

ICD-O coding

9366/3 Round cell sarcoma with EWSR1::non-ETS fusions

9366/3 Round cell sarcoma with EWSR1/FUS::NFATC2

9366/3 Round cell sarcoma with EWSR1::PATZ1

ICD-11 coding

2B5F Sarcoma, not elsewhere classified, primary site

Related terminology

Not recommended: Ewing-like sarcoma.

Subtype(s)

Round cell sarcoma with EWSR1/FUS::NFATC2; round cell sarcoma with EWSR1::PATZ1

Localization

NFATC2-rearranged sarcomas are commonly located in the metaphysis/diaphysis of long bones, and a few EWSR1::NFATC2 tumours arise in soft tissue {29431183; 31049020; 31078563}. All five reported paediatric cases arose in long bones {17175374; 24758504; 29431183; 31049020; 32327700}. EWSR1::PATZ1 sarcomas arise in soft tissue {32362012; 31189996; 30379650; 29679497}. EWSR1::PATZ1 tumours of the CNS have been reported {31232935; 29679497; 28912153; 2681007}.

Clinical features

EWSR1/FUS::NFATC2 sarcomas typically manifest as painful, locally destructive bone lesions or as primary soft tissue tumours {29626598; 31078563}. EWSR1::PATZ1 sarcomas manifest as isolated soft tissue masses {31189996; 30379650}.

Epidemiology

EWSR1/FUS::NFATC2 sarcomas occur rarely in children, with only 5 paediatric cases reported (age range: 12–16 years). The age range of all patients is 12–67 years (median: 32.3 years) and there is a male predominance (M:F ratio: 5:1) {31078563; 29661713; 31049020; 30994538}. EWSR1::PATZ1 sarcomas occur at any age (range: 1–81 years, mean: 42 years; 5 cases were in patients aged < 20 years), with an equal sex distribution {32362012; 31189996; 29431183; 10949935}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

The EWSR1::NFATC2 gene fusion, on an amplified derivative ring chromosome 22, induces activation of the NFATC2 transcription factor due to loss of the N-terminal regulatory domain {17175374; 19318479}. The FUS::NFATC2 fusion is not amplified. PATZ1 encodes a zinc finger protein with tumour suppressor functions and transcriptional regulation {29186807; 10949935}. The EWSR1::PATZ1 fusion event is the result of an intrachromosomal paracentric inversion {10949935; 30379650; 31189996}. Expression and methylome profiling reveal that NFATC2-rearranged and EWSR1::PATZ1 sarcomas are transcriptionally distinct subtypes of sarcomas {29431183; 30895378; 25010205; 29416716}.

Macroscopic appearance

EWSR1/FUS::NFATC2 sarcoma is a solid mass of variable size (40–180 mm), with a yellowish-tan, firm or fleshy cut surface. Most are poorly defined and locally destructive, or (more rarely) well circumscribed or confined to the intramedullary cavity {30994538; 29626598; 31078563}.

EWSR1::PATZ1 sarcoma is a solid-cystic mass; reported tumour sizes range from 35 to > 100 mm in greatest dimension {30379650; 31189996}.

Histopathology

EWSR1/FUS::NFATC2 sarcomas are composed of small to medium-sized round and/or spindled cells with limited eosinophilic or clear cytoplasm, predominantly arranged in cords, nests, trabeculae, and pseudoacinar structures in a fibrohyaline or myxohyaline background. Solid areas are often focal {24215322; 30714449; 30994538}. Small round monotonous to markedly pleomorphic nuclei featuring smooth or irregular nuclear contours, dense hyperchromatic or vesicular chromatin, and small or prominent nucleoli have been reported {24215322; 30994538; 30714449}. Tumour necrosis and mitotic activity are variable. Tumour cells diffusely express CD99 (in 50% of cases); NKX2-2 may be expressed, as well as dot-like keratin AE1/AE3 and CD138 {29920735; 28643791}. The emerging markers aggrecan and NKX3-1 may be helpful {32327700; 31972596}.

The histopathological and immunophenotypic features of EWSR1::PATZ1 sarcomas are diverse, with small round and/or spindled cells accompanied by fibrous stroma, variable necrosis, and mitoses {10949935; 29431183; 30379650; 31189996; 32362012}. Coexpression of myogenic (desmin, myogenin, MYOD1) and neurogenic (S100P, SOX10, MITF, GFAP) markers is seen. CD34 and CD99 may be positive.

Cytology

Insufficient data

Diagnostic molecular pathology

EWSR1::NFATC2, FUS::NFATC2, and EWSR1::PATZ1 fusions are best detected by sequence-based fusion assays {30994538; 31049020; 30379650; 31189996}. The EWSR1::PATZ1 fusion is easily missed using EWSR1 break-apart FISH {31189996}.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: spindled to rounded cytomorphology; abundant stromal changes (in most cases), e.g. fibrohyaline, osteogenic, cartilaginous; identification of a fusion transcript remains the gold standard.

Desirable: exclusion of a myoepithelial neoplasm (negativity for keratin and SOX10); for NFATC2-rearranged sarcomas: location in the bone (in most cases); positive staining for aggrecan and/or NKX3-1; for PATZ1-rearranged sarcomas: round/spindled cells with a divergent immunophenotype (myogenic and neurogenic).

Staging

Not established

Prognosis and prediction

For EWSR1/FUS::NFATC2 sarcoma, a minimal histological response was observed in patients treated with Ewing sarcoma chemotherapy {31078563}. Limited follow-up (< 1 year) showed no evidence of disease in 2 paediatric cases {24758504; 31049020}. In adults, metastases were reported as long as 10.5 years after diagnosis, and 2 patients died of disease {30994538}. FUS::NFATC2 sarcoma outcome data are limited, with some patients developing distant metastasis {31049020}. EWSR1::PATZ1 sarcomas behave aggressively, with metastases at or within 2 years of diagnosis (including 1 paediatric case with follow-up), and death within 5–32 months {10949935; 30379650; 31189996}.
