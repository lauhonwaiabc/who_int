5.4.2.7 Mesenchymal chondrosarcoma

Definition

Mesenchymal chondrosarcoma is a biphasic, high-grade sarcoma composed of primitive undifferentiated cells admixed with islands of well-differentiated hyaline cartilage.

ICD-O coding

9240/3 Mesenchymal chondrosarcoma

ICD-11 coding

2B50.Z & XH8X47 Chondrosarcoma of bone and articular cartilage of unspecified sites & Mesenchymal chondrosarcoma

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Mesenchymal chondrosarcoma occurs most often as a primary intramedullary bone lesion, most commonly involving the skull and craniofacial region, followed by the ribs, vertebral spine, ilium, and femur {3697943}. Common extraskeletal sites include the cranial and spinal meninges, followed by the lower extremities.

Clinical features

Common symptoms are pain and/or swelling. Lesions of the head and neck may be associated with headaches, visual disturbances, and vomiting. Radiologically, intraosseous lesions appear lytic, destructive, and permeative, sometimes with soft tissue extension. Most contain flocculent or stippled calcifications, including those arising in extraskeletal sites.

Epidemiology

Peak incidence is within the second to third decades of life; therefore, most affected children are adolescents. Although extraordinarily rare, mesenchymal chondrosarcoma is more commonly seen in children than is conventional chondrosarcoma. There is a roughly equal sex distribution {3697943; 9635527}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Originally considered a form of chondrosarcoma, recent data suggest that mesenchymal chondrosarcoma is a translocation-associated sarcoma, driven by a recurrent specific gene fusion involving HEY1 and NCOA2 {22034177}.

Macroscopic appearance

Grossly, the tumours are variably soft to firm and greyish to red-tan, with frequent calcific/chondroid debris. Bone lesions are destructive, reflecting their radiological appearance. Soft tissue lesions may appear circumscribed.

Histopathology

The lesion has a characteristic bimorphic pattern, consisting of a hypercellular round to slightly spindled undifferentiated cell population, alternating with islands of benign to low-grade hyaline cartilage. A background haemangiopericytoma-like vascular pattern is common in these more cellular regions. Rarely, the stroma may be hyalinized/myxoid, with neoplastic cells arranged as cords and in single file. Lace-like osteoid is generally absent, although bone formation may occur, mimicking osteosarcoma. Surprisingly, mitotic activity is usually inconspicuous. The undifferentiated cells typically exhibit strong membranous CD99 and nuclear NKX2-2 immunoexpression, but they also may display aberrant desmin and myogenin positivity {26847175}.

Cytology

The undifferentiated portion of the tumour usually resembles Ewing sarcoma but tends to show more variability. In the absence of molecular confirmation, only the presence of definite chondroid matrix can establish the diagnosis.

Diagnostic molecular pathology

The vast majority of mesenchymal chondrosarcomas contain a relatively specific gene fusion between HEY1 (8q21.1) and NCOA2 (8q13.3), which are separated by only 10 Mb on chromosome 8 {22034177}. Unlike in conventional chondrosarcomas, IDH1 and IDH2 mutations are absent.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: an undifferentiated small cell malignancy juxtaposed to mature hyaline cartilage islands.

Desirable: a background staghorn vascular pattern; HEY1::NCOA2 gene fusion.

Staging

Tumour staging is per bone sarcoma protocols.

Prognosis and prediction

Mesenchymal chondrosarcoma behaves aggressively. Overall 5- and 10-year survival rates are 40–60% and 20–40%, respectively {6825046; 3697943}. Tumours arising within the jaw bones, and those in children and adolescents, tend to have a better prognosis {9635527; 10813732}. An absence of metastases at presentation is significantly associated with improved survival. Histology does not predict prognosis. The standard of care is complete resection with chemotherapy, but the role of radiation therapy is unclear {25529371}.
