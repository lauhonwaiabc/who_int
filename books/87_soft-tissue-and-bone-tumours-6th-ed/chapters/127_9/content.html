1.2.7 Myolipoma of soft tissue

Definition

Myolipoma of soft tissue is a benign extrauterine neoplasm composed of mature adipose tissue and well-differentiated smooth muscle cells.

ICD-O coding

8890/0 Myolipoma

ICD-11 coding

2E80.01 & XH4VB4 Deep subfascial lipoma & Angiomyolipoma

Related terminology

Acceptable: extrauterine lipoleiomyoma.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Myolipoma usually presents as a deeply situated mass within the retroperitoneum, abdominal cavity, pelvic cavity, or inguinal region. Less commonly it occurs in the trunk wall, the pleura, or the extremities; even an intraosseous lesion has been reported {27635945; 1703396; 7959650; 34584729; 38508597}.

Clinical features

Lesions within the abdominal cavity are often found incidentally, whereas those involving the trunk or extremities present as palpable lesions {27635945; 1703396}. MRI shows a fatty mass with associated signal-rich areas (corresponding to the smooth muscle component) that are heterogeneous on T1-weighted postcontrast fat-suppressed images {17325918}. Hormone-secreting neoplasms may occur {38362362}.

Epidemiology

Myolipoma is a rare tumour occurring chiefly in women. It is not associated with tuberous sclerosis {27635945; 1703396; 7959650}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Cytogenetic alterations of the HMGA2 gene have been reported {26857357; 12419585}. Fusion of the HMGA2 and C9orf92 genes resulting from a t(9;12)(p22;q14) was noted in 1 case of myolipoma {26857357}.

Macroscopic appearance

Deep myolipomas are frequently large, often reaching 100–250 mm in size. Giant neoplasms may mimic a more aggressive neoplasm {38524654}. Superficial lesions are smaller. The tumour is well circumscribed and usually surrounded by a thin capsule. The cut surface shows an admixture of yellowish adipose tissue and tan-whitish whorled nodules, depending on the amount and distribution of the smooth muscle component {27635945; 1703396; 7959650}.

Histopathology

The lesions are composed of an intimate admixture of mature fat cells and spindled cells, in variable proportions. The spindled areas consist of well-differentiated smooth muscle, with intersecting fascicles of amitotic, cytologically bland cells having eosinophilic cytoplasm, cigar-shaped nuclei, and occasionally perinuclear vacuoles. The adipocytic component lacks any features to suggest atypical lipomatous tumour / well-differentiated liposarcoma (e.g. enlarged cells with enlarged and atypical nuclei, fibrous septation, lipoblasts) {27635945; 1703396; 7959650}. The lesions usually contain variable numbers of small blood vessels and uterine-type thick-walled vessels {27635945}. Immunohistochemically, desmin, caldesmon, and SMA positivity confirms smooth muscle differentiation. Nuclear positivity for HMGA2 is identified in 60% of cases. ER and PR are often positive. MDM2, CDK4, and HMB45 are negative {27635945; 1703396; 8872156; 25517956; 15144408}.

Cytology

Insufficient data

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: extrauterine location; admixture of well-differentiated, cytologically bland smooth muscle and mature fat.

Staging

Not applicable

Prognosis and prediction

Complete resection is curative.
