1.3.15 SMAD3-rearranged fibroblastic tumour

Definition

SMAD3-rearranged fibroblastic tumour is a benign spindle cell neoplasm with EWSR1::SMAD3 fusion and a predilection for superficial acral sites, characterized by alternating cellular fascicles and hyalinized areas.

ICD-O coding

None

ICD-11 coding

None

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Tumours are superficially located within dermis and/or subcutaneous fat. These lesions occur on the extremities with a large majority located in the hands and feet {29957732; 29309308; 32901982}. Rare extra-acral examples have been reported, mostly affecting lower extremities {29957732; 32901982}.

Clinical features

Patients usually present with a small, painless, superficial tumour in an acral site.

Epidemiology

The tumours are extremely rare, with < 20 reported cases to date. There is a wide age range (1–68 years), with a strong female predilection {29957732; 29309308; 32901982}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

The tumour is defined by an EWSR1::SMAD3 fusion {29957732; 29309308; 32901982}. SMAD3 is an important signal transducer in the TGF-β/SMAD signalling pathway, which is involved in extracellular matrix synthesis by fibroblasts. By RNA sequencing, 1 case showed substantial upregulation of FN1, similar to other fibroblastic tumours, as well as ERG overexpression {29309308}.

Macroscopic appearance

The tumours are small, measuring 10–20 mm in greatest dimension, and have a nodular appearance.

Histopathology

The tumours are typically well demarcated but show infiltration into subcutaneous fat. Superficial cases may directly abut the epidermis. The tumours consist of short intersecting cellular fascicles of monomorphic fibroblastic spindle cells lacking nuclear pleomorphism, hyperchromasia, or prominent nucleoli. Mitotic activity is typically low. The stroma is either collagenous or myxoid. In a subset of cases, a distinctive zonation of central acellular hyalinized areas is discerned, contrasting with the peripheral cellular component. Focally stippled dystrophic calcification may be found. By immunohistochemistry, most cases show diffuse strong ERG nuclear expression in the tumour cells, whereas staining for SMA, CD34, and other relevant markers is negative {29957732; 29309308; 32901982}.

Cytology

Insufficient data

Diagnostic molecular pathology

The EWSR1::SMAD3 fusion or EWSR1 gene rearrangement may be confirmed by molecular methods.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: superficial acral nodule; short intersecting cellular fascicles of bland spindle cells with variably hyalinized areas.

Desirable: immunoreactivity for ERG; EWSR1::SMAD3 fusion or EWSR1 gene rearrangement in select cases.

Staging

Not applicable

Prognosis and prediction

EWSR1::SMAD3–rearranged fibroblastic tumour is benign. Local recurrences often occur after incomplete excision.
