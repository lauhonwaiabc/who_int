1.2.5 Lipoblastoma-like tumour

Definition

Lipoblastoma-like tumour (LLT) is a benign spindle cell neoplasm of mature and immature adipocytic differentiation sharing morphological features of spindle cell lipoma, myxoid liposarcoma, and lipoblastoma.

ICD-O coding

8881/0 Lipoblastoma-like tumour

ICD-11 coding

XXX

Related terminology

Not recommended: fibrosarcoma-like lipomatous neoplasm.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

LLT has been reported more commonly in the genital/perigenital region, but tumours involving the extremities, trunk, pelvis, and retroperitoneum have been described {37307874; 37355153; 37771086; 31622502; 32617677}.

Clinical features

Most frequently LLT presents as a swelling, cyst, or nodule that can be associated with pain or tenderness {29976943; 25929353}.

Epidemiology

LLT is rare and occurs in both sexes, although it shows a predilection for the vulva {37355153}. Patients are often diagnosed in early to mid adulthood {37307874; 37355153}, with few reports of tumours in adolescence {12047760; 25929353} and rare cases in infancy {37355153}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Recurrent gene rearrangements, fusion events, and mutations have not been observed in LLT. Specifically, copy-number variation analysis, break-apart FISH, and next-generation sequencing have demonstrated no evidence of RB1 loss and DDIT3 or PLAG1 rearrangements {37307874; 37355153; 29976943}. Genomic copy number by chromosomal microarray revealed a normal diploid profile with occasional focal gains or losses {37307874; 29976943}. An IDH1 variant was reported in one LLT of the tongue {39925152}.

Macroscopic appearance

LLT forms a well-defined myxoid or gelatinous mass that is often lobulated and has a tan-yellow (fatty) cut surface. Small cysts may be present, but necrosis is not seen. Tumours have ranged from 20 mm to 300 mm {29976943; 37355153}.

Histopathology

LLT has a multinodular to multilobulated architecture that is delineated by acellular fibrous bands of varying thickness. Tumour matrix is often richly myxoid, but can be collagenous, and prominent thin-walled plexiform vasculature resembling that of myxoid liposarcoma is seen in either stromal configuration. The nodules and lobules are populated by a mixture of mature adipocytes, small univacuolated and bivacuolated lipoblasts, and cytologically bland spindle cells with fibrillary cytoplasm and blunt-ended to tapered nuclei. Lipoblasts can be sparse or plentiful and may be concentrated at the periphery of some tumour nodules or lobules. Mitoses are infrequent. Necrosis is absent {12047760; 25929353; 29976943; 37307874; 37355153; 40409687; 40205930; 30853315; 29300200}.

LLT immunohistochemically lacks expression of RB1 or has a heterogeneous (mosaic) pattern of alternating weakly positive to negative nuclear expression {29976943; 25929353}. Desmin and/or S100 expression has been reported in as many as 60% of cases {23887155}. CD34 positivity is observed in a minor subset of LLT {25929353}. Some tumours may be weakly positive for PLAG1, DDIT3, MDM2, and CDK4 by immunohistochemistry, but they do not harbour genetic abnormalities involving their coding genes {37307874; 37355153}.

Cytology

Insufficient data

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: lobulated growth delineated by variably sized septa; admixture of mature adipocytes, univacuolated and/or bivacuolated lipoblasts, and bland spindle cells; variably myxoid to collagenous matrix and thin-walled plexiform vasculature.

Desirable: molecular genetic testing to exclude mimics in selected cases.

Staging

Not applicable

Prognosis and prediction

Tumours infrequently recur locally, often because of resection margin involvement {25929353; 37307874; 37355153}. One myxoid tumour reported as LLT metastasized {37355153}, but distant dissemination has otherwise not been described in series of LLT.
