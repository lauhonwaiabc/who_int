1.5.11 YAP1::TFE3 haemangioendothelioma

Definition

YAP1::TFE3 haemangioendothelioma is a malignant vascular neoplasm composed of epithelioid endothelial cells that often display abundant eosinophilic cytoplasm and vasoformative features, and is characterized by a YAP1::TFE3 fusion.

ICD-O coding

9133/3 Epithelioid haemangioendothelioma NOS

ICD-11 coding

2B5Y & XH9GF8 Other specified malignant mesenchymal neoplasms & Epithelioid haemangioendothelioma

Related terminology

Acceptable: epithelioid haemangioendothelioma with YAP1::TFE3 fusion.

Not recommended: malignant epithelioid haemangioendothelioma.

Subtype(s)

Not applicable

Localization

YAP1::TFE3 haemangioendothelioma most commonly involves superficial and deep soft tissue, bone, lung, and liver, but can affect almost any anatomical site {23737213; 25680571; 28585251; 28855107; 31341686; 32151991; 34381186}.

Clinical features

Tumours can present as solitary or multifocal masses {23737213; 26840265; 28855107; 34381186}. Clinical symptoms depend on the anatomical site of involvement.

Epidemiology

YAP1::TFE3 haemangioendothelioma is a rare entity; reported cases affect patients over a wide age range (20–78 years, median: 44 years) and show a slight female predominance {23737213; 34381186; 37686662}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

YAP1::TFE3 haemangioendothelioma is characterized by a translocation event, YAP1::TFE3 {23737213}. The fusion breakpoint in YAP1::TFE3 haemangioendothelioma usually involves YAP1 exon 1 and TFE3 exons 4 or 6. YAP1 is highly homologous to WWTR1 (TAZ); both are transcriptional regulators of the Hippo pathway and promote oncogenic transcriptional programmes {32675324}. Activation of the Hippo pathway leads to phosphorylation of YAP1 and WWTR1 (TAZ), resulting in their cytoplasmic sequestration and degradation. Similar to that of WWTR1::CAMTA1, fusion of YAP1 to TFE3 results in dysregulation of the Hippo pathway {35740643; 33913810}. A rare case involving the non-canonical fusion variant, WWTR1::TFE3, which showed hybrid features of classic epithelioid haemangioendothelioma and YAP1::TFE3 haemangioendothelioma, has been reported {38380774}.

Macroscopic appearance

Tumours in visceral or deep soft tissue locations are usually larger than those arising at superficial sites {34381186}.

Histopathology

YAP1::TFE3 haemangioendothelioma often shows vasoformative areas and areas of solid growth, contrasting with the classic form of epithelioid haemangioendothelioma with WWTR1::CAMTA1 {23737213}. It may also demonstrate cells in coalescing nests, clusters, and discohesive strands, as well as a pseudoalveolar growth pattern. These growth patterns may be present in variable proportions. There is often a myxoid to myxohyaline stroma. An epithelioid cytomorphology usually predominates, with the cells showing well-demarcated cell borders and abundant, glassy eosinophilic to vacuolated cytoplasm. The nuclei are typically vesicular with prominent nucleoli {34381186}.

The tumour cells of YAP1::TFE3 haemangioendothelioma consistently express the endothelial markers ERG and CD31. Expression of CD34 (80%) and low molecular weight keratins (20%) is more variable {24986479; 23737213; 34381186}. Diffuse nuclear expression of TFE3 is also a consistent feature {23737213; 26840265; 34381186}, but can rarely be seen in tumours with WWTR1::CAMTA1 fusion {26414223; 24986479; 34381186}. YAP1::TFE3 haemangioendothelioma also frequently shows loss of expression of YAP1 C-terminus, which can also be used to support the diagnosis {34148063}. CAMTA1 is usually negative but has been reported in at least 1 case confirmed by fusion testing {34381186}.

Cytology

There is one published case report with FNAB showing round to spindle cells with abundant, eccentric, dense cytoplasm often pulled out in a tail, sometimes with a vacuole or haemosiderin, seen singly and in minute, discohesive tissue fragments. The nuclei are round to oval and moderately pleomorphic, with some nuclei appearing raisinoid with coarse chromatin. Occasional binucleated or multinucleated cells are present {34411462}.

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Identification of the YAP1::TFE3 fusion helps distinguish YAP1::TFE3 haemangioendothelioma from other vascular tumours.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: solid growth and/or well-formed vascular channels lined by epithelioid endothelial cells with abundant eosinophilic cytoplasm.

Desirable (in selected cases): TFE3 rearrangement; YAP1::TFE3 fusion; strong and diffuse immunohistochemical positivity for TFE3.

Staging

YAP1::TFE3 haemangioendothelioma of somatic soft tissue should be staged according to the American Joint Committee on Cancer (AJCC) ninth-edition TNM staging system for soft tissue sarcoma of the trunk and extremities.

Prognosis and prediction

Patients with YAP1::TFE3 haemangioendothelioma have a slightly more favourable clinical outcome than do patients with conventional epithelioid haemangioendothelioma with WWTR1::CAMTA1 fusion. The 5-year overall survival and progression-free survival are 86% and 88%, respectively {31537895; 34381186}. Median overall survival is 120 months. Multifocality, pleural disease, or lymph node metastases are associated with a more aggressive clinical course {31537895}.
