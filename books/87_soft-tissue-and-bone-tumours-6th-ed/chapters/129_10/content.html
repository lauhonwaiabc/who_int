1.2.9 Spindle cell lipoma and pleomorphic lipoma

Definition

Spindle cell lipoma is a benign adipocytic neoplasm composed of variable amounts of mature adipocytes, bland spindle cells, and ropy collagen. Pleomorphic lipoma in addition contains pleomorphic and multinucleated floret-like giant cells.

ICD-O coding

8857/0 Spindle cell lipoma

ICD-11 coding

2E80.0Z & XH30M7 Lipoma, unspecified & pleomorphic lipoma

2E80.0Z & XH4E98 Lipoma, unspecified & spindle cell lipoma

Related terminology

Not recommended: dendritic fibromyxolipoma.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Approximately 80% of spindle cell and pleomorphic lipomas arise within the subcutis of the posterior neck, back, and shoulders {1192370; 7459800; 31950475; 33392798; 30850230; 30709444}. The remaining 20% involve different sites, including the face, scalp, oral cavity, upper and lower limbs, and trunk {7459800; 998247; 3623439; 25219904; 27686175}. Cases in women are more likely to arise outside the typical locations {28719462}. Rarely, intramuscular lesions and dermal-based lesions are seen {27686175; 11190440}.

Clinical features

These tumours typically present as painless subcutaneous lumps in the upper trunk and neck but may also involve oral submucosa and skin (dermis). Most tumours measure < 5 cm. Occasionally, multiple lesions are seen, and these cases may be familial {9422314; 12522375; 3623439}.

Epidemiology

Spindle cell and pleomorphic lipoma are relatively rare compared with ordinary lipoma (1:60 ratio). There is a significant male predominance (> 80% of cases) with peak incidence in the fifth to seventh decades of life {37168965}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Spindle cell and pleomorphic lipoma are characterized by usually heterozygous loss RB1, often as a part of chromosome arm 13q losses; 16q deletion may also occur {9354891; 12494468; 21563233; 22790852; 8579124; 33802620}.

Macroscopic appearance

Grossly, spindle cell and pleomorphic lipoma typically form an ovoid yellowish mass that can be grossly mucinous.

Histopathology

Spindle cell lipoma is usually well circumscribed and often encapsulated. Dermal and intramuscular tumours may have an infiltrative appearance. All are characterized by a triad of variable amounts of bland spindle cells, mature adipocytes, and ropy collagen bundles. These components are usually seen in a fibrous or myxoid stroma {1192370; 998247; 3623439}. Mast cells are a consistent finding. The adipocytes are variable in size, and occasional lipoblasts can be identified {28546131}. The spindle cells are bland and randomly placed or arranged in short fascicles in a school-of-fish pattern, sometimes with nuclear palisading. Nuclei are uniform and elongated, with bipolar eosinophilic cytoplasmic processes. Mitoses are absent or very rare, and necrosis is absent. Pleomorphic lipoma in addition contains pleomorphic spindle cells and multinucleated floret-like giant cells {7459800; 6884998}.

Morphological patterns include fat-poor spindle cell and pleomorphic lipoma, with little to no fat {17890910; 19542913; 33602577; 33527490; 31115930; 31187478}; the myxoid pattern, with abundant myxoid stroma {25047505; 30853315}; the pseudoangiomatous pattern, with branching and dilated vascular-like spaces that separate the spindle cells and stroma to form pseudopapillary projections {8063285; 17199739; 37908107; 31187478}; the plexiform pattern {8838333}; spindle cell lipoma with focal cartilaginous and osseous metaplasia {3623439; 34812072}; and spindle cell lipoma with extramedullary haematopoiesis {11493343}. The spindle, pleomorphic, and floret-like giant cells characteristically stain for CD34, and a minority of cases have focal or more extensive desmin and S100 positivity {9001985; 9042286; 27444171}. Nuclear RB1 loss can be often detected by immunohistochemistry {22790852}.

Cytology

Aspirated material shows variable cellularity, tissue fragments of mature fat, bland spindle cells and eosinophilic (on H&E) hyaline collagen fibres, and (in some cases) a myxoid background and mast cells. In pleomorphic lipoma, a variable number of floret cells and pleomorphic epithelioid/polygonal or spindle cells are seen. Lipoblasts are rare {11748578; 19774616}.

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Heterozygous loss of RB1 can be detected by FISH or DNA copy-number plot but is usually not required for diagnosis.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: bland spindle cells; fibrous or myxoid matrix; mature fat; ropy collagen; scattered mast cells; floret-like multinucleated giant cells in pleomorphic lipoma.

Desirable: CD34 immunopositivity; RB1 loss in selected cases.

Staging

Not applicable

Prognosis and prediction

Spindle cell and pleomorphic lipoma are benign tumours that can rarely recur locally, but they do not metastasize.
