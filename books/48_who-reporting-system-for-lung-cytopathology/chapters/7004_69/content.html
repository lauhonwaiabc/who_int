7.0.0.4 Risk of malignancy and management recommendations

The risk of malignancy for the “Atypical” category is 62–100% for exfoliative samples and 46–55% for FNAB, and for the “Suspicious for malignancy” category, 75–100% for exfoliative samples and 75–88% for FNAB {26331778; 30051969}. The use of the category “Suspicious for malignancy” has a high rate of interobserver variability, so consultation with more experienced cytopathologists is recommended when assessing cases with a limited cellularity, obscuring or technical problems, or cytopathological features that may be shared by inflammatory reactive epithelium and malignancies. It is mandatory to review the clinical presentation and any imaging that is available, ideally in a multidisciplinary setting, before proceeding to further diagnostic steps or definitive management.

If a sputum sample is “Suspicious for malignancy”, it can be repeated, or the patient can proceed to bronchoscopy with bronchial washings and brushings or endobronchial ultrasound–directed transbronchial FNAB with or without core needle biopsy. Transthoracic FNAB is an option for peripheral lesions or where bronchoscopy is not available in low- and middle-income countries. If the bronchial washings/brushings and/or FNAB are "Benign" and correlate with benign clinical and imaging findings, routine follow-up with imaging at 3–6 months can be recommended. If the cytopathology remains “Suspicious for malignancy” and the clinical and imaging are suspicious, repeat FNAB (preferably with rapid onsite evaluation [ROSE]) is recommended and core needle biopsy can be added, although proceeding to surgical biopsy with or without frozen sectioning should be considered on the basis of the overall findings.

The categorization of a case as “Suspicious for malignancy” is not equivalent to a final diagnosis of malignancy. It is imperative to have good communication with the members of the clinical team (with review of all relevant clinical and imaging information) for the determination of a management plan, in order to avoid unnecessary additional risks and costs.
