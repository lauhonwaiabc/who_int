3.0.0.1 Introduction: The role of ancillary testing

With advances in minimally invasive sampling techniques, lung carcinoma is frequently diagnosed by cytopathology alone. The ability to perform reliable and accurate immunostaining in cytopathological specimens to confirm the diagnosis, subtype primary lung carcinoma, and differentiate it from metastases from other sites is crucial {30572031} [[Yatabe Y, Borczuk AC, Cooper WA, et al., editors. IASLC atlas of diagnostic immunohistochemistry. Denver (CO): International Association for the Study of Lung Cancer; 2020.]]. In addition, owing to the rapid growth in therapeutic options for patients with advanced-stage non-small cell lung carcinoma, predictive biomarker testing to identify targetable oncogenic driver alterations is the cornerstone for determining the best therapeutic option in these patients {30285222} [[National Comprehensive Cancer Network (NCCN) [Internet]. Fort Washington (PA): NCCN; 2021 [accessed 2021 Nov 16]. Available from: https://www.nccn.org.]]. Molecular profiling with matched targeted therapy has led to unprecedented outcome improvements in a significant subset of patients with non-small cell lung carcinoma {32786189; 33433946}.

Cytopathology specimens are highly relevant as a tissue source for ancillary testing, because they may often be the only source of tumour material available from a patient. Frequently, cytopathology provides complementary tumour material to small biopsies, which may have a limited number of tumour cells that may fall short of adequate material for molecular testing {28084342; 31913583}. Current clinical practice guidelines indicate that any cytopathology preparation (direct smears, cytospins, liquid-based preparations, or cell block), can be used for molecular testing {29398453}. A wide variety of ancillary tests, including ICC, FISH, and molecular techniques, can be successfully employed in cytopathology specimens, provided the assays have been adequately validated for cytopathological substrates and a robust and rigorous quality assurance is in place {32401054}.
