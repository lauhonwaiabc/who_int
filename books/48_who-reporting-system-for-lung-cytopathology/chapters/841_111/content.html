8.4.1 Sample reports: Malignant

The sample reports provided here are based on the standardized cytopathology report in the section Diagnostic categories and report structure section of the first chapter. See that section for a discussion on the structure and components.

These are sample reports that include the components required in one possible format; the final report format will depend on local practice and local laboratory information system requirements. For brevity, demographics and information on the macroscopic features and rapid onsite evaluation (ROSE) are not presented here.

Each institution should select a term for the main heading of the diagnostic summary, such as "Diagnostic summary" (as used in the examples below), “Diagnosis”, or “Final diagnosis”, and use it consistently.

Sample report 1

Type of specimen:

Sputum sample.

 

Clinical and imaging information:

30 mm mass in left upper lobe. No definite hilar lymphadenopathy. Smoker.

 

Diagnostic summary:

Category: Malignant

Diagnosis: Adenocarcinoma

 

Microscopic description:

Occasional crowded tissue fragments of large cells with large, highly pleomorphic nuclei and prominent single nucleoli, and with eccentric, pale, occasionally vacuolated cytoplasm, are present. Small bronchial sheets, siderophages, histiocytes, neutrophils, and squamous cells are also present. There is no mucin in the background.

Comment: Poorly differentiated adenocarcinoma is present. Correlation with imaging and clinical history is required. Further workup with FNAB is recommended to enable classification of the carcinoma.

Sample report 2

Type of specimen:

Bronchial brush.

 

Clinical and imaging information:

Smoker. Poorly defined mass in right middle lobe on routine CXR with hilar lymphadenopathy. Bronchial brush right middle lobe bronchus.

 

Diagnostic summary:

Bronchial brush right middle lobe bronchus

Category: Malignant

Diagnosis: Non-small cell carcinoma NOS

 

Microscopic description:

These highly cellular smears show occasional tissue fragments of intermediate-sized to large cells with variable (often high) N:C ratios, large pleomorphic nuclei, prominent nucleoli, and pale cytoplasm, with no definite squamous or glandular differentiation. Plentiful bronchial epithelial tissue fragments, neutrophils, and histiocytes, with some carbon-laden macrophages, are also present.

Comment: A poorly differentiated carcinoma is present, suggesting a non-small cell carcinoma of lung of uncertain type. There is no cell block available for ICC. FNAB is recommended to provide material for typing, staging, and ancillary tests.

Editorial note:

The diagnostic summary includes the specific information regarding the site of the lesion from the request form and specimen container of any needle-rinsing material.

Sample report 3

Type of specimen:

Endobronchial ultrasound–guided FNAB right upper lobe and subcarinal lymph node.

 

Clinical and imaging information:

Right upper lobe 30 mm mass FNAB. Lesion seen on CXR. Hilar lymphadenopathy. History of colonic cancer.

 

Diagnostic summary:

 

Specimen 1:

Subcarinal lymph node FNAB

Category: Malignant

Diagnosis: Metastatic small cell carcinoma

 

Specimen 2:

Right upper lobe 30 mm mass FNAB

Category: Malignant

Diagnosis: Small cell carcinoma of the lung

 

Microscopic description:

 

Specimen 1: These moderately cellular smears show metastatic small cell carcinoma in a lymphoid background.

 

Specimen 2: These moderately cellular smears show small cell carcinoma with tissue fragments of intermediate-sized cells with a high N:C ratio, pleomorphic nuclei showing finely dispersed chromatin and occasional small nucleoli, and poorly defined cytoplasm. There is chromatinic smearing and apoptosis is prominent. Occasional mitoses are seen.

Comment: Features are those of small cell carcinoma of lung.

 

Supplementary report 1:

Specimen 2: ICC on the cell block shows the carcinoma to be positive for thyroid transcription factor 1 (TTF1), chromogranin, and synaptophysin, and negative for p40, confirming small cell carcinoma of lung.

Editorial note:

The diagnostic summary includes the specific information regarding the site of the lesion from the request form and specimen container of any needle-rinsing material.

There has been a supplementary report on the ICC for this case performed subsequently on a cell block, and this is included in the single integrated report.

Sample report 4

Type of specimen:

Transthoracic FNAB left upper lobe of lung.

 

Clinical and imaging information:

Left upper lobe 30 mm solid mass transthoracic FNAB. Presented with haemoptysis. Prior history of colorectal carcinoma. ? metastasis.

 

Diagnostic summary:

Left upper lobe 30 mm solid mass transthoracic FNAB

Category: Malignant

Diagnosis: Adenocarcinoma of the lung

 

Microscopic description:

These highly cellular smears show moderately differentiated adenocarcinoma consisting of crowded sheets of large cells with a moderate N:C ratio and occasional vacuoles in the eccentric cytoplasm. There is no prominent necrosis or mucin.

Comment: Adenocarcinoma is present. ICC is underway on the cell block.

 

Supplementary report 1:

The adenocarcinoma in the cell block is positive for thyroid transcription factor 1 (TTF1), negative for p40, and negative for CDX2. Predictive biomarker analysis including PDL1 ICC and next-generation sequencing is underway.

Supplementary report 2:

The adenocarcinoma is negative for EGFR. PDL1 to follow.

Editorial note:

The diagnostic summary includes the specific information regarding the site of the lesion from the request form and specimen container of any needle-rinsing material.

There has been a supplementary report on the ICC for this case performed subsequently on a cell block, and a further supplementary report on the next-generation sequencing, and these are included in the single integrated report (for brevity, the full next-generation sequencing report has not been included here). If a further study such as a PDL1 is underway, this is noted in this report to alert the clinical team that a further report will be issued as an integrated report.
