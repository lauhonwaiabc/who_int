6.3 Imprinting disorders

There are few disorders of imprinting. The Beckwith–Wiedemann spectrum is associated with aberrant expression of imprinted genes located at the 11p15 chromosomal region, including KCNQ1OT1, H19, CDKN1C, and IGF2. The characteristic overgrowth seen in patients with Beckwith–Wiedemann spectrum may be partially explained by inappropriate IGF2 expression of both parental alleles, or loss of expression of the CDKN1C maternal allele {29514882}. CDKN1C and IGF2 are protein-encoding genes. CDKN1C encodes a cell-cycle, cell-division, and growth regulator that acts as a tumour suppressor gene, while IGF2 encodes an insulin-like growth-promoting factor via the RB1 pathway. KCNQ1OT1 is an imprinted long non-coding RNA. It interacts with chromatin and regulates transcription of multiple target genes through epigenetic modifications.
