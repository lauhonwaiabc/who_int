4.6 DNA polymerization

DNA polymerases catalyse the synthesis of DNA from nucleosides. They are essential for DNA replication and repair and act in concert with other proteins involved in DNA synthesis and repair, including helicases.

Germline POLE and POLD1 variants that affect the exonuclease proofreading domain are responsible for polymerase proofreading–associated polyposis and predispose patients to intestinal polyposis and colorectal cancer {26822575}.
