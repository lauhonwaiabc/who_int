5.1 Telomere biology disorders

Telomere biology disorders are characterized by a spectrum of syndromes in which affected individuals have very short telomeres for their age. They are at risk of a variety of tumours, including myelodysplastic syndrome, acute myeloid leukaemia, head-and-neck squamous cell carcinoma, and others. Patients are also at risk of bone marrow failure, pulmonary fibrosis, liver disease, and many other complications, but may present with just one of the associated features. Pathogenic germline variants have been noted in at least 18 genes involved in telomere biology with X-linked recessive, autosomal dominant, autosomal recessive, or de novo occurrence {20301779;34852175;36485133;36151328;35929966}.
