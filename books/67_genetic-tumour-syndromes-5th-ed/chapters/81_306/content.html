8.1 Ubiquitin pathway

Ubiquitylation, the addition of ubiquitin to a protein, marks proteins for degradation via the proteasome. In addition, it often alters the activity and location of the protein.

There is only one genetic tumour predisposition syndrome in this volume that reflects a defect in a ubiquitin pathway: the BAP1-related tumour predisposition syndrome. The protein affected is involved in homologous recombination DNA repair, and the tumours found in affected individuals reflect this.
