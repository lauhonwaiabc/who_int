4.3.3 MBD4-associated neoplasia syndrome (MBD4)

Definition

MBD4-associated neoplasia syndrome (MANS) is an autosomal recessive neoplasia predisposition disorder resulting from germline (constitutional) pathogenic variants affecting MBD4, whose phenotype includes adenomatous intestinal polyposis, colorectal adenocarcinoma, acute myeloid leukaemia, and uveal melanoma.

MIM numbering

619975 MBD4-associated neoplasia syndrome; MANS

ICD-11 coding

None

Related terminology

Acceptable: MBD4-related tumour predisposition syndrome.

Not recommended: tumour predisposition syndrome 2.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

MANS affects the intestinal tract, bone marrow / blood, and eye, giving rise to intestinal adenomatous polyposis, colorectal adenocarcinoma, acute myeloid leukaemia, and uveal melanoma.

Clinical features

This is a recently described disorder whose phenotype is emerging. Adenomatous polyposis of the large and small intestines is seen, indistinguishable from that seen in attenuated familial adenomatous polyposis and MUTYH-associated polyposis, together with colorectal adenocarcinomas {35460607;34866929;31322271;35381620}. Acute myeloid leukaemia with early onset and myelodysplastic syndrome occur.

Uveal melanoma is reported both in individuals with biallelic MBD4 pathogenic variants and in carriers with only one MBD4 allele affected by a pathogenic variant (in whom second hits such as loss of heterozygosity are observed in their tumours) {29760383;32239153;32421892}. Hence, MANS is an autosomal recessive disorder in which manifesting carriers occur {32239153;35460607}.

A vestibular schwannoma and a glioblastoma have been observed in single patients {35381620}.

Epidemiology

This is a rare and recently described syndrome for which as yet there are insufficient data {35460607}. In a uveal melanoma cohort from Finland, the frequency of likely pathogenic variants was 0.23% (1/432; 95% CI 0.01–1.28) {32421892}.

Etiology

MANS is an autosomal recessive neoplasia predisposition syndrome resulting from germline pathogenic variants affecting MBD4, encoding a protein involved in base excision repair.

Pathogenesis

MBD4 is responsible for base excision repair of deaminated 5-methylcytosine residues, which give rise to G:T mismatches at CpG dinucleotides in DNA {30049810;31322271;35381620;35460607}. MBD4 binds preferentially to 5-methylcytosine CpG:TpG mismatches, which are the primary product of deamination at methyl-CpG sites. Complete loss of functional MBD4 protein (resulting from biallelic inactivation of MBD4) thus causes accumulation of C>T transitions at CpG dinucleotides (mCpG>TpG), giving rise to a characteristic mutation signature (SBS1) and hypermutator phenotype, which in turn stimulates a strong immune response amenable to immune blockade therapy {32239153;35863105;29760383}. In carriers of a single MBD4 pathogenic variant, acquired mutations affecting the normal MBD4 allele are seen in the cells of tumours, such as uveal melanomas, including loss of heterozygosity involving 3q21.3, the chromosomal locus of MBD4 {29760383}.

Macroscopic appearance

Solid tumours related to MANS have the same appearance as their sporadic counterparts.

Histopathology

Colorectal adenomas are mostly tubular adenomas that appear similar to their sporadic counterparts, as do colorectal adenocarcinomas with increased tumour-infiltrating lymphocytes. Typical uveal melanomas and acute myeloid leukaemia are seen {35460607;31322271}.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Loss of expression of MBD4 is observed on immunohistochemistry, together with the characteristic mutation signature SBS1 (> 60% of SNVs are mCpG>TpG) on the background of hypermutation {31322271;35381620;35460607}.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: any combination of intestinal adenomatous polyposis (with or without adenocarcinoma), acute myeloid leukaemia, and/or uveal melanoma, together with identification of MBD4 pathogenic variants and/or the characteristic mutation signature SBS1 (> 60% of SNVs are mCpG>TpG) on the background of hypermutation.

Desirable: loss of expression of MBD4 on immunohistochemistry.

Staging

Union for International Cancer Control (UICC) / American Joint Committee on Cancer (AJCC) TNM staging is applicable to individual malignant tumours.

Prognosis and prediction

Prognosis and prediction is as for sporadic counterparts, except that uveal melanomas and, by extension, possibly other neoplasms exhibit a good response to immune blockade therapy {29760383;35460607}.
