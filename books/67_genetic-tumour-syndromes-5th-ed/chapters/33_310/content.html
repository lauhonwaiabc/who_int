3.3 FAS pathway

The FAS cell-signalling pathway has an important role in the regulation of apoptosis. FAS (also called APO-1 or CD95) is a death domain–containing member of the TNFR superfamily. Germline pathogenic variants in FAS, its ligand, or other members of the apoptosis pathway {20538792;34171534} that abrogate intracellular signalling produce primary immune regulatory disorders which share an autoimmune lymphoproliferative phenotype due to failure of lymphocyte apoptosis {9028957;7540117} and increased proliferation {17724145;27099149}. Failure of FAS-dependent T-cell immune surveillance {24487434} and FAS-deficient germinal-centre B cells {33237303} may contribute to lymphoma predisposition.
