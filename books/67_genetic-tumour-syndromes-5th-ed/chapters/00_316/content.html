0.0 Website beta version

The beta version of this volume has been uploaded to allow early access for the WHO Classification of Tumours Online community. The content may change during further editing and the review of proofs of the book, but such changes are usually minor and the taxonomy itself as agreed by the editorial board is unlikely to change. The print volume is planned for publication in 2025.

Please note that the volume has not yet been through copy editing and some typographical errors are inevitable. We use WHO English, which is mid-way between US and UK usage, so you may see some linguistic differences. Please ignore these.

Comments can be added using the feedback tool within the individual sections. We hope you find it helpful to have this information early and enjoy using the website.
