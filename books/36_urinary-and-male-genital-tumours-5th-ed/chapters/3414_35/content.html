3.4.1.4 Renomedullary interstitial cell tumour

Definition

Renomedullary interstitial cell tumour is a benign neoplasm composed of cells resembling those of the renomedullary interstitium.

ICD-O coding

8966/0 Renomedullary interstitial cell tumour

ICD-11 coding

2F35 & XH3470 Benign neoplasm of urinary organs & Renomedullary interstitial cell tumour

Related terminology

Acceptable: medullary fibroma.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Tumours occur approximately equally in both kidneys, overwhelmingly within the body of the renal medulla, and rarely in the renal papilla or corticomedullary junction {27635944}.

Clinical features

The vast majority of renomedullary interstitial cell tumours are ≤ 6 mm and found incidentally at pathological examination of the kidney for a larger neoplasm, or at autopsy. However, rare tumours have manifested as clinically evident masses, sometimes causing renal obstruction {8514473; 8126845; 11061906; 19891244; 24642074}.

Epidemiology

Renomedullary interstitial cell tumours occur predominantly in adults.

Etiology

The exact etiology of this lesion is unknown. It was earlier postulated to be related to systemic hypertension, but more recent studies have not found any conclusive evidence of an association with hypertension {27635944}.

Pathogenesis

Unknown

Macroscopic appearance

The prototypical appearance of renomedullary interstitial cell tumour is a round to oval whitish nodule in the renal medulla, rarely larger than 6 mm.

Histopathology

Renomedullary interstitial cell tumour is composed of elongated spindle-shaped to stellate cells with bland nuclei and scant cytoplasm. The density ranges from hypocellular with prominent collagen to more cellular {27635944}. Eosinophilic, rope-like strands of collagen are sometimes present within the lesion; initially, these were thought to be amyloid {6119030}, but more recent studies have found them to be consistently negative for Congo red stain {30031100; 27635944}. Entrapped renal tubules are common, and sometimes these are dilated.

Immunohistochemistry

The immunohistochemical staining characteristics are nonspecific with weak to moderate labelling for SMA and calponin, negative or weak CD34, and usually weak ER and PR {30031100}. The ultrastructural characteristics are thought to resemble the normal renomedullary interstitial cells {4264334}.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: a round or oval spindle cell nodule in the renal medulla, lacking cytological atypia, usually < 6 mm in size.

Staging

Not applicable

Prognosis and prediction

Renomedullary interstitial cell tumour is a benign tumour. Malignant behaviour or transformation has not been described.
