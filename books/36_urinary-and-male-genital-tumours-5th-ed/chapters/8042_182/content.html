8.0.4.2 Cystadenoma of the epididymis

Definition

Cystadenoma of the epididymis is a benign epithelial tumour that arises from epididymal ducts.

ICD-O coding

8440/0 Cystadenoma of the epididymis

ICD-11 coding

2F34 & XH5RJ2 Benign neoplasm of male genital organs & Cystadenoma, NOS

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Epididymal cystadenoma is usually located in the caput epididymis {16841375}.

Clinical features

The symptoms include scrotal mass and infertility, and 10% of patients present with pain. Bilaterality has been reported {1225459}.

Epidemiology

Insufficient data

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Epididymal cystadenoma is derived from a proliferation of the epithelium of the efferent ductular system located in the caput epididymis {16841375}. Epididymal cystadenoma may also represent a hamartomatous lesion of ectatic efferent ducts {7756240}.

Macroscopic appearance

The cyst surface is smooth. On the cut section, the cyst may be unilocular or multilocular and is often filled with clear fluid {16040296}.

Histopathology

The tubular and cystic structures are lined by a single row of bland tall columnar cells with eosinophilic cytoplasm. Although these may also be papillary, they lack the cuboidal, clear cell morphology of the papillary cystadenoma. No mitosis, necrosis, or pleomorphism is observed {16841375}.

The differential diagnosis includes metastases from prostatic carcinoma in the paratesticular region. The cystadenoma may show microcystic, cribriform, and microglandular areas; however, the cells lack nuclear atypia and prominent nucleoli. The cells are negative for PSA and PAP {16040296; 6612245}.

Cytology

Not relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: presence of tubules, papillae, and a cyst arising from the epididymis, lined by bland tall columnar epithelium; eosinophilic cytoplasm; absence of mitotic figures and features of malignancy.

Desirable: absence of cuboidal, clear cell morphology.

Staging

Not relevant

Prognosis and prediction

Cystadenoma is a benign tumour, and surgical excision is curative {1225459}.
