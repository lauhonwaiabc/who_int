3.1.6.1 Tubulocystic renal cell carcinoma

Definition

Tubulocystic renal cell carcinoma (TcRCC) is a rare cystic renal epithelial neoplasm with exclusive tubulocystic growth.

ICD-O coding

8316/3 Tubulocystic renal cell carcinoma

ICD-11 coding

2C90.0 & XH7K79 Renal cell carcinoma of kidney, except renal pelvis & Tubulocystic renal cell carcinoma

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

The tumour involves the cortex and corticomedullary junction with a left predominance {19011562}.

Clinical features

Most cases are incidental findings. Haematuria, abdominal pain, and (rarely) end-stage renal disease are observed {19011562}.

Epidemiology

This is a rare tumour, representing < 1% of renal tumours. There is a male predominance and a median age of 58.4 years (range: 30–74 years) {19011562}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

A consistent gain of chromosome 17 and losses of chromosomes 9 and Y are reported {26310887; 30622286}. Non-synonymous mutation in ABL1 and PDGFRA were found in a few cases, whereas others have shown mutations in the chromatin-modifying genes KMT2C and KDM5C {30622286; 29056573}. Unlike in papillary renal cell carcinoma, downregulation of the microRNAs miR-155 and miR-34a has also been reported {29056573}.

Macroscopic appearance

TcRCC is usually well circumscribed and ranges in size from 7 to 210 mm (median: 90 mm). Mostly it occurs as a solitary mass, with a spongy or bubble wrap–like appearance. The cut surface consist of small white cysts containing clear fluid. No haemorrhage or necrosis is observed {23427871; 19011562; 23525677; 24375036; 27635946}.

Histopathology

TcRCC comprises small to intermediate-sized tubules admixed with larger cysts, which are lined by a single layer of flattened, cuboidal/columnar, and hobnail epithelium. The nuclei are enlarged and irregular, with intermediate to large and prominent nucleoli; cytoplasmic features include abundant eosinophilic cytoplasm. The stroma is often fibrotic/desmoplastic. Cases with a prominent papillary, poorly differentiated, or sarcomatoid component are probably unrelated to true TcRCC {21439613; 24375036; 27635946}. Histological grading is not necessary because nucleoli are always large {25398389}.

Several other tumour types may show a degree of tubulocystic morphology and should be excluded. Low-grade fumarate hydratase–deficient tumours must be excluded by immunohistochemistry, but they are recognizable by their heterogeneous architecture and high-grade features {26900816}.

Cytology

Not relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: a well-circumscribed tumour with small to intermediate-sized tubules and larger cysts; regular hobnail, cuboidal, or flattened cells with large nucleoli; fibrotic stroma.

Desirable: exclusion of other tumours with tubulocystic morphology or fumarate hydratase deficiency.

Staging

Staging follows the Union for International Cancer Control (UICC) criteria for clear cell renal cell carcinoma.

Prognosis and prediction

Pure TcRCC is usually an indolent tumour with a good prognosis {26310887}, with rare reports of metastasis {19011562; 31441383; 33354487}.
