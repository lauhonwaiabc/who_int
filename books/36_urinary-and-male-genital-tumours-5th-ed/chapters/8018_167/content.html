8.0.1.8 Clear cell adenocarcinoma

Definition

Clear cell adenocarcinoma is a malignant epithelial tumour consisting of Müllerian-type epithelium with clear cell morphology.

ICD-O coding

8310/3 Clear cell adenocarcinoma

ICD-11 coding

2C9Y & XH6L02 Other specified malignant neoplasms of urinary tract & Clear cell adenocarcinoma, NOS

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Intratesticular or paratesticular

Clinical features

The patient age range is 43–71 years (mean: 52 years). Patients present with an intratesticular or paratesticular mass, with or without local or abdominal pain.

Epidemiology

Clear cell adenocarcinoma is a very rare tumour, with only 3 cases reported {15260857; 30921621}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Unknown

Macroscopic appearance

These are solid or cystic tumours with a haemorrhagic cut surface.

Histopathology

The tumours consist of polygonal, columnar, or hobnail cells with clear cytoplasm and show a solid, tubulocystic, or papillary architecture. Nuclear atypia is mild to moderate; the mitotic count may be low. By immunohistochemistry, the tumours are positive for CK7, EMA, PAX8, and AR, and negative for WT1, CA19-9, CK20, calretinin, D2-40, ER, and PR {30921621}. Despite a low mitotic count, the Ki-67 index is high {30921621}. The differential diagnosis includes mesothelioma (positive for calretinin and D2-40) {16295392}; serous carcinoma (more pronounced cellular budding and more frequent psammoma bodies) {8653294}; and metastatic renal cell carcinoma, particularly with clear cell morphology {11469344; 32190396; 16512598}.

Cytology

Not relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: a tumour with clear and hobnail cells in a solid, tubulocystic, confluent, or complex papillary pattern; exclusion of metastasis.

Desirable: immunoreactivity for CK7 and PAX8; negativity for mesothelial markers and RCCm.

Staging

There is no American Joint Committee on Cancer (AJCC) staging system for these tumours.

Prognosis and prediction

Both an aggressive and an indolent course have been reported, as well as response to radiotherapy {30921621}.
