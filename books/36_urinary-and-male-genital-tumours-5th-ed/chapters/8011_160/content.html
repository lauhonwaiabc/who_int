8.0.1.1 Serous cystadenoma

Definition

Serous cystadenoma is a benign epithelial neoplasm lined by cells resembling fallopian tube epithelium.

ICD-O coding

8441/0 Serous cystadenoma, NOS

ICD-11 coding

2F34 & XH8TJ0 Benign neoplasm of male genital organs & Serous cystadenoma, NOS

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Paratestis or testis

Clinical features

Serous cystadenomas are usually cystic tumours. They may cause symptoms related to mass effects, or they may be incidental findings.

Epidemiology

These are rare tumours. Patient ages range from 35 to 73 years.

Etiology

Ovarian-type epithelial tumours of the testis are believed to originate from remnants of Müllerian ducts in paratesticular connective tissue, epididymis, and spermatic cord, or from Müllerian metaplasia of the mesothelium of the tunica vaginalis of the testis {28543997}.

Pathogenesis

Unknown

Macroscopic appearance

The cut surface of the testis shows circumscribed cystic lesions about 35 mm in size {32619438}.

Histopathology

Tumours are cystic and contain serous fluid. The cysts are lined by a non-stratified cuboidal to ciliated columnar epithelium with abundant pale cytoplasm and bland nuclei {32619438}.

Immunohistochemically, the tumour cells are positive for pancytokeratin, WT1, PAX8, ER, PR, and CK7, and they are negative for OCT3/4 and calretinin. Ki-67 expression is low {28543997}.

The differential diagnosis is with reactive mesothelial hyperplasia.

Cytology

No published data

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: cystic tumours lined by a single layer of ciliated columnar or cuboidal epithelium without atypia or mitotic figures.

Desirable: positive immunohistochemistry for ER, PR, WT1, and PAX8; negative immunohistochemistry for calretinin.

Staging

Not applicable

Prognosis and prediction

Serous cystadenomas are benign. Tumour resection is curative.
