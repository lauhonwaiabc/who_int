3.1.3.1 Clear cell papillary renal cell tumour

Definition

Clear cell papillary renal cell tumour (CCPRCT) is an indolent renal epithelial neoplasm composed of low-grade clear cells arranged in tubules, nests, and papillae with luminally oriented nuclei. These tumours have a characteristic immunophenotype with diffuse CK7 and cup-like CAIX (CA9) staining.

ICD-O coding

8323/1 Clear cell papillary renal cell tumour

ICD-11 coding

2C90.0 & XH9T60 Renal cell carcinoma of kidney, except renal pelvis & Clear cell papillary renal cell carcinoma

Related terminology

Not recommended: clear cell tubulopapillary renal cell carcinoma; clear cell papillary renal cell carcinoma; renal angiomyoadenomatous tumour.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Renal cortex

Clinical features

The vast majority of these tumours are incidentally detected.

Epidemiology

The tumour accounts for approximately 3% of cases of renal cell carcinoma (RCC) in adults, with an age range of 8–88 years {24182559; 29133143}. The tumours can occur in end-stage renal disease {16434887}. Tumours of identical morphology that occur in patients with von Hippel–Lindau syndrome appear genomically related to clear cell RCC and distinct from CCPRCT {25081542; 23648463}. In 20% and 25% of cases, patients have multifocal and bilateral renal tumours, respectively, and in > 50% of these patients, there is a discordant subtype {33046271}.

Etiology

The etiology of these tumours is not known. The tumours can occur in end-stage renal disease but there are other associated risk factors.

Pathogenesis

There are no known disease-specific genetic abnormalities that indicate a driver for tumorigenesis. The tumour has a low mutation burden {31175917; 23887297}. Instead of characteristic genomic alterations, these tumours exhibit a unique metabolic profile with depletion of mitochondrial DNA and resultant oxidative stress with the accumulation of sorbitol {30924768}.

Macroscopic appearance

The tumours are usually small (< 40 mm); well-circumscribed, and encapsulated, and cystic change can occur {18594469; 21707708; 26426379; 24182559; 29133143}. Tumours are nearly always confined and > 95% are pT1.

Histopathology

These tumours are composed of an admixture of tubules, papillae, nests, and cysts in varying proportions. Cells are cuboidal to low columnar with small nucleoli (histological grade 1 or 2) {20924276; 24485757; 28130179; 21707708}. CCPRCT can be nearly entirely cystic {31356826}. The nuclei are oriented towards the luminal aspect of the tubules and papillae. There may be an associated fibromyomatous stromal component, but evidence suggests that tumours with conspicuous fibromyomatous stroma may be a distinct renal epithelial neoplasm harbouring mutations in TSC1, TSC2, MTOR, and ELOC (TCEB1) {25970683; 30986793; 2597068; 31850909}. There may be morphological overlap with low-grade clear cell RCC, but the presence of high-grade areas, tumour necrosis, and local regional invasion indicate a clear cell RCC. Because CCPRCT and clear cell RCC may focally exhibit histologically identical areas, the unequivocal diagnosis of CCPRCT on needle biopsy may not be possible {26752401; 26457355; 23434146}.

Immunohistochemistry

The tumour exhibits a distinctive immunophenotype. The tumour is diffusely positive for CK7 and exhibits cup-like staining for CAIX with no luminal border staining. GATA3 may be positive, as are PAX2, PAX8, and 34βE12. AMACR and CD10 are negative or only patchy {27556823; 20924276; 21707708; 23238627; 21602815}.

Cytology

Not relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

There are specific molecular pathological features diagnostic of this tumour. Importantly, this tumour lacks chromosome 3p loss and alterations of VHL characteristic of clear cell RCC, and no mutations in TSC1, TSC2, MTOR, or ELOC (TCBE1) (all characteristic of RCC with fibromyomatous or leiomyomatous stroma) have been reported {31850909}.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: pT1 tumour (histological grade 1 or 2) composed of varying proportions of tubules, cysts, papillae, and small nests, in a fibrous/fibromyomatous stroma lacking VHL gene abnormalities (mutation, hypermethylation, or loss of heterozygosity at 3p).

Desirable: immunophenotype of diffuse CK7 staining, cup-like CAIX staining, and patchy to negative CD10 expression.

Staging

Nearly all tumours (> 95%) are pT1N0M0. Tumours are confined and there is no invasion of vessels, lymphatics, renal sinus, or peripheral renal fat {20924276; 24485757; 28130179; 21707708}.

Prognosis and prediction

CCPRCTs are indolent tumours {20924276; 24485757; 28130179; 21707708; 26426379}.
