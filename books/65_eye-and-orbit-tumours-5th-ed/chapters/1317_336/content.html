1.3.1.7 Combined naevus

Definition

Combined naevus is a conjunctival compound or subepithelial melanocytic naevus with multiple histopathological phenotypes (clones) within the same lesion.

ICD-O coding

8720/0 Combined naevus

8780/0 Combined conventional and blue melanocytic naevus

8720/1 Combined conventional melanocytic naevus and WNT-activated deep penetrating / plexiform melanocytoma (naevus)

8720/0 Combined conventional and granular cell melanocytic naevus

8722/0 Combined conventional and balloon cell melanocytic naevus

ICD-11 coding

2F20.Y & XH0DU8 Other specific types of melanocytic naevus & Combined naevus

Related terminology

Acceptable: biphenotypic naevus; combined conjunctival melanocytoma.

Subtype(s)

Combined conventional and blue melanocytic naevus; combined conventional melanocytic naevus and WNT-activated deep penetrating / plexiform melanocytoma (naevus); combined conventional and granular cell melanocytic naevus; combined conventional and balloon cell melanocytic naevus

Localization

The distribution of combined naevi seems to be similar to that of conventional naevi; they are most common on the bulbar conjunctiva {10496382;31558492;31469587;19667333;26002588}.

Clinical features

Combined naevi may show atypical features, including atypical dark coloration, irregular pigmentation, and a history of recent growth, prompting surgical excision or even raising the clinical suspicion of a melanoma {10496382;31558492;31469587;19667333}.

Epidemiology

Combined blue melanocytic naevi and combined WNT-activated deep penetrating / plexiform melanocytomas (naevi) (DPMNs) are relatively common {10496382;14769591;31558492}. Combined balloon cell naevi and combined granular cell naevi are rare. Age distribution seems to be similar to that of conventional naevi; they are most common in children and young adults but may affect elderly people {10496382;31558492;31469587;19667333;26002588;34818422}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

It is currently unknown whether, similarly to combined WNT-activated DPMNs, the development of a blue, balloon cell, or granular cell component within a conventional naevus is caused by a secondary genetic event or is only a morphological spectrum of conventional naevi {10496382;31558492;31469587;19667333;26002588;34818422}.

Macroscopic appearance

See Clinical features.

Histopathology

Combined melanocytic naevi are composed of a conventional naevus component (including inflamed juvenile naevus) and another morphologically distinct component {30122258}.

In combined blue naevus, a subepithelial blue naevus component is characterized by moderately to heavily pigmented fusiform and dendritic melanocytes. The extent of the blue naevus component varies from focal to extensive; it tends to be located in the deepest part of the naevus but may be superficial {10496382;4014368}.

In combined WNT-activated DPMNs, a WNT-activated component is composed of nests of relatively large, finely pigmented ovoid or epithelioid melanocytes accompanied by melanophages (see section WNT-activated deep penetrating / plexiform melanocytoma (naevus)) {31558492;33005620}.

Combined granular cell naevus, characterized by large melanocytes with centrally placed nuclei, inconspicuous nucleoli, and abundant eosinophilic to lightly pigmented granular cytoplasm, has also been reported in the conjunctiva {31469587;19667333}. The number of cases is small, and genetic changes are not yet well characterized, making it difficult to determine if these represent a distinct subtype. In contrast to those in WNT-activated DPMN, melanocytes in granular cell naevus lack nuclear expression of β-catenin {34818422}.

Balloon cell naevus is composed of large epithelioid melanocytes with abundant vacuolated, clear, or slightly foamy cytoplasm, usually lacking melanin pigment. There is no significant cytological atypia or mitotic activity. Balloon cells tend to lack expression of HMB45 {26002588}.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: the presence of two or more morphologically distinct melanocytic naevus components in a single lesion; a melanoma ex naevus must be ruled out.

Staging

Not clinically relevant

Prognosis and prediction

Combined naevi are benign lesions {10496382;31558492;31469587;26002588;34818422}.
