6.1.2.5 Epithelial-myoepithelial carcinoma

Definition

Epithelial-myoepithelial carcinoma (EMC) is a low-grade malignant biphasic tumour of the lacrimal gland characterized by tubular structures, usually composed of tightly coupled inner ductal and prominent outer myoepithelial cells.

ICD-O coding

8562/3 Epithelial-myoepithelial carcinoma

ICD-11 coding

2D0Y & XH9JP2 Other specified malignant neoplasms of eye or ocular adnexa & Epithelial-myoepithelial carcinoma

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Lacrimal gland

Clinical features

EMC usually presents as a slow-growing painless orbital mass with proptosis. It may present with a history of a rapid increase in size, diplopia, pain, and restriction of ocular movements {30080758;15534138;19667347;32341837;21546298}. Associated lymphadenopathy is rare.

Imaging

EMC is frequently well defined and multilobulated on CT and MRI, with intermediate signals in T1- and T2-weighted images. It enhances heterogeneously.

Epidemiology

EMC is extremely rare in the lacrimal gland {32332692;32341837}, with an M:F ratio of 3:2. The median age at diagnosis is approximately 59.6 years (range: 29–86 years).

Etiology

EMC arises from intercalated ducts {19542868}. It may arise as a de novo lesion or in a pre-existing pleomorphic adenoma {32332692;26231189;32341837}.

Pathogenesis

A significant number of EMCs harbour HRAS mutations, most commonly at codon 61 {30994537;33481348}.

Macroscopic appearance

The cut surface shows a greyish-white, lobulated, firm mass {32332692}. Cystic changes may be noted {30080758}.

Histopathology

EMC generally exhibits a multinodular invasive growth pattern. The histological hallmark is the biphasic arrangement of inner, smaller, eosinophilic ductal epithelial cells and outer, relatively larger, often clear myoepithelial cells {6950345;17197918}. The myoepithelial component varies from single layered to multilayered or even with solid areas. Nuclear atypia tends to be mild to moderate. Cribriform, basaloid, sebaceous, oncocytic, and double-clear histological variations have been described {17197918;19492889;22427262;29135517}. High-grade transformation rarely occurs {20679885}.

Immunohistochemistry

The inner cells are positive for pancytokeratin and CK7, whereas the outer myoepithelial cells are usually positive for p63, SMA, calponin, and S100 {17197918;33526226}. Diffuse and membranous/cytoplasmic RAS p.Q61R expression, almost always restricted to myoepithelial cells, has been observed in 65% of EMCs {33481388;30994537} and helps in their differentiation from EMC ex pleomorphic adenoma and also from its histological mimics {30994537}.

Differential diagnosis

The entities include other lacrimal gland tumours with biphasic or/and clear cell morphology, such as adenoid cystic carcinoma, basal cell adenoma/adenocarcinoma, pleomorphic adenoma, myoepithelial carcinoma, and clear cell carcinoma {33526226;33481348;30994537}.

Cytology

Aspirates classically show tubular structures with a dual epithelial and myoepithelial cell population with mild to moderate atypia {9839128;12619100;32332692}.

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Assessment of HRAS mutations is useful for diagnosing EMC and excluding its mimics {30994537;33526226}. Identification of MYB fusion by FISH or immunohistochemistry may help in differentiation of these tumours from adenoid cystic carcinoma and hybrid tumours {29135517}.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: usually multinodular invasive growth; at least partly with a dual arrangement of inner ductal cells and outer prominent, and usually clear, myoepithelial cells.

Staging

EMC is staged according to the eighth-edition Union for International Cancer Control (UICC) TNM staging system for lacrimal gland tumours.

Prognosis and prediction

EMC has a favourable prognosis but occasionally recurs locally; lymph node and distant metastases are rare. High-grade transformation, myoepithelial differentiation, solid growth pattern, increased mitotic activity, Ki-67 > 10%, perineural invasion, and necrosis are indicators of aggressive biology and might indicate poor prognosis {32332692;7197918;20679885}.
