10.2.4.2 Epithelioid haemangioendothelioma

Definition

Epithelioid haemangioendothelioma (EHE) is a fusion gene–driven sarcoma composed of epithelioid cells with endothelial differentiation.

ICD-O coding

9133/3 Epithelioid haemangioendothelioma

9133/3 Epithelioid haemangioendothelioma with WWTR1::CAMTA1 fusion

9133/3 Epithelioid haemangioendothelioma with YAP1::TFE3 fusion

ICD-11 coding

2B5Y & XH9GF8 Other specified malignant mesenchymal neoplasms & Epithelioid haemangioendothelioma

Related terminology

Not recommended: malignant epithelioid haemangioendothelioma.

Subtype(s)

Epithelioid haemangioendothelioma with WWTR1::CAMTA1 fusion; epithelioid haemangioendothelioma with YAP1::TFE3 fusion

Localization

Lesions can occur in the orbit (bone, soft tissue), skin, and lacrimal gland {27132490;23514025;30675477;1480392;20139294}.

Clinical features

Patients present with a slowly growing mass, which is sometimes painful owing to vascular occlusion. Other symptoms are site- and size-dependent including proptosis, dystopia, and visual loss. There is a propensity for lymph node metastases. Multifocal disease may occur {31950476;31463731;27132490}.

Epidemiology

Patient age ranges from childhood to elderly people; young to middle-aged adults are mainly affected {22420724;24986479;31950476;31463731}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

As a specific driver genetic alteration, the WWTR1::CAMTA1 fusion gene occurs in most EHEs (~90%) {11342784;21584898;21885404;25961935}. Remaining cases display a YAP1::TFE3 fusion gene {23737213;24986479}. The chimeric transcription factors are oncogenic owing to diminished negative regulation by the Hippo pathway {25961935;33766982;33766984;32675324} and recruitment of epigenetic modifiers {33913810}.

Macroscopic appearance

EHE presents as a (multi)nodular mass with a pale, solid cut surface, sometimes with haemorrhage {24986479}.

Histopathology

Epithelioid-, spindle-, and histiocytoid-appearing endothelial cells with minimal cytological atypia are arranged in short cords and strands in a myxohyaline stroma. They show abundant hyaline cytoplasm with subtle intracytoplasmic lumina. Striking nuclear atypia is seen in approximately 30% of tumours. Clusters of epithelioid and spindle cells with round to ovoid nuclei and occasional vacuolated cytoplasm with signet ring–cell appearance are observed. Binucleated and multinucleated cells may occur. Mitotic activity is usually low {32797671;34381186}. Multicellular vascular channels can be present in individual cases and are reported to be more prominent in lesions associated with YAP1::TFE3.

Immunohistochemistry

CD31 and ERG are consistently expressed. CAMTA1 and TFE3 are expressed depending on the fusion gene present {7093931;23737213;21584898;24986479;31950476;31463731;34381186}.

Cytology

Not relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Identification of a WWTR1::CAMTA1 or YAP1::TFE3 fusion helps distinguish EHE from other tumours.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: strands, cords, and nests of epithelioid, spindle, and histiocytoid cells with usually minimal cytological atypia, glassy cytoplasm, and subtle intracytoplasmic lumina; myxohyaline background; positivity for CD31 and ERG.

Desirable: occasionally well-formed vessels (EHE with YAP1::TFE3); positivity for CAMTA1 or TFE3, and/or detection of the related fusion gene.

Staging

The Union for International Cancer Control (UICC) and American Joint Committee on Cancer (AJCC) TNM8 staging system for soft tissue sarcoma of the trunk and extremities is used.

Prognosis and prediction

Most cases are indolent. There is a risk of metastatic disease and a significant mortality rate (as high as 45%) {31950476;31463731;34381186}.
