10.1.1.7 Dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans

Definition

Dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans (DFSP) is a locally aggressive fibroblastic neoplasm with a cellular storiform appearance and carries a COL1A1::PDGFB or related fusion.

Related terminology

Not recommended: Bednar tumour.

Subtype(s)

Pigmented DFSP; fibrosarcomatous DFSP; myxoid DFSP; DFSP with myoid differentiation; plaque-like DFSP

Localization

Periocular DFSP most commonly arises in the superficial periocular skin and secondarily involves deeper orbital soft tissues and the nasolacrimal system {29217050;21804433;15266155;24988500;15234144}. Primary orbital DFSP is extremely rare {30981544;23448305;23514021}. Advanced tumours can involve bone, paranasal sinuses, and the cranial cavity {25924890;28905843}.

Clinical features

Signs and symptoms include a nodular or multinodular painless mass, often with a history of indolent but persistent growth, sometimes with rapid enlargement, proptosis, ptosis, epiphora, and decreased visual acuity {30981544}.

Epidemiology

DFSP is very rare in the orbit/adnexa with < 20 reported cases with a wide age range (26–72 years) {30981544}.

Etiology

Most of these tumours occur sporadically.

Pathogenesis

DFSP is commonly characterized by a reciprocal linear translocation or supernumerary rings involving chromosomes 17 and 22, leading to fusion transcripts that place the PDGFB gene under the control of the COL1A1 promoter {12661001;12550751;8988177}.

Macroscopic appearance

DFSP lesions may appear as plaque-like or nodular/multinodular masses with a firm grey-white cut surface.

Histopathology

Classic DFSP is characterized by a storiform, cartwheel, whorled, or fascicular arrangement of monomorphic, cytologically uniform slender spindle cells containing plump or elongated wavy nuclei. Mitotic activity is low. Inflammatory cells may be scant to absent. Rare cases may show prominent vessels, granular cell change, prominent nuclear palisading, and Verocay body formation {22257899;26725533}. Rarely DFSP can show myoid components, prominent myxoid change {6614310;17721193}, giant cells, pigmented cells {3901787}, and fibrosarcomatous areas {9591728;10935653;16625088}. Exceptionally, transformation to pleomorphic sarcomatous areas has been reported {9591728;21478726}.

Immunohistochemistry

Tumour cells stain positively for CD34 and may express EMA {12426982;15252314}. Fibrosarcomatous areas in a DFSP can show loss of CD34 expression {9591728}. Tumour cells in myoid nodules and bundles stain strongly for SMA {8720984}.

Differential diagnosis

This includes dermatofibroma, which is more polymorphous and lacks the infiltrative pattern and diffuse CD34 positivity of DFSP. Spindle cell melanoma, spindle cell carcinoma, and leiomyosarcoma may be excluded by appropriate immunohistochemical stains (see also section Invasive cutaneous melanoma, section Leiomyosarcoma, and section Squamous cell carcinoma of the eyelid).

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Detection of the COL1A1::PDGFB fusion can help in diagnosis of challenging cases; however, cases with cryptic fusions {25081750;26970672} and alternative fusions involving PDGFD are also reported {29955147;30014607}.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: a storiform architecture and uniform spindle-cell morphology; diffusely infiltrative growth; expression of CD34.

Desirable: COL1A1::PDGFB gene fusion or rarely alternative PDGFD rearrangements (in select cases).

Staging

The American Joint Committee on Cancer (AJCC) or Union for International Cancer Control (UICC) TNM system can be applied for fibrosarcomatous DFSP.

Prognosis and prediction

DFSP is characterized by locally aggressive growth and frequent, often repeated local recurrences unless widely excised {21804433;30981544;15266155}. Distant metastases from periocular DFSP have not been documented {30981544}. DFSP arising in non-ocular sites can rarely metastasize to the lungs. Tyrosine kinase inhibitors have been used as adjuvant and palliative agents in locally advanced periocular DFSP {27051721;28905843}.
