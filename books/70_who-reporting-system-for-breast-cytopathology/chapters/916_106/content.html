9.1.6 Sample reports: Malignant

Sample report 1:

Clinical and imaging findings

Palpable hard mass left 2 o’clock 50 mm to nipple; mammogram shows round 20 mm tumour, and ultrasound shows irregular lesion

Type of specimen

FNAB, two passes with 23 gauge needle

Diagnostic summary

Site: Left 2 o’clock 50 mm to nipple

Category: Malignant

Diagnosis: Carcinoma

Material received

Three air-dried smears, one alcohol-fixed smear

Microscopic features

These highly cellular smears show small syncytial tissue fragments of epithelial cells with large pleomorphic nuclei with large single often angulated nucleoli, along with dispersed single similar cells with high N:C ratios and pale cytoplasm in a background of stripped nuclei, plentiful lymphocytes, and some necrotic debris.

Comment: Carcinoma is present with features suggesting a medullary pattern. There is no material for ICC, and repeat FNAB for cell block preparation or CNB is recommended.

Sample report 2

Clinical and imaging findings

Impalpable 20 mm firm mass right breast at 10 o’clock, 20 mm to nipple. Mammogram shows spiculated mass with occasional calcifications. Ultrasound shows 18 mm spiculated mass and abnormal 15 mm diameter round lymph node in axilla.

Type of specimen

FNAB, three passes with 23 gauge needle, right breast FNAB, two passes with 23 gauge needle, right axillary lymph node

Diagnostic summary

Specimen 1:

Site: Right 10 o’clock 20 mm to nipple

Category: Malignant

Diagnosis: Invasive lobular carcinoma

Specimen 2:

Site: Right axillary lymph node

Category: Malignant

Diagnosis: Metastatic carcinoma

Material received

Specimen 1: Four air-dried Giemsa smears from ROSE, two alcohol-fixed smears, 2 mL cloudy rinsings spun for cell block

Specimen 2: Two air-dried Giemsa smears from ROSE, one alcohol-fixed smear, 1 mL cloudy rinsings for cell block (spun down, no plug for processing)

Microscopic features

Specimen 1: These mildly cellular smears show dispersed small to medium epithelial cells with mildly atypical irregular nuclei, inconspicuous nucleoli, and eccentric cytoplasm sometimes containing a single large vacuole. A very occasional loosely cohesive irregular tiny tissue fragment of similar cells is present.

Comment: Carcinoma is present with features suggesting a lobular carcinoma.

Specimen 2: These highly cellular smears show small to intermediate epithelial cells with mildly pleomorphic nuclei and eccentric cytoplasm with frequent single vacuoles, seen dispersed and occasionally in small crowded, discohesive tissue fragments in a background of limited lymphocytes.

Comment: Metastatic carcinoma is present with the same features as seen in Specimen 1.

Supplementary report 1

Specimen 1: The cell block shows a small number of carcinoma cells seen singly and in small tissue fragments, strongly positive for CK7 and ER, negative for PR, and negative for HER2 (ERBB2). The carcinoma is negative for E-cadherin, confirming the lobular carcinoma of breast.

(Editor’s comment: The report has been issued as “Carcinoma” and “Metastatic carcinoma”, and a subsequent integrated report has been issued with the ICC confirming the “Lobular carcinoma”.)

Sample report 3

Clinical and imaging findings

40 mm palpable mass right 12 o’clock 25 mm to nipple; ultrasound shows round lesion ? fibroadenoma

Type of specimen

FNAB, two passes with 23 gauge needle

Diagnostic summary

Site: Right 12 o’clock 25 mm to nipple

Category: Malignant

Diagnosis: Carcinoma with features suggesting a mucinous carcinoma

Material received

Four air-dried smears Giemsa stain from ROSE, two alcohol-fixed smears

Microscopic features

These moderately cellular smears show epithelial cells in small sheets and occasional 3D tissue fragments and singly, with mildly atypical and enlarged nuclei and eccentric cytoplasm with occasional large vacuoles, in a background of abundant fibrillary mucin. A branching thin capillary fragment is present within the mucinous background.

Comment: The features of this carcinoma suggest a mucinous carcinoma. Final classification awaits assessment of the excised carcinoma.

Sample report 4

Clinical and imaging findings

Hard rapidly growing 30 mm mass in patient with history of breast carcinoma, right breast 9 o’clock 40 mm from breast

Type of specimen

FNAB, three passes with 22 gauge needle

Diagnostic summary

Site: Right 9 o’clock 40 mm to nipple

Category: Malignant

Diagnosis: Malignant phyllodes tumour

Material received

Six air-dried smears, three alcohol-fixed smears

Microscopic features

These highly cellular smears show large cellular tissue fragments consisting of plump spindle cells showing marked nuclear pleomorphism in a variable stroma, along with occasional hyperplastic ductal epithelial tissue fragments. Occasional mitoses are present in the stromal cells. In the background, dispersed single plump spindle cells, some with markedly pleomorphic hyperchromatic nuclei, are present.

Comment: This is a malignant fibroepithelial tumour with a benign epithelial tissue fragment component consistent with a malignant phyllodes tumour.
