8.0.1 Sample reports: Suspicious for malignancy

Sample report 1

Type of specimen

FNAB, three passes, 23 gauge needle

Clinical and imaging information

67-year-old woman with 40 mm palpable, mammographically poorly defined left breast mass at 3 o’clock 20 mm from nipple; ultrasound shows irregular echodense lesion

Diagnostic summary

Site: Left 9 o’clock 20 mm from nipple

Category: Suspicious for malignancy

Diagnosis: Suspicious for lobular carcinoma

Material received

Two air-dried slides

Microscopic features

The direct smears show a few discohesive minute tissue fragments and dispersed single mildly enlarged epithelial cells, with a high N:C ratio and enlarged nuclei with irregular nuclear contours and prominent nucleoli, along with fibroadipose tissue fragments. Very occasional cells have eccentric cytoplasm containing single vacuoles. There is no material for cell block.

Comment: The features are suspicious of a lobular carcinoma.

Sample report 2

Specimen type

FNAB, one pass, 22 gauge needle

Clinical and imaging information

45-year-old with 20 mm palpable breast mass at 3 o’clock 25 mm from nipple; mammography shows rounded mass, with minor lobulation

Diagnostic summary

Site: Left 9 o’clock 20 mm from nipple

Category: Suspicious for malignancy

Diagnosis: Suspicious for low-grade carcinoma

Material received

Two air-dried slides

Microscopic features

These mildly cellular smears show small cohesive, crowded tissue fragments, some with a tubular architecture, and focally a small number of single cells with mildly enlarged nuclei, occasional nucleoli, and eccentric cytoplasm. No definite myoepithelial cells on tissue fragments. No bare bipolar nuclei are present in the background. There is no stroma.

Comment: The features are suspicious of a low-grade carcinoma.

Sample report 3

Type of specimen

FNAB, two passes with 23 gauge needle

Clinical and imaging information

28-year-old pregnant woman with 30 mm palpable mass in right breast at 3 o’clock 20 mm from nipple; ultrasound shows rounded mass

Diagnostic summary

Site: Right 3 o’clock 20 mm from nipple

Category: Suspicious for malignancy

Diagnosis: Suspicious for carcinoma

Material received

Three air-dried slides, one alcohol-fixed slide

Microscopic features

Very occasional small discohesive tissue fragments of large cells with large atypical nuclei and prominent single nucleoli are present in a background of milk, cellular debris, and small discohesive sheets of small cells with variably bubbly vacuolated cytoplasm and small mildly pleomorphic nuclei.

Comment: There are a very small number of atypical cells in a lactational background, regarded as suspicious for carcinoma.
