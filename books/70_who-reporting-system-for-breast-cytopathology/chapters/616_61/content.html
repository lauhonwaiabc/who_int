6.1.6 Sample reports: Benign

The sample reports provided here are based on the standardized cytopathology report in section Diagnostic categories and report structure. See that section for a discussion on the structure and components.

These are sample reports that include the components required in one possible format; the final report format will depend on local practice and local laboratory information system requirements. For brevity, demographics and ROSE are not presented here.

Each institution should select a term for the main heading of the diagnostic summary, such as “Diagnostic summary” (as used in the examples below), “Diagnosis”, or “Final diagnosis”, and use it consistently.

Sample report 1

Specimen type

FNAB, three passes with 23 gauge needle

Clinical and imaging findings

Palpable irregular thickening in left breast at 2 o’clock, 40 mm to nipple. Mammogram shows nonspecific benign region. Ultrasound shows possible fibrocystic change.

Diagnostic summary

Site: Left 2 o’clock 40 mm to nipple

Category: Benign

Diagnosis: Fibrocystic change

Material received

Three air-dried Giemsa smears from ROSE, one alcohol-fixed smear

Microscopic features

Mildly cellular smears show apocrine sheets and occasional ductal epithelial tissue fragments with myoepithelial cells in a proteinaceous background with some histiocytes. The cell block shows occasional apocrine sheets and histiocytes.

Comment: Fibrocystic change is present.

Sample report 2

Specimen type

FNAB, two passes 22 gauge needle

Clinical and imaging features

35-year-old woman with left central mobile rounded 25 mm lesion, which was well circumscribed on mammography and ultrasound

Diagnostic summary

Site: FNAB left central

Category: Benign

Diagnosis: Fibroadenoma

Material received

Four air-dried smears for Giemsa and two alcohol-fixed Pap smears; no cell block

Microscopic features

These highly cellular smears show predominantly large cohesive tissue fragments of ductal epithelial cells with myoepithelial cells, a small number of dispersed columnar cells, plentiful bare bipolar nuclei, and scattered myxoid stromal fragments.

Sample report 3

Specimen type

FNAB, three passes

Clinical and imaging features

32-year-old with firm poorly defined 20 mm region right breast at 10 o’clock, 20 mm from nipple. Ultrasound shows irregular mass with spiculated margin on inferior aspect. Ceased breast feeding 6 months previously.

Diagnostic summary

Site: Right 10 o’clock 20 mm from nipple

Category: Benign

Diagnosis: Organizing abscess; see Comment below

Material received

Eight air-dried smears, four Giemsa-stained (ROSE), four for special stains. Two alcohol-fixed smears. 3 mL viscous yellow fluid for microbiology. 2 mL yellow fluid for cell block.

Microscopic features

Fragments of granulation tissue are present with neutrophils, prominent histiocytes, some lymphocytes and multinucleated histiocytes with abundant cytoplasm, and very occasional small poorly formed epithelioid granulomas without necrosis. Scattered fat cells and one tissue fragment of necrotic fat are present. No organisms are seen in the routine stains, and Gram, auramine for mycobacteria, and methenamine stains on direct smears and cell block are negative.

Comment: The features suggest an organizing abscess, but there are occasional poorly formed epithelioid granulomas. Correlation with microbiological findings is recommended.

Supplementary report 1

Staphylococcus aureus is present in cultures. PCR for mycobacterial infection is negative.

(Editor’s comment: A supplementary report has been issued integrating the microbiological findings.)

Sample report 4

Specimen type

FNAB, three passes

Clinical and imaging findings

Woman, 42 years old with right 15 mm subareolar firm nodule, with intermittent pale to dark nipple discharge. Imaging shows an oval 12 mm lesion within an apparent duct.

Diagnostic summary

Site: Right subareolar nodule

Category: Benign

Diagnosis: Intraductal papilloma with epithelial hyperplasia; see comment below

Specimen received

Four air-dried Giemsa-stained slides (ROSE); two alcohol-fixed slides for Pap

Microscopic findings

Moderately cellular smears with large cohesive tissue fragments of ductal epithelial cells with myoepithelial cells, stellate fibroelastotic papillary tissue fragments with attached epithelial sheets with myoepithelial cells, apocrine sheets, plentiful siderophages in a proteinaceous background

Comment: Intraductal papilloma with epithelial hyperplasia is present. Correlation with imaging is required. The patient has nipple discharge and excision biopsy of the specific duct is recommended.

Sample report 5

Specimen type

FNAB three passes 23 gauge needle

Clinical and imaging findings

45-year-old woman with right breast lump. Note: subsequent discussion with clinician provided history of a lesion in the left lateral breast predominantly involving skin of the breast. Uncertain duration.

Diagnostic summary

Site: Right breast

Category: Benign

Diagnosis: Nodular fasciitis

Material received

Three air-dried, two alcohol-fixed direct smears; 2 mL bloodstained fluid processed to cell block

Microscopic features

These moderately cellular smears show myxoid tissue fragments with plump spindle cells and dispersed single spindle cells with oval nuclei, fine chromatin, and small single nucleoli. Occasional larger cells with mildly pleomorphic nuclei are present. The background is finely granular in some smears and bloody in others. The cell block shows similar material.

Comment: The features favour those of nodular fasciitis. USP6 FISH is underway on the cell block to confirm the diagnosis in view of the uncertain length of time the lesion has been present.

Supplementary report 1

USP6 FISH is positive, confirming the diagnosis of nodular fasciitis.

(Editor’s comment: The cytopathologist has sought extra clinical information from the clinician before issuing the initial report, alerting the clinician that FISH has been ordered. The FISH has then been integrated into the final report, where the diagnosis is firmed up.)

Sample report 6

Specimen type

FNAB, four passes

Clinical and imaging findings

65-year-old woman with possible recurrence in right 12 o’clock 30 mm from nipple wide local excision scar; 10 mm partially stellate lesion on ultrasound

Diagnostic summary

Site: Right 12 o’clock 30 mm from nipple

Category: Benign

Diagnosis: Fat necrosis in scar

Material received

Six air-dried Giemsa smears (ROSE) and three alcohol-fixed smears for Pap; no cell block material

Microscopic features

Scattered tissue fragments of necrotic, anucleate fat are present in a background of granular blue, brown, yellow, and red punctate material, fat globules, and occasional histiocytes. Two cohesive tissue fragments of ductal cells with myoepithelial cells show mild nuclear enlargement and occasional large hyperchromatic nuclei in large cells.

Comment: Fat necrosis with minor epithelial component showing features consistent with therapy.
