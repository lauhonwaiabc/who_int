5.0.0.5 Sample reports: Insufficient/Inadequate/Non-diagnostic

Sample report 1

Nature of specimen

FNAB, one pass 23 gauge needle

Clinical and imaging information

11 o’clock, right breast, 40 mm from nipple, 25 mm solid irregular mass

Diagnostic summary

Site: Right breast, 11 o’clock, 40 mm from nipple

Category: Insufficient

Material received

Three air-dried smears, one alcohol-fixed smear

Microscopic findings

Scant ductal epithelial cells with air-drying artefact, insufficient for diagnostic purposes.

Sample report 2

Nature of specimen

FNAB, two passes, 23 and 25 gauge needles

Clinical and imaging information

Left breast, 2 o’clock, 60 mm from nipple, 30 mm palpable mass, solid mildly heterogeneous mass on imaging with focally bosselated margins

Diagnostic summary

Site: Left breast, 2 o’clock, 60 mm from nipple

Category: Insufficient

Material received

Four air-dried Giemsa-stained smears from ROSE, two alcohol-fixed smears

Microscopic findings

A proteinaceous background is present with no epithelial component.

Sample report 3

Nature of specimen

FNAB, three passes, 23 gauge needle

Clinical and imaging information

Left breast, 20 mm subareolar mass, with irregular margins; nipple retraction

Diagnostic summary

Site: Left subareolar

Category: Insufficient

Material received

Four air-dried Giemsa-stained smears from ROSE, and two alcohol-fixed slides

Microscopic findings

Scattered small tissue fragments of epithelial cells are present showing marked crush artefact and obscuring blood, preventing diagnostic assessment.

Sample report 4

Nature of specimen

FNAB, two passes 23 gauge needle

Clinical and imaging information

10 o’clock, 20 mm from nipple, 35 mm oval mass with calcifications

Diagnostic summary

Site: Right 10 o’clock, 20 mm from nipple

Category: Insufficient

Material received

Six air-dried slides, and two alcohol-fixed smears

Microscopic features

Scant scattered epithelial cells are present obscured by ultrasound gel, which prevents diagnostic assessment.
