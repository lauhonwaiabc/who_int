8.1 Tumours of the male breast: Introduction

The male breast is a rudimentary organ, whose glandular composition is limited to ducts located in the retroareolar region. Male breast epithelium shows ER, PR, and AR expression and is exposed to hormonal variation throughout life. In particular, sex chromosome copy-number variations have been demonstrated to be quite frequent in ageing men {23299533}. The male breast can be affected by a variety of benign and malignant diseases over a wide age range. Almost all diseases and conditions of the female breast occur in men. Nevertheless, benign and malignant lesions with papillary architecture appear to be more frequent in the male breast {31403965}.

Clinical and radiological presentation can be similar in benign and malignant nodules {30122313}, most of which appear as retroareolar lumps. The cytological and histological preoperative diagnoses are similar to those encountered in the female breast. Conversely, the rarity of individual male breast entities makes the acquisition of sufficient knowledge to establish diagnostic, prognostic, and predictive criteria difficult.

Among male breast lesions, male breast cancer is rare (accounting for < 1% of all breast cancers), recurrences are frequent, and a high proportion of patients die {30267249}. Although male breast cancer has been treated in a similar manner to female postmenopause breast cancer over the last decade, studies based on multi-institutional series have shed light on male breast cancer pathogenesis, showing differences from female breast carcinoma. Molecular studies have shown a high number of differently expressed genes {20625818;26960396;34642874}, among which AR seems to play a role as driver in the neoplastic transformation of male breast epithelial cells.

A potential further challenge might arise from gender-affirming care, either male to female or female to male. Little evidence is currently available, but published data indicate that hormonal stimulation might induce breast cancer development {30087072;30037639;36789830}.
