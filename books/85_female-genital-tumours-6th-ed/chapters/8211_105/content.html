8.2.1.1 Serous cystadenoma, serous adenofibroma, and serous cystadenofibroma

Definition

Serous cystadenoma, serous adenofibroma, and serous cystadenofibroma are benign ovarian serous tumours composed of lining epithelial cells resembling fallopian tube epithelium.

ICD-O coding

Classification in progress, Data not available in this Beta

ICD-11 coding

Classification in progress, Data not available in this Beta

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Ovary

Clinical features

Patients often present with symptoms related to an ovarian mass, or tumours may be found incidentally.

Epidemiology

Serous cystadenomas and serous cystadenofibromas are among the most common benign ovarian neoplasms.

Patients typically present between 40 and 60 years of age {14984967; 33320368}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Most serous cystadenomas develop from polyclonal expansion of epithelial inclusions; some are monoclonal but lack BRAF or KRAS mutations {15077125}. DNA copy-number changes may be seen in the stromal fibromatous cells and (rarely) in epithelial cells {21976534}.

Macroscopic appearance

Tumours vary widely in size. Cystadenomas have smooth outer and inner surfaces; they may be septated and are filled with watery fluid. Cysts < 1 cm are designated as cortical inclusion cysts. Cystadenofibromas contain cyst(s) surrounded by a solid component. Adenofibromas are typically solid and punctated with small cysts.

Histopathology

The epithelial lining of these tumours consists of non-stratified cuboidal or columnar cells (resembling tubal secretory or ciliated cells) in varying proportions. In some tumours, the epithelial lining is flattened and nondescript. Serous cystadenomas are predominantly cystic. Serous adenofibromas are composed of small glands and cysts in a prominent fibromatous stroma. Serous cystadenofibromas have cyst(s) and often exhibit broad-based papillary projections with prominent fibromatous stroma.

The presence of a minor component of epithelial proliferation that would otherwise qualify as serous borderline tumour, comprising < 10% of the total tumour tissue volume (or < 0.5 cm3), warrants designation as serous cystadenoma/adenofibroma with focal epithelial proliferation {18156968; 15297960}; however, distinction of this entity from an early serous borderline tumour is arbitrary for equivocal cases. If there is focal epithelial proliferation involving the ovarian surface or with micropapillary architecture, serous borderline tumour should be considered.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: tumour with benign serous epithelium, with or without fibromatous stroma; for cystadenoma, the cysts should be > 1 cm.

Staging

These are benign tumours and are not staged.

Prognosis and prediction

Serous cystadenomas, serous adenofibromas, and serous cystadenofibromas are benign, but they may recur after incomplete excision. Tumours with focal intracystic epithelial proliferation are also considered to be benign, but data are limited.
