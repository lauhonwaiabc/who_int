6.2.0.3 Papillary cystadenoma

Definition

Papillary cystadenoma is a benign papillary cystic tumour of probable mesonephric origin.

ICD-O coding

Classification in progress, Data not available in this Beta

ICD-11 coding

Classification in progress, Data not available in this Beta

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Uterine ligaments {3341511; 22317868; 22296276} and less commonly fallopian tube {25366076} and ovary {31985579}

Clinical features

The tumour occurs in adults and may present with abdominal pain; some cases are incidental findings {22296276}.

Epidemiology

Papillary cystadenoma is extremely rare. Most patients have von Hippel–Lindau syndrome {22296276; 22317868; 3341511; 2386149}.

Etiology

The anatomical location and immunophenotype suggest a mesonephric origin {22296276}.

Pathogenesis

Allelic loss of VHL has been reported {11493991}.

Macroscopic appearance

The tumours range from < 10 to 80 mm, have a thick fibrotic capsule, and are traversed by fibrous bands creating a cystic-solid lobulated appearance {22296276; 9421096}.

Histopathology

Tumours are characterized by tubulocystic to papillary growth with varying broad-based and delicate branching and stromal hyalinization. Focal solid growth may be present. The cells are cuboidal to low columnar and non-ciliated, with clear to eosinophilic cytoplasm. The nuclei exhibit uniform chromatin and inconspicuous nucleoli. Prominent subepithelial blood vessels are common. Cytological atypia, mitotic activity, or necrosis are typically absent. The cells demonstrate expression of epithelial and mesothelial markers. Tumours are frequently positive for AE1/AE3, EMA, CK7, PAX8, and PAX2. WT1, CD10, and calretinin are variably positive; ER and PR are negative {22296276; 24441657; 22317868; 15767808; 31985579}.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Demonstration of VHL mutation may be helpful.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: a cystic/lobulated mass; complex tubulopapillary growth of non-ciliated and cuboidal cells.

Desirable: von Hippel–Lindau syndrome.

Staging

Not applicable

Prognosis and prediction

The vast majority are benign. Rare exceptions have been reported {22296276; 38914169}.
