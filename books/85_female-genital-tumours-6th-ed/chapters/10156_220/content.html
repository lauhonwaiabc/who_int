10.1.5.6 Dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans

Definition

Dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans (DFSP) is a superficial, intermediate, locally aggressive spindle cell neoplasm with a diffusely infiltrative, storiform growth pattern. Fibrosarcomatous DFSP is characterized by a fascicular growth pattern, increased mitotic activity, and metastatic potential.

ICD-O coding

Classification in progress, Data not available in this Beta

ICD-11 coding

Classification in progress, Data not available in this Beta

Related terminology

Not recommended: Bednar tumour.

Subtype(s)

Fibrosarcomatous dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans

Localization

Vulvar DFSP arises in labia majora, mons pubis, and clitoral/paraclitoral area, in descending order of frequency {28323651}.

Clinical features

Patients with vulvar DFSP typically present with a slow-growing mass, ranging from 5 to 150 mm (median: 40 mm) {20139758}.

Epidemiology

DFSP is a rare tumour for which a limited number of cases have been reported in literature. These cases describe a broad age range for the symptom onset (range: 27–73 years), with a mean of 45.4 years {20139758; 28323651; 40814369; 40861842}.

Etiology

Most tumours develop sporadically.

Pathogenesis

DFSP often has a COL1A1::PDGFB fusion, which typically results from supernumerary ring chromosomes (derived from chromosomes 17 and 22) or an unbalanced t(17;22)(q21.3;q13.1). The latter is more commonly found in paediatric patients {20139758; 36810566}.

Macroscopic appearance

Tumours are infiltrative or poorly defined with a fibrous appearance.

Histopathology

Conventional DFSP is a tumour of uniform, hyperchromatic spindled cells with a storiform growth pattern that infiltrates the dermis and subcutis in a lace-like or honeycomb pattern. Mitotic activity is low. Patterns include myxoid, pigmented, and myoid. Myxoid DFSP is composed of spindle to stellate cells uniformly distributed in myxoid stroma with a more apparent capillary vasculature {17890911; 17721193; 26355764}. Pigmented DFSP has scattered dendritic pigmented S100-positive cells. Myoid DFSP has myoid cells concentrically arranged around vessels {25943241}. Tumours with features of giant cell fibroblastoma show multinucleated cells, myxoid stroma, and pseudovascular spaces and often have a conventional DFSP component {21319518; 17415117}.

Fibrosarcomatous DFSP is characterized by an abrupt transition to a herringbone pattern of spindled cells with greater cytological atypia and increased mitotic activity relative to conventional DFSP {20139758}.

By immunohistochemistry, CD34 is frequently diffusely positive in conventional DFSP, but expression may be reduced or absent in myxoid or fibrosarcomatous DFSP {29140881; 26355764; 20139758}.

Cytology

The diagnostic value of cytology is limited. If well-sampled, smears are variably cellular and composed of tissue fragments of cohesive spindle cells showing mild to moderate nuclear atypia, uniform chromatin, and a storiform architecture within a collagenous matrix, along with single cells and stripped nuclei in the background {15830369}.

Diagnostic molecular pathology

PDGFB or PDGFD rearrangements can be detected in selected settings.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: conventional DFSP: storiform pattern; uniform spindled cells; fibrosarcomatous DFSP: fascicular architecture; increased nuclear atypia and mitotic activity.

Desirable: CD34 staining (uniform positivity for DFSP; reduced or absent staining for fibrosarcomatous DFSP); COL1A1::PDGFB gene fusion or, rarely, alternative PDGFD rearrangements in selected settings.

Staging

The Union for International Cancer Control (UICC) / American Joint Committee on Cancer (AJCC) TNM staging system is applicable for fibrosarcomatous DFSP.

Prognosis and prediction

DFSP has a propensity for local recurrence unless widely excised {15503305}. Fibrosarcomatous DFSP has metastatic potential {20139758}.
