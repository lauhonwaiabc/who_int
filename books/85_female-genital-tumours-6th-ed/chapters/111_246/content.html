11.1 Peritoneal mesothelial tumours: Introduction

In the sixth edition of the WHO classification of Female genital tumours, peritoneal mesothelial tumours, including adenomatoid tumour, well-differentiated papillary mesothelial tumour, and mesothelioma, are discussed together in this chapter and separated from epithelial and mesenchymal tumours, as well as metastatic entities that can involve the abdominal/pelvic peritoneum. The epithelial tumours of Müllerian origin are discussed in the chapters on epithelial tumours of the fallopian tube and ovary; the mesenchymal and metastatic entities are described in the respective chapters on mesenchymal tumours and metastasis. In addition, the following have been removed as they represent tumour-like lesions and are not considered neoplastic: mesothelial hyperplasia, peritoneal inclusion cysts, transitional cell metaplasia, endosalpingiosis of peritoneum, histiocytic nodule, ectopic decidua, splenosis, and other tumour-like lesions of the peritoneum.
