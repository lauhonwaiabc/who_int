3.2.1.4 Squamous cell carcinoma, HPV status undetermined

Definition

Squamous cell carcinoma (SCC), HPV status undetermined, is an invasive and/or exophytic malignant epithelial tumour exhibiting squamous differentiation and in which the HPV status is undetermined.

Histopathology

Vaginal squamous cell carcinoma should be classified according to HPV status by ancillary testing (such as p16 immunohistochemistry and HPV testing by in situ hybridization or other molecular techniques). In cases where ancillary testing of the tumour tissue is not feasible or the results are inconclusive, the designation squamous cell carcinoma, HPV status undetermined, is used. It is important to note that even in the absence of testing, most of these tumours are likely to be HPV-associated, although at a lower rate than in cervical squamous cell cancers {30561092; 34650187}.
