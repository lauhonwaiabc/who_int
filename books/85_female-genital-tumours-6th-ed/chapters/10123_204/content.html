10.1.2.3 High-grade endometrial stromal sarcoma

Definition

High-grade endometrial stromal sarcoma is a malignant endometrial stromal tumour with uniform high-grade round and/or spindle morphology, sometimes with a low-grade component.

ICD-O coding

Classification in progress, Data not available in this Beta

ICD-11 coding

Classification in progress, Data not available in this Beta

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Uterus

Clinical features

Patients present with abnormal uterine bleeding, pelvic pain/pressure, or a uterine mass.

Epidemiology

These tumours are rare {30324234} and account for around 0.2% of all uterine malignancies. Their annual incidence is 1–2 per million women. Compared with other uterine malignancies, these malignancies can affect patients across a wide age range (17 to 79 years), with the majority being perimenopausal {22754201}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Tumours harbour YWHAE::NUTM2A/B fusions {22223660; 22456610; 23599159; 24592973; 27219024; 28390819}, ZC3H7B::BCOR fusions {23580382; 29192652; 27631520; 30789359}, or BCOR internal tandem duplication (BCOR ITD) {29200103; 28621321; 30380541}. Less common variants include fusions between BCOR and alternate partner genes such as EPC1, EP300, L3MBTL2, LPP, NUTM2G, RALGPS1, MAP7D2, RGAG1, ING3, NUGGC, KMT2D, KCTD15, and CREBBP; genetic fusions involving BCORL1 (JAZF1::BCORL1, EP300::BCORL1, EPC1::BCORL1); and BCOR/BCORL1 internal gene rearrangements {30144186; 28331900; 30789359; 32156473; 34302054; 35499168; 39921027}. Tumours harbouring these less common variant BCOR or BCORL1 fusions display similar global methylation and gene expression profiles as tumours harbouring ZC3H7B::BCOR or BCOR ITD {33077922; 35499168}. There is also emerging evidence that tumours harbouring KDM2B fusion such as EPC1::KDM2B may be similar to tumours harbouring BCOR fusion {38501593; 40865919}. Biologically, YWHAE::NUTM2 oncoprotein regulates proliferation and cyclin D1 via RAF/MAPK and Hippo pathways {33947829}. A subset of tumours with YWHAE or BCOR/BCORL1 abnormalities can also harbour concurrent CDK4, CCND2, and/or MDM2 amplification with CDKN2A/B loss {32156473; 35499168; 32352245; 36788095}. BCOR- and YWHAE-altered tumours frequently express high levels of NTRK3, FGFR3, RET, GLI1, and PTCH1 mRNA {33077922}. High-grade histological transformation of recurrent endometrial stromal sarcoma harbouring classic low-grade genetic fusion has been linked to the development of ESR1 hotspot mutation that is associated with endocrine therapy resistance {34961764}. In contrast to undifferentiated uterine sarcoma, high-grade endometrial stromal sarcomas harbouring recognized genetic fusion/ITD are consistently TP53 wild type {33118176}.

Macroscopic appearance

High-grade endometrial stromal sarcomas are typically bulky, intracavitary or intramural, tan-yellow, fleshy masses, often displaying haemorrhage and necrosis {22456610; 25355621; 29192652; 30380541}.

Histopathology

Tumours may show expansile, permeative, or infiltrative growth, and more than one pattern of invasion may be seen. The cytomorphology varies from a pure round cell, pure spindle cell, to a mixture of round and spindle cell proliferations, with monomorphic mild to moderate nuclear atypia and readily identifiable mitotic activity (> 4.21 mitoses/mm2, equating to > 10 mitoses/10 HPF of 0.55 mm in diameter and 0.24 mm2 in area). Lymphovascular invasion and necrosis are usually present. A subset of cases may contain a low-grade component with minimal nuclear atypia and infrequent mitotic activity, and this low-grade component may predominate, underscoring the need for adequate tumour sampling. Less commonly, tumours harbouring high-grade genetic fusion / ITD may display purely low-grade histology {37032555; 37562066; 36811828; 37562066}, although recurrences may exhibit high-grade histology {28863072}.

Tumours with YWHAE::NUTM2A/B fusion display round cells with eosinophilic cytoplasm and high-grade nuclei with irregular nuclear contours, vesicular chromatin, and variably distinct nucleoli with high mitotic activity. They may be associated with a fibromyxoid or conventional low-grade endometrial stromal component {22456610; 23599159; 25355621; 28863072}. Pseudopapillary/glandular architecture, rhabdoid morphology, pseudorosettes, and sex cord–like differentiation are rare {21737666; 22456610}. The high-grade component is positive for cyclin D1 (diffuse and strong nuclear expression), BCOR (diffuse and strong nuclear expression), KIT, CD56, and CD99, and negative for DOG1 {24186140; 22456610; 28621321}. Tumours may also show pan-TRK staining unrelated to NTRK rearrangement {33077922}.

Tumours with ZC3H7B::BCOR or variant BCOR::BCORL1 fusions are comprised of a haphazard fascicular spindle cell proliferation with fibrous to fibromyxoid stroma showing mild to moderate nuclear atypia and usually at least focally high mitotic activity. They typically show strong and diffuse nuclear cyclin D1 expression and diffuse CD10 positivity with variable ER and PR positivity. In contrast to the other genotypes of high-grade endometrial stromal sarcoma, only 50% of cases express BCOR {29192652}. They may show focal expression of SMA and caldesmon but are negative for desmin. Tumours with ZCH37B::BCOR fusion may also show pan-TRK staining unrelated to NTRK rearrangement {33077922}.

Tumours with BCOR ITD can be comprised of a mixture of round and spindle cell proliferation or a pure haphazard spindle cell proliferation. The tumour cells display mild to moderate nuclear atypia with readily identified mitotic activity. Some tumours exhibit a cytologically bland and mitotically quiescent low-grade spindle cell component. Collectively, they typically show diffuse and strong nuclear cyclin D1 and/or BCOR expression {29200103; 30789359; 33077922; 33718245; 34456278; 35174661; 35499168}. They may express CD10, particularly in the spindle cell component, and are usually negative for ER and PR, although focal positivity may be seen in a subset of cases. Furthermore, they may express desmin but are negative for SMA and caldesmon. Similar to tumours with BCOR and YWHAE fusion, tumours with BCOR ITD show pan-TRK staining unrelated to NTRK rearrangement {33077922}.

If no supporting genetic fusion / ITD can be demonstrated in a suspected high-grade endometrial stromal sarcoma after appropriate genetic analyses have been performed, one should reconsider such a diagnosis, particularly if the genetic analysis demonstrates the presence of a TP53 mutation and/or a high degree of copy-number alterations that are usually not seen in molecularly confirmed high-grade endometrial stromal sarcomas {33118176; 34302054; 32156473; 35499168; 32352245; 36788095}.

Rare endometrial stromal sarcomas harbouring genetic fusions typically associated with low-grade endometrial stromal sarcoma can undergo high-grade histological transformation either de novo in part of the primary tumour or in recurrences. Such cases may be classified as low-grade endometrial stromal sarcoma with high-grade transformation at this time {18580489; 32317704}, although experience and prognostication remain limited. High-grade transformed tumours typically show loss of CD10, ER, and PR expression.

Cytology

Smears display discohesive small cells with scant cytoplasm and metachromatic, extracellular hyaline material {9067109}.

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Demonstration of gene fusion (YWHAE::NUTM2A/B fusions, ZC3H7B::BCOR fusions, variant BCOR and BCORL1 gene fusions) or BCOR ITD may be helpful for diagnosis.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: monomorphic high-grade round and/or spindle cells with brisk mitotic activity (at least focal).

Desirable: diffuse cyclin D1 and/or BCOR immunohistochemical positivity; molecular confirmation of YWHAE fusion, BCOR/BCORL1 fusion, and/or BCOR ITD.

Staging

This entity is staged as uterine sarcoma using the FIGO, Union for International Cancer Control (UICC), and American Joint Committee on Cancer (AJCC) systems.

Prognosis and prediction

High-grade endometrial stromal sarcomas are more aggressive than low-grade endometrial stromal sarcomas, especially tumours with YWHAE::NUTM2A/B fusion and concurrent CDKN2A loss {36788095}. Experience remains limited regarding the clinical behaviour of molecularly high-grade endometrial stromal sarcomas showing purely low-grade histology or of molecularly low-grade endometrial stromal sarcomas showing high-grade histology. Anthracycline-based therapy may benefit patients with recurrent YWHAE::NUTM2A/B sarcoma {28390819}.
