15.1 Melanocytic lesions: Introduction

In this sixth edition of the Female genital tumours volume of the WHO Classification of Tumours series, melanocytic lesions for all genital tract sites are discussed together in this chapter. This is the same as was done in the fifth edition, with the same six sections, except that the section title for Acquired melanocytic naevus has been changed to section Junctional, compound, and dermal naevi , to align with the terminology used in the Skin tumours volume of this series. The reason for including melanocytic lesions in this edition of the Female genital tumours volume rather than simply referring to the Skin tumours volume was 2-fold. The atypical melanocytic naevus of genital type is specific to the female genital tract, although there are some clinical, pathological, and molecular differences between genital tract and cutaneous lesions for the other entities that do occur in both the genital tract and skin; these are highlighted in this volume.
