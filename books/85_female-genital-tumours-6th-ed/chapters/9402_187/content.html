9.4.0.2 Rete cystadenoma and rete adenoma

Definition

Rete cystadenoma and rete adenoma are benign tumours derived from the rete ovarii.

ICD-O coding

Classification in progress, Data not available in this Beta

ICD-11 coding

Classification in progress, Data not available in this Beta

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

These tumours are located at the ovarian hilum where the rete ovarii is present.

Clinical features

Rete cystadenomas are typically encountered in postmenopausal women, who may present with pelvic/abdominal discomfort or signs of virilization due to elevated serum levels of testosterone {21139896; 3804558}. Rete adenomas are typically incidental findings in perimenopausal or postmenopausal women {9416702; 2852648}.

Epidemiology

These lesions are very rare, with limited data on incidence. Rete cystadenoma is the most common {2852648}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Unknown

Macroscopic appearance

Rete cystadenomas are typically unilateral with a hilar location but may uncommonly be bilateral. They range in size (mean: 90 mm) and may be unilocular or multilocular, with thin walls, smooth lining, and clear fluid {2852648; 15991159}. Rete adenomas are typically well-circumscribed white and spongy lesions centred in the ovarian hilum {2852648; 9416702}.

Histopathology

Rete cystadenomas are centred in the ovarian hilum and are characterized by dilated irregularly spaced crevices with an inner epithelial lining composed of flat to cuboidal cells with scant eosinophilic to clear (uncommon) cytoplasm and minimal nuclear stratification. Cilia are absent. Cytological atypia and mitoses are rare or absent {2852648; 9416702}. Hilar Leydig cells may form a band peripheral to the cyst wall. Rete ovarii can also be seen in the vicinity {3804558}. Rete adenomas are also centred in the ovarian hilum; they are well circumscribed and composed of closely packed small elongated to round and dilated tubules, some with focal, variably complex intraluminal papillae. The lining epithelial cells are cuboidal to slightly columnar, with scant eosinophilic to pale cytoplasm, and lack significant cytological atypia and mitotic activity. They may contain Leydig/luteinized cells within the scant intervening stroma {2852648; 9416702}. The immunophenotype of the lining cells has not been well studied, but the cells are positive with epithelial markers {9416702}.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: rete cystadenoma: hilar location, cyst lined by non-ciliated epithelium; rete adenoma: hilar location, well-circumscribed; small compact tubules; no cytological atypia.

Staging

These are benign neoplasms and are not staged.

Prognosis and prediction

Rete cystadenomas and rete adenomas are benign {2852648; 9416702}.
