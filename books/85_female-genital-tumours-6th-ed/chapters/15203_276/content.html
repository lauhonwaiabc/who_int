15.2.0.3 Blue naevus

Definition

Blue naevus is a benign subepithelial melanocytic neoplasm with a component of characteristic dendritic cells.

ICD-O coding

Classification in progress, Data not available in this Beta

ICD-11 coding

Classification in progress, Data not available in this Beta

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

Cellular blue naevus (87900/0)

Localization

Genital blue naevi are predominantly reported in the vaginal mucosa {589575}, but they can also occur in the cervix area, including the endocervix {689487}, and on the clitoris {27924655}. Vulvar lesions are rare.

Clinical features

Blue naevus typically presents as an incidental small brown or black macular or papular lesion with a blue hue. Cellular blue naevus is larger and can become a pedunculated mass protruding into the vaginal cavity {21477275}. Multiple lesions have been described {10664947}.

Epidemiology

Blue naevi are less common in the genital area than are other tumour types. In adults, vulvar melanocytic naevi are generally found in women in the reproductive age group {28778635; 25267379}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Blue naevi are thought to arise from precursors derived from dermal melanoblasts migrating to the skin during embryogenesis {24460190}. They are associated with activating mutations in GNAQ, GNA11, PLCB4 (or less frequently in CYSLTR2) {19078957} or gene fusions in PRKCA or PRKCB {37474004}.

Macroscopic appearance

See Clinical features, above.

Histopathology

Blue naevi are located in the subepithelial tissue and form poorly circumscribed aggregates of small, darkly pigmented dendritic or spindled melanocytes associated with stromal fibroplasia. Involvement of adnexal adventitia or perivascular space is common. Melanophages can be present in some lesions. Cellular blue naevi are defined by the presence of often well-demarcated nodules composed of less-pigmented uniform spindled or oval melanocytes with vesicular nuclei and small nucleoli, arising in the context of otherwise unremarkable common blue naevus.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: poorly circumscribed aggregates of pigmented dendritic melanocytes with fibroplasia.

Staging

Not applicable

Prognosis and prediction

The prognosis for blue naevus is excellent. As in other locations, transformation of a genital cellular blue naevus to melanoma can exceptionally occur {9700383}.
