8.4.1.1 Endometrioid adenofibroma

Definition

Endometrioid adenofibroma is a benign epithelial tumour exhibiting endometrioid differentiation.

ICD-O coding

Classification in progress, Data not available in this Beta

ICD-11 coding

Classification in progress, Data not available in this Beta

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Ovary

Clinical features

Patients may present with symptoms related to a pelvic mass, or the tumour may be an incidental finding.

Epidemiology

These are uncommon tumours typically occurring in postmenopausal women.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Endometrioid adenofibromas are sometimes associated with endometriosis or an endometriotic cyst {40527365; 21534769}.

Macroscopic appearance

Endometrioid adenofibromas are typically solid with a firm, white cut surface, sometimes with small cysts. They may form a nodule in the wall of an endometriotic cyst {21534769}.

Histopathology

Endometrioid adenofibromas are often associated with endometriosis. They are characterized by fibromatous stroma containing widely spaced, cytologically bland, endometrioid glands {8463037}. Morular, squamous, and mucinous differentiation can occur {8463037}. Dystrophic stromal calcifications may be present.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: widely spaced benign endometrioid glands associated with fibromatous stroma.

Desirable: endometriosis.

Staging

These are benign tumours and are not staged.

Prognosis and prediction

These tumours are benign.
