8.7.1 Sample reports: Malignant

The sample reports provided here are based on the standardized cytopathology report in the section Diagnostic categories and report structure section of the first chapter. See that section for a discussion on the structure and components.

These are sample reports that include the components required in one possible format; the final report format will depend on local practice and local laboratory information system requirements. For brevity, demographics and information on the cytopathology and ROSE are not presented here.

Each institution should select a term for the main heading of the diagnostic summary, such as “Diagnostic summary” (as used in the examples below), “Diagnosis”, or “Final diagnosis”, and use it consistently.

Sample report 1

73-year-old male

Target site and type of specimen:

Ultrasound FNAB left axillary lymph node

Clinical and imaging information:

Multiple superficial enlarged lymph nodes; fever, weight loss, asthenia. Peripheral lymphocytosis.

Material received:

Four air-dried Giemsa-stained and four alcohol-fixed Pap-stained direct smears; rinsed RPMI fluid for flow cytometry and rinsed fluid from a separate pass for cell block

 

(Editor’s note: The “Material received” section above is included in this report to demonstrate the placement of the record of the material received. For brevity it is not shown in the other sample reports.)

 

Diagnostic summary:

Category: MALIGNANT

Diagnosis: Chronic lymphocytic leukaemia / small lymphocytic lymphoma

 

Microscopic description:

Highly cellular smears show a monotonous lymphoid population of small cells with scant cytoplasm, round nuclei, clumped chromatin, and inconspicuous nucleoli. Flow cytometry shows coexpression of CD5, CD19, CD20, CD23, and CD200. CD10 is negative. Kappa light chain restriction is detected.

 

Comment: The cytopathological features are suggestive of a low-grade, small cell lymphoma, and chronic lymphocytic leukaemia / small lymphocytic lymphoma is confirmed by flow cytometry.

 

(Editor’s note: In this case, the cytopathologist has waited to review and include flow cytometry before issuing a final integrated report. An alternative approach would be to issue a provisional report based on just the cytopathology, categorizing the case as “Suspicious for malignancy”, stating that the findings were suspicious for chronic lymphocytic leukaemia / small lymphocytic lymphoma [CLL/SLL], and then issuing a supplementary report when the flow cytometry became available. The final report with the flow cytometry information would then include an updated categorization as “Malignant” with the final diagnosis of CLL/SLL.)

Sample report 2

78-year-old female

Target site and type of specimen:

Ultrasound FNAB right inguinal lymph node

Clinical and imaging information:

40 mm lymphadenopathy in the right inguinal region; absence of other clinically significant lymph nodes in other anatomical sites; weight loss, asthenia

 

Diagnostic summary:

Category: MALIGNANT

Diagnosis: Follicular lymphoma

 

Microscopic description:

These highly cellular smears show a dispersed mixed lymphoid population of immature lymphoid cells resembling centrocytes, intermingled with a variable number of small reactive mature lymphocytes and larger cells resembling centroblasts. Tingible-body macrophages are scant. Two or more mitoses per mm2 are detected.

Flow cytometry shows kappa light chain restriction; CD19 (dim), CD10, CD20, and CD23 expression; and negativity for CD5.

 

Comment: the cytomorphology and flow cytometry are those of follicular lymphoma. Confirmatory ancillary testing is underway.

 

Supplementary report 1:

On cell block, CD10, BCL2, and BCL6 are positive. Cyclin D1, SOX11, T-bet, MYC, and MUM1/IRF4 were negative. FISH demonstrates IGH::BCL2 fusion (t(14;18)(q32;q21)), confirming the follicular lymphoma diagnosis. BCL6 and MYC are not rearranged.

Sample report 3

54-year-old male

Target site and type of specimen:

Ultrasound FNAB left lateral cervical lymph node

Clinical and imaging information:

Multiple superficial and deep lymph nodes

 

Diagnostic summary:

Category: MALIGNANT

Diagnosis: Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma NOS. See Supplementary reports 1 and 2 below.

 

Microscopic description:

These highly cellular smears show a dispersed monotonous large lymphoid cell population with high N:C ratio, large cells showing round to more pleomorphic nuclei with one to three nucleoli, and a dense (Giemsa) cytoplasmic rim.

Occasional small mature lymphocytes are present in the background along with cytoplasmic fragments (“lymphoglandular bodies”). Numerous mitoses are detected.

 

Comment: The cytomorphological features are those of a high-grade lymphoma. Correlation with flow cytometry and further ancillary testing is required.

 

Supplementary report 1:

Flow cytometry is non-contributory (small lymphoid cell gate consisting of CD3-positive T lymphocytes and abundant debris).

On cell block, the large cells are positive for CD19, CD20, MUM1/IRF4, and BCL2, and negative for CD10, BCL6, CD5, and MYC. The Ki-67 is > 50%. Assessment of BCL2, BCL6, and MYC rearrangement performed by FISH is underway.

 

Supplementary report 2:

BCL2, BCL6, and MYC are not rearranged. A diffuse large B-cell lymphoma NOS of non–germinal-centre B-cell (GCB) type is present.

 

(Editor’s note: The initial diagnosis issued in this case would have been “Category: MALIGNANT and Diagnosis: Large cell lymphoma, ancillary tests underway”. The ancillary test results have been added in two supplementary reports, with the final diagnosis modified to reflect the results.)

Sample report 4

65-year-old male

Target site and type of specimen:

Endobronchial ultrasound–guided FNAB (EBUS-FNAB) subcarinal lymph node

Clinical and imaging information:

25 mm nodule in the left lower lobe of the lung; multiple enlarged lymph nodes in the left hilar and subcarinal areas

 

Diagnostic summary:

FNAB of subcarinal lymph node

Category: MALIGNANT

Diagnosis: Metastatic small cell neuroendocrine carcinoma

 

Microscopic description:

These highly cellular smears show pleomorphic intermediate cells with high N:C ratio, highly pleomorphic nuclei showing finely dispersed chromatin and occasional small nucleoli, and poorly defined cytoplasm. Nuclear moulding, chromatin smearing, and prominent apoptotic debris are present. Lymphocytes are present in the background.

 

Comment: Metastatic small cell neuroendocrine carcinoma is present.

 

Supplementary report:

The carcinoma is positive for TTF1, INSM1, chromogranin, and synaptophysin, and negative for p40, confirming small cell neuroendocrine carcinoma of lung.

 

(Editor’s note: In this case the exact site of the FNAB is stated in the diagnostic summary, which is a recommended option.)

Sample report 5

54-year-old female

Target site and type of specimen:

Ultrasound FNAB right supraclavicular lymph node

Clinical and imaging information:

15 mm lymphadenopathy. Prior history of metastatic breast carcinoma.

 

Diagnostic summary:

Right supraclavicular lymph node FNAB

Category: MALIGNANT

Diagnosis: Metastatic carcinoma of the breast

 

Microscopic description:

These highly cellular smears show tissue fragments of medium to large atypical cells with round to oval nuclei; small nucleoli; and dense, often eccentric cytoplasm containing in occasional cells a single vacuole. There is necrosis. Small lymphocytes are present in the background.

 

Comment: Poorly differentiated adenocarcinoma is present and immunocytochemistry is underway.

 

Supplementary report 1:

The carcinoma is positive for GATA3 and estrogen receptor, confirming metastatic breast carcinoma.

Predictive and prognostic biomarker analysis is underway.

 

Supplementary report 2:

Estrogen: strongly positive in 70% of nuclei;

Progesterone: moderately positive in 50% of nuclei;

ERBB2 (HER2): negative (1+);

Ki-67: 30%

 

(Editor’s note: The diagnostic summary includes the exact site of the FNAB. The initial report could appropriately have been issued with a diagnosis of “metastatic poorly differentiated adenocarcinoma”, and when the immunocytochemistry was available finalized as “metastatic carcinoma of breast”.)

Sample report 6

45-year-old male

Target site and type of specimen:

Ultrasound FNAB right axillary lymph node

Clinical and imaging information:

Multiple enlarged lymph nodes, the largest 32 mm in maximum diameter. No other medical history available.

 

Diagnostic summary:

Right axillary lymph node

Category: MALIGNANT

Diagnosis: Metastatic melanoma

 

Microscopic description:

These highly cellular smears show dispersed medium to large cells with round to occasionally more pleomorphic nuclei with prominent single and occasionally two nucleoli, which are irregular, and considerable, often eccentric cytoplasm. Some nuclei contain pseudoinclusions. Grey pigment (Giemsa) is present in the cytoplasm of some malignant cells and in macrophages and in the background. No lymphoid cells are seen in the background.

 

Comment: Metastatic melanoma is present. Correlation with clinical history is required.

 

Supplementary report 1:

The melanoma is positive for SOX10, S100, and MART1, confirming the diagnosis of metastatic melanoma.

Assessment of BRAF status by immunocytochemistry and NGS evaluation is underway.

 

Supplementary report 2:

The evaluation of BRAF through the mutation-specific antibody is positive. The BRAF p.V600E mutation is detected on NGS.

Sample report 7

32-year-old male

Nature of specimen:

Ultrasound-guided FNAB left lateral cervical lymph node

Clinical and imaging information:

13 mm hypoanechoic nodule with hyperechoic spots located in IV level of left lateral cervical region of the neck. Previous Hodgkin lymphoma, chemotherapy, and radiotherapy.

 

Diagnostic summary:

Left lateral cervical lymph node

Category: MALIGNANT

Diagnosis: Papillary thyroid carcinoma lymph node metastasis

 

Microscopic description:

In a cystic background with numerous macrophages and siderophages, papillary epithelial tissue fragments are present. The cells have distinct cytoplasm (Giemsa) and round to irregular nuclei with frequent grooves and occasional intranuclear pseudoinclusions.

 

Comment: Metastatic papillary carcinoma of thyroid.

 

Supplementary report:

No cell block is available. Thyroglobulin in the supernatant is positive.

Sample report 8

79-year-old female

Nature of specimen:

Ultrasound FNAB spleen

Clinical and imaging information:

Voluminous hypoechoic mass, inhomogeneous, which expands and deforms the inferior–posterior margin of the spleen

 

Diagnostic summary:

FNAB of spleen

Category: MALIGNANT

Diagnosis: Splenic angiosarcoma

 

Microscopic description:

These highly cellular smears show predominantly single, dispersed cells with only a few tissue fragments. The cells are polygonal to frequently spindle and show mild to moderate nuclear pleomorphism with few nucleoli. Some cells show cytoplasmic elongations. Within the tissue fragments, there are occasional poorly formed vessels, surrounded by spindle cells.

 

Comment: Malignant cells are present, with features suggestive of an angiosarcoma.

 

Supplementary report:

The cell block shows positivity for CD31, CD34, and ERG and negativity for cytokeratin AE1/AE3 / pankeratin, desmin, SMA, and S100 in the spindle cells, confirming the splenic angiosarcoma.
