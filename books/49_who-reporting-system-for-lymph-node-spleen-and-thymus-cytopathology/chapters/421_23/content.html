4.2.1 Sample reports: Insufficient/Inadequate/Non-diagnostic

The sample reports provided here are based on the standardized cytopathology report in the section Diagnostic categories and report structure section of the first chapter. See that section for a discussion on the structure and components.

These are sample reports that include the components required in one possible format; the final report format will depend on local practice and local laboratory information system requirements. For brevity, demographics and ROSE are not presented here.

For consistency, the term “Insufficient” has been selected for these three sample reports. Cytopathologists and their institutions should decide whether their laboratory will use “Insufficient”, “Inadequate”, or “Non-diagnostic” for this category and should not use these terms in an ad hoc way. See the introduction at the beginning of this chapter for a discussion regarding the use of the term “Non-diagnostic”.

Each institution should select a term for the main heading of the diagnostic summary, such as “Diagnostic summary” (as used in the examples below), “Diagnosis”, or “Final diagnosis”, and use it consistently.

Sample report 1

Specimen type:

FNAB

Clinical and imaging information:

25-year-old male, 15 mm palpable nuchal lymph node

 

Diagnostic summary:

Category: INSUFFICIENT

Diagnosis: Insufficient material for diagnostic assessment

 

Macroscopic description:

One air-dried slide and one alcohol-fixed slide. No fluid for flow cytometry or cell block.

 

Microscopic description:

No lymphoid or non-lymphoid cells are present. Repeat FNAB is recommended.

Sample report 2

Specimen type:

FNAB

Clinical and imaging information:

62-year-old female, 50 mm palpable left supraclavicular lymph node

 

Diagnostic summary:

Category: INSUFFICIENT

Diagnosis: Necrotic material, neoplastic process cannot be excluded

 

Macroscopic description:

Three air-dried slides and three alcohol-fixed slides. 3 mL pink fluid for cell block; no pellet on centrifuging, no cell block prepared.

 

Microscopic description:

Necrotic debris only is present and no viable cells are recognized. Repeat FNAB is recommended, with separate passes taken for acid-fast stain, microbiological studies, and cell block.

Sample report 3

Specimen type:

FNAB

Clinical and imaging information:

75-year-old male, history of mantle cell lymphoma. Currently, 30 mm mediastinal mass, EBUS-FNAB.

 

Diagnostic summary:

Category: INSUFFICIENT

Diagnosis: Insufficient material for diagnostic purposes

 

Macroscopic description:

Three air-dried slides and three alcohol-fixed slides.

 

Microscopic description:

A few small lymphocytes only are present.

 

Comment: There is insufficient lymphoid material for diagnostic assessment, and there was no flow cytometry sample submitted. Repeat FNAB is recommended, preferably with ROSE, and a separate pass for flow cytometry.
