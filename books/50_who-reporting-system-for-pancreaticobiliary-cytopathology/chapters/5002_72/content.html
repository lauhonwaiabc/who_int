5.0.0.2 Definition

A specimen categorized as “Atypical” demonstrates features predominantly seen in benign lesions and minimal features that may raise the possibility of a malignant lesion, but with features insufficient in either number or quality to diagnose a process or lesion as “Benign”, “Pancreaticobiliary neoplasm, low-risk/grade (PaN-low)”, “Pancreaticobiliary neoplasm, high-risk/grade (PaN-high)”, or “Malignant”.
