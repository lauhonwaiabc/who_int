3.2.1.5 Pilar sheath acanthoma

Definition

Pilar sheath acanthoma is a benign follicular adnexal neoplasm with infundibular and isthmic differentiation.

ICD-O coding

8104/0 Pilar sheath acanthoma

ICD-11 coding

2F22 & XH3EY8 Benign neoplasms of epidermal appendages & Pilar sheath acanthoma

Related terminology

Not recommended: infundibuloisthmicoma; outer root sheath acanthoma; infundibular adenoma.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

The most common location is the face, with a predilection for the upper lip {521535; 2697113; 718186; 6662259; 3557980}.

Clinical features

Pilar sheath acanthoma arises as a single papule or nodule with a central depression filled with keratin. Clinical diagnosis is usually as a giant comedo.

Epidemiology

Pilar sheath acanthoma is rare. It usually develops in middle-aged and elderly patients (age range: 30–76 years), with no sex predilection.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Unknown

Macroscopic appearance

Patients typically present with a papule with a central keratotic plug.

Histopathology

Pilar sheath acanthoma is a symmetrical, well-circumscribed, vertically oriented lesion. It consists of a large central crater filled by basket-weave orthokeratotic keratin and formed as a result of the confluence of several contiguous dilated follicular infundibula. Emanating from this central crater, in a radial arrangement, are solid lobules composed of pale eosinophilic cells similar to those seen in the outer root sheath of the isthmus of the hair follicle. These lobules often show peripheral palisading of the cells and contain small ducts lined by a wavy eosinophilic cuticle. In rare instances, the neoplastic lobules are distributed in a horizontal configuration, and they may contain ductal structures showing decapitation secretion or contain small clusters of mature sebocytes {28609346; 7235505}.

Differential diagnosis

This includes dilated pore of Winer and trichofolliculoma.

Cytology

Not relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: solid lobules of follicular isthmic epithelium around dilated infundibular structures.

Staging

Not relevant

Prognosis and prediction

Pilar sheath acanthoma is a benign neoplasm.
