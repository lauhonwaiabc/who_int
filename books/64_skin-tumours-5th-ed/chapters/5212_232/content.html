5.2.1.2 Epithelioid fibrous histiocytoma

Definition

Epithelioid fibrous histiocytoma (EFH) is a distinctive, usually exophytic and well-circumscribed benign cutaneous neoplasm composed of epithelioid cells, including binucleated forms. ALK rearrangement is present in the vast majority of cases.

ICD-O coding

8830/0 Epithelioid fibrous histiocytoma

ICD-11 coding

2E85.2 & XH15M9 Benign fibrohistiocytic tumour of skin & Epithelioid fibrous histiocytoma

Related terminology

Acceptable: epithelioid cell histiocytoma.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

EFH most commonly affects the lower extremities, followed by the upper extremities. The trunk and the head and neck region are uncommon sites {7909998; 10027517; 2466472; 8181804}.

Clinical features

EFH manifests as an exophytic nodule, often with a vascular appearance {2466472}.

Epidemiology

EFH typically arises in young to middle-aged adults (median age: 40 years), with a female predominance {7909998; 2466472; 8181804}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Although the histogenesis is unknown, EFH is probably biologically distinct from dermatofibroma (conventional fibrous histiocytoma) and its subtypes {26763770; 25857825}. EFHs harbour rearrangements of ALK (in ~90% of cases) or a PRKC gene (rarely), as mutually exclusive events {25857825; 24666231; 26121314}. SQSTM1 and VCL are the most common ALK fusion partners; rare fusions partners are DCTN1, ETV6, PPFIBP1, SPECC1L, TPM3, PRKAR2A, MLPH, CLTC, and EML4 {25413595; 29327718; 29329131; 35289445}.

Macroscopic appearance

Patients typically present with a well-circumscribed dermal papule.

Histopathology

EFH is usually well circumscribed; exophytic examples often have an adnexal collarette. The tumour is composed of uniform plump epithelioid cells with vesicular nuclei, small nucleoli, and abundant pale eosinophilic or amphophilic cytoplasm, often including binucleated forms. Small, thin-walled blood vessels may be prominent, sometimes with perivascular accentuation of tumour cells. Chondroblastoma-like pericellular calcification {29068085; 34086645} and lesions mostly composed of spindle cells {29430713; 33217002} have been described. ALK is overexpressed in about 90% of cases {25857825}, with higher positivity with the D5F3 or 5A4 clones than with the ALK1 clone {25857825; 34929229}, and EMA is positive in 65% {21752057}. CD30 expression may be seen {25857825; 24666231}. CD31, SMA, and desmin are usually negative {21752057; 25596243}.

Differential diagnosis

The differential diagnosis includes Spitz naevus, cutaneous syncytial myoepithelioma, epithelioid sarcoma, and cellular neurothekeoma.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: a well-circumscribed dermal tumour composed of uniform plump epithelioid cells with vesicular nuclei, small nucleoli, and abundant pale eosinophilic or amphophilic cytoplasm.

Desirable: usually positive for ALK.

Staging

Not relevant

Prognosis and prediction

Epithelioid fibrous histiocytoma is benign; rarely, it recurs locally {2466472; 8181804}.
