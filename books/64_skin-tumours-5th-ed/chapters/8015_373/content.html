8.0.1.5 Onychocytic matricoma

Definition

Onychocytic matricoma is a benign nail plate–producing tumour that usually presents as longitudinal pachymelanonychia.

ICD-O coding

8052/0 Onychocytic matricoma

ICD-11 coding

2F2Y Other specified benign cutaneous neoplasms

Related terminology

Acceptable: longitudinal subungual acanthoma.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Onychocytic matricoma is composed of areas of matrix epithelium and nail plate localized within a single nail unit.

Clinical features

Most reported cases of onychocytic matricoma have clinically presented as longitudinal pachymelanonychia (a longitudinal hyperpigmented streak in the nail unit associated with thickening of the nail plate). Rare cases with hypopigmentation have also been reported, where the thickened nail plate demonstrates longitudinal pachyxanthonychia (a yellow discolouration of the nail, with a thickened nail plate) {22197859; 23521538; 31090828}.

Epidemiology

Onychocytic matricoma is an unusual condition, and epidemiological studies have not been performed.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Unknown

Macroscopic appearance

See Clinical features, above.

Histopathology

Three main histopathological patterns have been described {22197859; 23521538}. The acanthotic pattern demonstrates acanthosis of the matrix epithelium and includes whorls of epithelium that may show differentiation into onychocytes (nail plate cells); the associated nail plate is thickened. The papillomatous pattern demonstrates papillomatous projections of matrix-derived epithelium associated with a thickened nail plate; there are cavities within the nail plate. The keratogenous pattern demonstrates a thickened keratogenous zone and forms a channel along the nail plate. The cells in the keratogenous zone show delayed maturation into onychocytes, which occurs in the channel at the distal aspect of the nail unit. None of these patterns have any dermal component, which helps in the differentiation from onychomatricoma and other nail unit tumours.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: the nail plate should be thickened in some area of the tumour; hyperplasia (in some form) of the matrix epithelium and no dermal component of the lesion.

Desirable: whorls of epithelium differentiating into onychocytes, papillomatous projections of the epithelium, or an expanded keratogenous zone, depending on subtype.

Staging

Not relevant

Prognosis and prediction

Onychocytic matricoma is a benign lesion, and the prognosis is excellent {31090828}.
