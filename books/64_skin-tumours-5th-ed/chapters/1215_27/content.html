1.2.1.5 Sclerosing/morphoeic basal cell carcinoma

Definition

Sclerosing/morphoeic basal cell carcinoma (BCC) is a BCC type characterized by very thin cords of basaloid cells surrounded by abundant collagenous stroma.

ICD-O coding

8092/3 Sclerosing/morphoeic basal cell carcinoma

ICD-11 coding

2C32.1 Sclerosing basal cell carcinoma of skin

Related terminology

Acceptable: morpheaform basal cell carcinoma.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

See the family description (section Basal cell carcinomas).

Clinical features

Patients with this type present with a poorly defined, scar-like plaque that rarely ulcerates or bleeds {16446711}. On dermoscopy it is typified by thin serpentine vessels.

Epidemiology

See the family description (section Basal cell carcinomas).

Etiology

See the family description (section Basal cell carcinomas).

Pathogenesis

See the family description (section Basal cell carcinomas).

Macroscopic appearance

See Clinical features, above.

Histopathology

Narrow cords of tumour (1–5 cells thick) are compressed by induced sclerotic collagenous stroma, disrupting the normal dermal architecture. Peripheral palisading and retraction artefact are uncommon. These tumours penetrate deeply and show an irregular/tentacular, deeply infiltrative border with the surrounding stroma. There is often overlap with infiltrating BCC, which lacks the prominent induced collagenous stroma.

Differential diagnosis

Sclerosing/morphoeic BCC can be difficult to distinguish from desmoplastic trichoepithelioma and microcystic adnexal carcinoma; immunostaining may facilitate this distinction. Fibroblast activation protein is positive in stromal fibroblasts of BCC but not in desmoplastic trichoepithelioma {20711172}. BerEP4 is expressed in BCC but not in microcystic adnexal carcinoma {23398472}. A cytoplasmic staining pattern of laminin-γ2 is found in infiltrating and morphoeic BCC, whereas in microcystic adnexal carcinoma, laminin-γ2 is present both along tumour nests and in the cytoplasm. Laminin-γ2 is negative in desmoplastic trichoepithelioma and syringoma {31429981}.

Cytology

Not relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: narrow cords of tumour compressed by sclerotic collagenous stroma.

Desirable: perineural invasion is commonly present.

Staging

Staging is according to the eighth edition of the Union for International Cancer Control (UICC) / American Joint Committee on Cancer (AJCC) TNM system.

Prognosis and prediction

The prognostic and predictive factors are similar to those of infiltrating and micronodular BCCs. This type of BCC has a tendency for perineural invasion and local recurrence; surgical excision with strict margin control is mandatory.
