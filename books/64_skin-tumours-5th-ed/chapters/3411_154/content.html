3.4.1.1 Hidradenoma papilliferum

Definition

Hidradenoma papilliferum is a benign tumour associated with anogenital mammary-like glands, characterized by branching and interconnecting epithelial and stromal elements, microscopically resembling mammary intraductal papilloma.

ICD-O coding

8405/0 Hidradenoma papilliferum

ICD-11 coding

2F22 & XH4DX4 Benign neoplasms of epidermal appendages & Papillary hidradenoma

Related terminology

Acceptable: papillary hidradenoma.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Most cases affect the vulva; less frequently, the perianal and perineal skin is involved.

Clinical features

The lesion occurs as a solitary asymptomatic nodule or cyst-like lesion measuring 2–20 mm {26863059}.

Epidemiology

Hidradenoma papilliferum is the most common benign glandular neoplasm of the vulva. It almost exclusively affects White women aged 20–89 years; it occurs rarely in children and in Black populations {10411423; 10986678}.

Etiology

Hidradenoma papilliferum is thought to arise from anogenital mammary-like glands {8005737}.

Pathogenesis

Tumorigenic alterations in the PI3K/AKT and MAPK pathway have been identified {28291131; 28648934; 27184479; 31010589; 26493284}.

Macroscopic appearance

The tumour is typically a tan or red nodule with a solid to variably cystic cut surface.

Histopathology

Hidradenoma papilliferum appears as a well-circumscribed intradermal solid-cystic nodule with a complex pattern of branching and anastomosing tubules interconnected in a labyrinthine manner, focally forming papillae. The tubules and papillae consist of a central axis of fibrous tissue lined by a luminal layer of epithelial cells surrounded by a layer of myoepithelial cells. Decapitation secretion is common.

Cystic change, sclerosing adenosis–like and mammary-type ductal changes, and necrosis have been identified. Oxyphilic metaplasia, especially in areas with solid growth with nuclear polymorphism, may simulate adenocarcinoma. Remnants of anogenital mammary-like gland adjacent to the lesion can be seen {26863059}.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

None

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: a solitary asymptomatic nodule or cyst-like lesion; complex growth of branching and anastomosing epithelial papillae and tubules with a myoepithelial cell layer.

Staging

Not clinically relevant

Prognosis and prediction

This is a benign neoplasm. Cases of carcinoma (including ductal carcinoma in situ) arising in hidradenoma papilliferum have been reported {33908057; 34926767; 1662944; 18580327; 18645314}.
