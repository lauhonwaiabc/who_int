5.8.0.4 Superficial CD34-positive fibroblastic tumour

Definition

Superficial CD34-positive fibroblastic tumour (SCD34FT) is a distinctive low-grade neoplasm of the skin and subcutis, characterized by a proliferation of spindled cells with characteristic morphological features and diffuse expression of CD34. PRDM10 rearrangements are often but not always present.

ICD-O coding

8810/1 Superficial CD34-positive fibroblastic tumour

8810/1 PRDM10-rearranged soft tissue tumour

ICD-11 coding

2B53.Y Other specified fibroblastic or myofibroblastic tumour, primary site

Related terminology

Acceptable: PRDM10-rearranged soft tissue tumour.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

This tumour most frequently occurs in the lower extremities (especially the thigh), followed by the arm, buttock, shoulder, and (rarely) vulva {23887307; 26946226; 27636918; 30004071; 34121219; 34969957}.

Clinical features

The tumour typically presents as a slow-growing, painless mass of the superficial soft tissues. A long prebiopsy duration (> 5 years) is often noted {23887307}. Rare cases present as deep-seated lesions {34121219}. Tumour size varies from 15 to 100 mm, but it is usually < 50 mm {23887307; 27636918; 28937375; 34121219; 34969957}.

Epidemiology

Slightly more than 100 cases have been reported. Most have occurred in middle-aged adults (median age: 38 years), with a slight male predominance {23887307; 27636918; 34121219; 34969957; 33969057; 32055270}.

Etiology

None

Pathogenesis

The morphological, immunohistochemical, and molecular genetic features of SCD34FT and tumours referred to as “PRDM10-rearranged soft tissue tumour” are identical, strongly supporting their unity {30570551; 34121219; 34969957}. The terminology “superficial CD34-positive fibroblastic tumour” is preferred, because not all tumours harbour demonstrable PRDM10 rearrangements. PRDM10 rearrangements have been identified and have been reported in close to 60% of cases previously classified as SCD34FT or PRDM10-rearranged soft tissue tumour {30570551; 34969957}. Specific identified fusions have included MED12::PRDM10, CITED2::PRDM10, PRDM10::ARHGAP32, and PRDM10::RAB30 {30570551; 34121219; 34969957}.

Macroscopic appearance

The tumours are circumscribed, firm, yellow to tan soft tissue masses with a variably gelatinous appearance {23887307; 27636918; 28937375; 34121219}.

Histopathology

The lesions grow in a relatively circumscribed (but at least partially infiltrative) fashion and are composed of cellular fascicles and sheets of spindled and pleomorphic cells with abundant eosinophilic cytoplasm, often having a granular or “glassy” appearance. Lipidized tumour cells are commonly present. The neoplastic cells show moderate to marked nuclear pleomorphism, often with bizarre, hyperchromatic nuclei containing prominent nucleoli and intranuclear cytoplasmic pseudoinclusions. Mitotic activity is very low, and necrosis is rarely seen {23887307; 27636918; 26946226; 30004071}. A mixed inflammatory cell infiltrate is often present. Rarely, myxoid stroma and metaplastic bone are reported in PRDM10-rearranged soft tissue tumours {34121219}.

Immunohistochemistry

These tumours invariably express CD34, and they are focally immunoreactive for keratins in close to 70% of cases (most often with the AE1/AE3 clones). SMARCB1 (INI1) expression is retained, and p53 expression shows a wildtype pattern. The Ki-67 (MIB1) proliferation index is very low (< 5%) {23887307; 27636918; 34121219; 34969957}. PRDM10 immunohistochemistry is consistently positive {30570551}. CADM3 (SynCam3) immunopositivity is reported in 95–100% of cases {34121219; 34969957}.

Differential diagnosis

The differential diagnosis of SCD34FT includes pleomorphic dermal sarcoma, dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans, plaque-like CD34-positive dermal fibroma, myxoinflammatory fibroblastic sarcoma, superficial solitary fibrous tumours, inflammatory myofibroblastoma, superficial myxofibrosarcoma, fibrous histiocytoma, and epithelioid sarcomas {33969057}. It may also include atypical fibrous histiocytoma, atypical fibroxanthoma, and undifferentiated pleomorphic sarcoma.

Cytology

Cytology shows cellular smears composed of large, pleomorphic cells with granular to glassy cytoplasm {27432164; 36151940}.

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Demonstration of PRDM10 rearrangement is useful in selected cases.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: superficial location; large eosinophilic cells with granular to glassy cytoplasm, marked nuclear pleomorphism, and a low mitotic rate; diffuse CD34 expression.

Desirable: CADM3 expression; keratin immunoreactivity; PRDM10 rearrangement.

Staging

Not clinically relevant

Prognosis and prediction

The prognosis for patients with this tumour is excellent, with only a single lymph node metastasis and no local recurrences reported in 45 cases with follow-up. All patients with this tumour have been reported to be alive and disease-free at the time of last follow-up {23887307; 27636918; 34121219}.
