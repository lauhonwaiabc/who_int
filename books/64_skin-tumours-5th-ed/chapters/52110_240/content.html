5.2.1.10 Plaque-like CD34-positive dermal fibroma

Definition

Plaque-like CD34-positive dermal fibroma is a benign tumour of fibroblastic origin characterized histologically by a band-like proliferation of bland CD34-positive spindle cells in the upper dermis, typically sparing the papillary dermis and adnexal structures.

ICD-O coding

8810/0 Plaque-like CD34-positive dermal fibroma

ICD-11 coding

2B53.Y & XH3665 Other specified fibroblastic or myofibroblastic tumour, primary site & Plaque-like CD34-positive dermal fibroma

Related terminology

Acceptable: medallion-like dermal dendrocyte hamartoma.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

The tumour has a broad site distribution but shows a predilection for the proximal limbs, upper trunk, and neck {15337977; 20061935}.

Clinical features

The tumour typically presents as a solitary, slowly growing, asymptomatic, well-demarcated, round to oval or medallion-shaped, erythematous to brown-yellow plaque, or (less often) as a nodular or protuberant growth {26645569}, measuring several centimetres in diameter {15337977; 20061935}. The surface is often wrinkled, depressed, and pliable {20061935}. Some proliferations can be pruritic and painful {24003290}.

Epidemiology

The tumour can be either a congenital or an acquired proliferation {15337977; 20061935; 30635930}. It has a wide age distribution but most commonly develops in children and young adults {15337977; 20061935; 28750558} and shows a female predominance {15337977; 20061935; 30635930}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Unknown

Macroscopic appearance

See Clinical features, above.

Histopathology

The tumour is distinguished by a band-like proliferation of variably cellular and fairly uniform fibroblasts in the upper and mid dermis, characteristically sparing the papillary dermis {15337977; 20061935}. Extension into the subcutaneous fatty tissue is rare {15337977; 20061935}. Some proliferations display a zonation pattern with perpendicular growth towards the surface and more storiform arrangements of tumour cells in the lower parts {20061935}. Concentric growth of tumour cells around adnexal structures, vascular channels, and nerves is often seen. The tumour cells are bland, with small nuclei, inconspicuous nucleoli, and rare mitoses {20061935}. Multiple dilated venules are present throughout the neoplasm {20061935}. Elastic fibres are often fragmented and reduced in number.

Immunohistochemistry

The tumour cells are diffusely positive for CD34 and fascin {15337977; 20061935; 30635930}. Staining for factor XIIIa is inconsistent.

Differential diagnosis

Plaque-like CD34 positive dermal fibroma must be distinguished from dermal dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans, dermatomyofibroma, and fibroblastic connective tissue naevus.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

None

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: a band-like proliferation of uniform fibroblasts in the upper and mid dermis; diffuse CD34 positivity; multiple dilated venules throughout the neoplasm.

Staging

None

Prognosis and prediction

Plaque-like CD34-positive dermal fibroma is a benign tumour {20061935}. Complete excision is curative {26645569; 28750558}. Recurrences have not been reported.
