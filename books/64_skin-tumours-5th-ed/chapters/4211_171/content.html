4.2.1.1 Mature plasmacytoid dendritic cell proliferation associated with myeloid neoplasm

Definition

Mature plasmacytoid dendritic cell proliferation (MPDCP) associated with myeloid neoplasm is a clonal proliferation of plasmacytoid dendritic cells (pDCs) with low-grade morphology identified in the context of a defined myeloid neoplasm.

ICD-O coding

Code as underlying neoplasm.

ICD-11 coding

Code as underlying neoplasm.

Related terminology

Acceptable: mature plasmacytoid dendritic cell proliferation.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Most cases have been described in the skin and bone marrow; very rarely, they occur in lymph nodes {1845925; 2301697}.

Clinical features

MPDCP in the skin manifests as rash, macules, papules, and (rarely) nodular lesions {33152058; 32871587; 21883371; 15105645; 22895265}.

Epidemiology

Patients are predominantly male, and the median age is 69–72 years {26743477; 32871587; 33054115}. MPDCP has been described in association with chronic myelomonocytic leukaemia (CMML) most frequently, as well as with acute myeloid leukaemia (AML), myelodysplastic neoplasms, and myeloproliferative neoplasms. MPDCP is present in 4.9% of cases of AML {32871587}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Clonal MPDCP cells accumulate in the bone marrow of patients with myeloproliferative CMML harbouring activating RAS pathway mutations {30894665}. In patients with AML, clonal MPDCP (pDC-AML) shares the same mutation landscape as CD34-positive blasts and frequently arises in association with RUNX1 mutations {32871587; 33054115}. It is unknown whether the pathogenetic mechanisms leading to MPDCP in association with CMML and with AML are the same. Nevertheless, while the concept of MPDCP currently pertains to the detection of mature pDCs in these diseases, it is recognized that in some AMLs there might be continuity between such cells and the dominant AML clone. In contrast to CMML, where pDCs in nodules are typically mature, in pDC-AML the pDCs show a spectrum from early immature pDCs (CD34+, CD303 dim/−) to mature pDCs (CD34−, CD303+). Additional studies are needed to further elucidate the spectrum of MPDCP and its relationship to blastic plasmacytoid dendritic cell neoplasm and/or associated myeloid neoplasms.

Macroscopic appearance

Not relevant

Histopathology

MPDCP consists of medium-sized cells with eccentrically located round or oval nuclei showing clumped chromatin. Cutaneous involvement is characterized by a dermal infiltrate that may be nodular, with or without a diffuse component, consisting of mature pDCs admixed with small lymphocytes and sometimes a few plasma cells and eosinophils. The epidermis is usually spared, and mitotic figures are absent {22895265; 21228369}.

Immunophenotype

The immunophenotype of MPDCP recapitulates that of mature pDCs (e.g. positive for CD123, TCF4, CD4, CD2AP, SPIB, CD303, CD304, and MX1) {24441662; 18218851; 12576313; 23165482; 21228369}. Aberrant loss of markers expressed on normal pDCs (e.g. CD303 and TCL1) may be detected, and CD56 is typically negative or weak/partial {21228369; 33054115; 32871587}. The Ki-67 proliferation index in MPDCP associated with CMML is usually low {22895265}.

Differential diagnosis

Reactive types of mature pDC proliferation often seen in conditions such as infections, systemic lupus erythematosus, Kikuchi–Fujimoto disease, and Castleman disease should be excluded.

Cytology

See above.

Diagnostic molecular pathology

No specific molecular or cytogenetic tests are required to make a diagnosis of MPDCP.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: accumulation of mature cells with plasmacytoid morphology and expression of CD123 and/or other pDC markers, in the context of a defined myeloid neoplasm.

Desirable: aberrant pDC immunophenotype; absent or low/partial expression of CD56.

Staging

Not applicable

Prognosis and prediction

The presence of MPDCP has been shown to correlate with adverse outcomes of the associated myeloid neoplasms in most studies {33152058; 32871587; 21883371}. Its recognition may have therapeutic implications {32871587}.
