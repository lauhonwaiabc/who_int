5.2.1.13 Sclerotic fibroma

Definition

Sclerotic fibroma is a benign cutaneous fibrous tumour with a prominent storiform pattern and clefting. The tumours can be sporadic or associated with Cowden syndrome.

ICD-O coding

8823/0 Sclerotic fibroma

ICD-11 coding

2F23.Y & XH5JG7 Other specified benign dermal fibrous or fibrohistiocytic neoplasms & Sclerotic fibroma

Related terminology

Acceptable: storiform collagenoma.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Sclerotic fibroma is most common in the head and neck region and in the arms {2029087}.

Clinical features

Tumours are solitary or multiple, typically slow-growing, longstanding, painless, white to skin-coloured papules or nodules {2029087; 2464630}.

Epidemiology

Sclerotic fibroma has a predilection for middle-aged adults. Cases associated with Cowden syndrome occur predominantly in men {2029087}, while sporadic cases occur predominantly in women {2464630}.

Etiology

Sclerotic fibroma (in particular when multiple) has been associated with Cowden syndrome {2029087; 1430474}.

Pathogenesis

Tumours that arise in association with Cowden syndrome have loss of PTEN (10q23.3), resulting in loss of its expression.

Macroscopic appearance

Sclerotic fibroma typically appears as small, flesh-coloured papules or nodules of 5–30 mm in size.

Histopathology

Sclerotic fibromas are polypoid, well-circumscribed, hypocellular lesions with abundant collagen with plywood-like clefting, admixed with banal small spindle cells arranged in a storiform pattern. Myxoid change can be seen {2029087; 2464630}. The tumours express CD34 {10698210}.

Differential diagnosis

Dermatofibroma is the main differential diagnosis. Sclerosing dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans may also mimic a sclerotic fibroma, especially on partial biopsies {28134725}.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Loss of PTEN by immunohistochemistry may suggest Cowden syndrome.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: hypocellular banal spindle cells arranged in a storiform pattern; abundant collagen with plywood-like clefting.

Desirable: for Cowden syndrome: loss of PTEN expression by immunohistochemistry.

Staging

Not relevant

Prognosis and prediction

Sclerotic fibromas are benign.
