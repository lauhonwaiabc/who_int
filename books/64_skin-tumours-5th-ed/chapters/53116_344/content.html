5.3.1.16 Acquired elastotic haemangioma

Definition

Acquired elastotic haemangioma (AEH) is a superficial band-like proliferation of small vascular channels arising in sun-exposed skin and associated with solar elastosis.

ICD-O coding

9120/0 Acquired elastotic haemangioma

ICD-11 coding

2F2Y Other specified benign cutaneous neoplasms

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

AEH has a predilection for the forearms and the lateral aspect of the neck.

Clinical features

The lesion manifests as an asymptomatic, small papule or plaque developing in sun-exposed skin {12196746; 31268926}. Multiple lesions are exceptional {35170476}.

Epidemiology

AEH is rare. It occurs in middle-aged and elderly adults, with a slight female predilection.

Etiology

It is likely that chronic sun damage plays a role in the development of lesions.

Pathogenesis

Unknown

Macroscopic appearance

See Clinical features, above.

Histopathology

AEH shows a band-like proliferation of capillaries, each surrounded by a layer of pericytes and oriented parallel to the epidermis in the upper reticular dermis. There is background solar elastosis.

Differential diagnosis

Microvenular haemangioma involves the mid to deep dermis and is poorly defined and not associated with solar elastosis. Poikilodermatous plaque-like haemangioma is also a superficial plaque-like proliferation but the lesions are larger, the stroma is oedematous, elastic fibres are missing, and there is no solar elastosis.

Cytology

Not relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: a superficial dermal plaque-like proliferation of capillaries in association with solar elastosis.

Staging

Not relevant

Prognosis and prediction

AEH is benign.
