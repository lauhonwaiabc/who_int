4.3.0.1 Reactive B-cell-rich lymphoid proliferations

Definition

Reactive B-cell–rich lymphoid proliferations (rB-LPs) are defined by a B-cell–predominant lymphocytic infiltrate. Histologically, and in some cases also clinically, rB-LPs can resemble cutaneous B-cell lymphomas.

ICD-O coding

None

ICD-11 coding

4B07 Acquired lymphocytosis

Related terminology

Acceptable: cutaneous lymphoid hyperplasia.

Not recommended: lymphadenosis cutis benigna (restricted to Borrelia-associated form); lymphocytoma cutis; benign lymphadenosis cutis; cutaneous B-cell pseudolymphoma.

Subtype(s)

Borrelia-associated reactive B-cell–rich lymphoid proliferation; miliarial-subtype reactive B-cell–rich lymphoid proliferation

Localization

rB-LP is most frequently located on the head and neck region, upper extremities, and trunk {34541667}. Borrelia-associated forms occur on the nipples (59%), earlobes (17%), and genital areas (9%) but also on other body sites (15%) {14984575; 9631994}.

The miliarial subtype of rB-LP is typically located in sunlight-exposed areas (face, décolleté, head, neck); in about one third of cases, multiple areas are affected {34541667; 22998442; 1681652; 34309911}.

Clinical features

rB-LP manifests with solitary red, brown, or bluish nodules. Sometimes a few nodules or papules are arranged in clusters. Disseminated papules or nodules rarely occur {7986687; 9631994; 28477891; 31237707; 14984575; 11806505}.

The miliarial subtype is characterized by multiple small semitranslucent papules, a few millimetres in diameter {34541667; 22998442; 1681652; 34309911}.

Epidemiology

The exact incidence is unknown. The Borrelia-associated form is found particularly in areas where Borrelia is endemic (e.g. North America, Europe, Asia) {14984575}.

Data about sex predominance are heterogeneous {3916177; 34541667; 14984575; 26313568}. rB-LP can occur at any age, including in childhood {34541667; 34221277; 11806505; 14984575; 26313568}.

Etiology

Many causes have been implicated, including drugs {29361381; 21501893}, antigen injection for allergy treatment {8647974; 18358197} or vaccination {18032948; 15793512}, tick bites {18496425}, arthropod bites {2197304}, herpes zoster {16148411}, hair dyes {17012922}, piercing {33150803}, tattoo pigment {19496897}, and Borrelia sp. {14984575} {26313568}. However, identification of a trigger is often not possible.

Pathogenesis

The pathogenesis is poorly understood. The above-mentioned causes can produce a direct or indirect proliferation of B cells.

Macroscopic appearance

See Clinical features, above.

Histopathology

A nodular or (in incisional biopsies) diffuse dermal lymphocytic infiltrate predominantly composed of B cells is present {11806505; 9631994; 28477891; 31237707}. The subcutis can also be affected {28477891; 31237707}. T lymphocytes, plasma cells, macrophages/histiocytes, and eosinophils are admixed in variable degrees {21501893; 9631994; 28477891; 31237707}. Plasmacytoid dendritic cells are found in 75% and closely resemble T cells and plasma cells {19718787; 19940749}. Reactive germinal centres are common {34221277; 21501893}. They can be well defined with clear mantle zones, or irregular with reduced to absent mantle zones {21501893}. A polarization of the reactive germinal centres is only found in a minority of cases {21501893}. Tingible-body macrophages are common (50–83%) {21501893}.

Borrelia-associated rB-LP often shows confluent large germinal centres with small or absent mantle zones, and a lack of polarization in 82% {14984575}. Tingible-body macrophages are a constant finding {14984575; 14726817}. Plasma cells (99%) and eosinophils (84%) are very common {14984575}.

The miliarial subtype shows a more superficial and less dense infiltrate, with a diffuse, nodular, or follicle-accentuated growth pattern.

Immunohistochemistry

The B cells express B-cell markers (e.g. CD20, CD79a, PAX5) {21501893; 28477891; 31237707}. Reactive germinal centres are positive for CD10 and BCL6 but negative for BCL2 {21501893; 28477891; 31237707; 14726817}. CD21 and CD23 highlight follicular dendritic cell networks, which are sharply restricted to the germinal centres {21501893; 28477891; 31237707}. The proliferative activity outlined by Ki-67 (MIB1) is high (> 90%) and relatively well limited to the reactive germinal centres {21501893; 11806505; 28477891; 31237707}.

The data on the discriminatory value of CD1a in rB-LP versus primary cutaneous marginal zone lymphoma are conflicting {25384543; 33326629}.

Cytology

Not relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Clonality studies reveal a polyclonal result in most cases for immunoglobulin heavy chain (IGH) and light chain (IGK) rearrangement {21501893; 10052401; 9208979}. Nevertheless, clonality can be detected in about 10–20% of rB-LP cases {10052401; 9208979; 16148405; 21501893; 18547309}. Immunoglobulin light chain κ and λ shows a ratio of < 5:1 in most cases {21501893; 11806505}, but rarely light chain restriction can also be found in rB-LP {21501893}. This highlights the importance of interpreting clonality results only in the context of the pathological and clinical findings. Next-generation sequencing appears to be a more sensitive assay {34343238}.

Detection of Borrelia sp. in Borrelia-associated rB-LP by PCR is useful, but in some cases the number of microorganisms is very low and thus Borrelia PCR can show false negative results.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: a dense dermal and/or subcutaneous B-cell–predominant infiltrate (the infiltrate is less dense in the miliarial subtype); exclusion of B-cell lymphoma; compatible with the clinical picture of rB-LP.

Desirable: reactive germinal centres; tingible-body macrophages; absence of monoclonality for IGH or light chain restriction; detection of Borrelia sp. in Borrelia-associated rB-LP (PCR and/or Borrelia serology).

Staging

Not relevant

Prognosis and prediction

rB-LP is a reactive and benign proliferation of B lymphocytes. The prognosis is excellent. Progression in a B-cell lymphoma has been rarely reported, but it remains unclear whether these cases may account for B-cell lymphomas from the beginning {27104170}.
