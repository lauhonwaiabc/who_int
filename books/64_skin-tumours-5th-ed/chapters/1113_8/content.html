1.1.1.3 PUVA keratosis

Definition

PUVA keratosis is a hyperkeratotic cutaneous lesion developing on sun-protected skin following prolonged psoralen and ultraviolet A (PUVA) treatment.

ICD-O coding

8070/0 PUVA keratosis

ICD-11 coding

EK90.Y & XH8MN8 Other discrete epidermal dysplasias & PUVA keratosis

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

The development of PUVA keratoses is confined to areas of previous PUVA therapy and typically affects non–sun-exposed skin of the trunk and thighs.

Clinical features

Patients with PUVA keratoses present with multiple warty, hyperkeratotic, and scaly papules with a broad base, measuring from a few millimetres to 10 mm {8445056}.

Epidemiology

The affected individuals are those receiving PUVA treatment for underlying skin conditions (e.g. psoriasis).

Etiology

This type of keratosis arises in association with prolonged medical use of PUVA treatment for skin conditions. The effect is dose-dependent {8445056}.

Pathogenesis

Frequent TP53 (57%) and HRAS (44%) mutations of the ultraviolet (UV) radiation signature type point to UV radiation exposure as the main etiological factor in these lesions {14962108}.

Macroscopic appearance

See Clinical features, above.

Histopathology

PUVA keratoses are well demarcated and characterized by acanthosis and overlying hyperkeratosis. A papillomatous growth and focal parakeratosis may be present. Mild keratinocyte dysplasia is noted in approximately 50% of biopsies. An additional finding is the presence of a superficial dermal chronic inflammatory infiltrate {8445056}.

Cytology

Not relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: a well-circumscribed squamoproliferative lesion with up to mild squamous dysplasia in the setting of prolonged PUVA treatment.

Staging

Not relevant

Prognosis and prediction

There is an increased risk of the development of non-melanoma skin cancer, particularly squamous cell carcinoma and less often basal cell carcinoma, years after prolonged PUVA treatment in patients with PUVA keratosis {7430462; 22264671; 8445056}.
