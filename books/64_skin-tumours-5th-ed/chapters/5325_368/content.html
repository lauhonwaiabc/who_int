5.3.2.5 Epithelioid haemangioendothelioma

Definition

Epithelioid haemangioendothelioma (EHE) is a malignant endothelial neoplasm composed of epithelioid cells within a myxohyaline stroma; almost all have a WWTR1::CAMTA1 fusion. A distinctive subtype composed of epithelioid cells with abundant cytoplasm and solid, nested, and/or vasoformative growth has a YAP1::TFE3 fusion.

ICD-O coding

9133/3 Epithelioid haemangioendothelioma

9133/3 Epithelioid haemangioendothelioma with WWTR1::CAMTA1 fusion

9133/3 Epithelioid haemangioendothelioma with YAP1::TFE3 fusion

ICD-11 coding

2B5Y & XH9GF8 Other specified malignant mesenchymal neoplasms & Epithelioid haemangioendothelioma

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

Epithelioid haemangioendothelioma with WWTR1::CAMTA1 fusion; epithelioid haemangioendothelioma with YAP1::TFE3 fusion

Localization

EHE rarely involves the skin, with no site predilection {9855348; 9130982}.

Clinical features

Most are nondescript nodules, sometimes with a thickened epidermis {25782038; 9855348; 25386269} or ulceration {24354555}.

Epidemiology

EHE most often arises in middle-aged adults, with a slight female predominance {25782038; 9130982; 9855348; 25386269}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

More than 90% of EHEs have t(1;3) with WWTR1::CAMTA1 fusion {21584898; 21885404}; a rare subtype has a YAP1::TFE3 fusion {23737213; 34381186}.

Macroscopic appearance

Not relevant

Histopathology

EHE is composed of relatively bland epithelioid cells, often with intracytoplasmic vacuoles, arranged in cords and nests, embedded in a variably myxohyaline stroma {7093931}. Vasoformative architecture is not seen in conventional EHE. The EHE subtype with YAP1::TFE3 fusion has tumour cells with abundant eosinophilic cytoplasm, arranged in a solid or nested growth pattern, often forming vascular spaces {23737213; 34381186}.

Immunohistochemically, EHE is positive for CD31, FLI1, ERG, and (less often) CD34. Low-molecular-weight cytokeratins are expressed in 20–30% of cases {11014572; 24986479; 34381186}. EHE with WWTR1::CAMTA1 shows diffuse strong nuclear expression of CAMTA1 {26414223; 24986479}. Tumours with YAP1::TFE3 fusion show diffuse nuclear TFE3 expression {23737213}; however, staining for TFE3 can also be seen in tumours with WWTR1::CAMTA1 fusion {26414223; 24986479}. Loss of expression of the C-terminus of YAP1 is more specific for YAP1::TFE3 fusions {34148063}.

Cytology

Not relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Identification of WWTR1::CAMTA1 and YAP1::TFE3 gene fusions is useful for subtyping.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: conventional EHE is composed of cords or nests of epithelioid cells with cytoplasmic vacuolization within a variably myxohyaline stroma; EHE with YAP1::TFE3 fusion shows solid or nested growth, often with well-formed vascular channels, lined by epithelioid cells with abundant eosinophilic cytoplasm.

Desirable (in selected cases): CAMTA1 expression by immunohistochemistry and/or WWTR1::CAMTA1 fusion; TFE3 overexpression by immunohistochemistry and/or TFE3 rearrangement (YAP1::TFE3 fusion).

Staging

Not relevant

Prognosis and prediction

Approximately 20% of EHEs of soft tissues metastasize and pursue an aggressive clinical course {7093931; 9130982; 18551749; 24986479}. Cutaneous EHE has an excellent prognosis {9130982; 9855348}. The clinical behaviour of cutaneous EHE with YAP1::TFE3 is unknown but may be less aggressive {34381186}.
