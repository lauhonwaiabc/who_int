5.2.1.16 Pleomorphic fibroma

Definition

Pleomorphic fibroma is a benign, often polypoid, fibroblastic dermal neoplasm characterized by bizarre pleomorphic cells.

ICD-O coding

8832/0 Pleomorphic fibroma

ICD-11 coding

2F23 & XH1BV2 Benign dermal fibrous or fibrohistiocytic neoplasms & Pleomorphic fibroma

2F23.Y & XH1BV2 Other specified benign dermal fibrous or fibrohistiocytic neoplasms & Pleomorphic fibroma

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Pleomorphic fibroma affects the extremities, trunk, and head and neck {23506010; 2916726; 33534206}.

Clinical features

The tumours are painless, single, slow-growing, fleshy polyps or papules {23506010; 2916726; 33534206}.

Epidemiology

This fibroma occurs in middle-aged adults {23506010; 2916726; 33534206}.

Etiology

Pleomorphic fibromas, when multiple and in young patients, may be associated with a genetic cancer syndrome involving TP53 (Li–Fraumeni syndrome) {32187703}.

Pathogenesis

These tumours have RB1 deletion and are negative for MDM2 amplification {28543636; 2916726}. Cases associated with Li–Fraumeni syndrome harbour TP53 germline mutations {32187703}.

Macroscopic appearance

Pleomorphic fibromas are small, dome-shaped to polypoid, and relatively well circumscribed.

Histopathology

These tumours are hypocellular and composed of dense, haphazardly arranged collagen bundles admixed with spindle cells and large pleomorphic cells that have hyperchromatic multilobulated nuclei with smudgy chromatin {23506010; 2916726}.

Immunohistochemistry

They are positive for CD34 and show loss of RB1 expression {28543636; 33534206}. There is aberrant p53 expression in Li–Fraumeni syndrome–associated cases {32187703}.

Differential diagnosis

The differential diagnosis includes superficial atypical lipomatous tumour, pleomorphic lipoma, and atypical fibroxanthoma.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: dermal dome-shaped to polypoid tumours; dense, haphazardly arranged collagen bundles; hypocellular spindle cells and large pleomorphic cells with hyperchromatic, smudgy chromatin.

Desirable: loss of RB1 expression by immunohistochemistry.

Staging

Not relevant

Prognosis and prediction

These are benign tumours, with rare recurrence {23506010; 2916726}.
