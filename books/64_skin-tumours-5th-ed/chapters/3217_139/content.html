3.2.1.7 Melanocytic matricoma

Definition

Melanocytic matricoma is a benign adnexal neoplasm recapitulating the bulb of the anagen hair follicle, composed of neoplastic aggregates of matrical and supramatrical cells and numerous intratumoural pigmented dendritic melanocytes.

ICD-O coding

8110/0 Melanocytic matricoma

ICD-11 coding

2F22 & XH7CM2 Benign neoplasms of epidermal appendages & Melanocytic matricoma

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

The head and neck are the most frequent sites for this tumour, followed by the trunk and the limbs.

Clinical features

Melanocytic matricoma appears as a solitary, pigmented, purple or brownish-black papule or nodule. The surface may be ulcerated or crusted, and the lesion may be painful {24641267}.

Epidemiology

Melanocytic matricoma is rare, with only about 25 cases reported {10446775}, predominantly in elderly men.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Unknown

Macroscopic appearance

See Clinical features, above.

Histopathology

Patients with melanocytic matricoma present with a well-circumscribed dermal nodule composed of solid aggregates of basophilic matrical and supramatrical cells with scant cytoplasm, round nuclei, prominent nucleoli, and frequent mitotic figures {30133753}. Small clusters of shadow cells may be seen at the centre of the aggregates. Neoplastic cell aggregates contain numerous scattered pigmented dendritic melanocytes and deposits of melanin. With rare exceptions {17598856}, there is no connection with the epidermis or hair follicles, although consumption of the epidermis overlying the tumour has been described {29095740}. Rare tumours contain atypical melanocytes {25678301} or epithelial cells {26730694}.

Immunohistochemistry

The matrical cells express PHLDA1 and nuclear β-catenin, but not BerEP4 {24673383; 17518772; 24641267}. Intratumoural melanocytes express S100, HMB45 antigen (PMEL [gp100]), melan-A, and SOX10 {25678301; 24641267}.

Differential diagnosis

The differential diagnosis includes pigmented pilomatricoma / pilomatrical carcinoma and basal cell carcinoma with matrical differentiation {35195926}.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: a well-circumscribed dermal nodule composed of solid aggregates of matrical cells with numerous scattered dendritic pigmented melanocytes and melanin deposits.

Staging

Not relevant

Prognosis and prediction

Melanocytic matricoma is benign. A few cases with more-prominent melanocytic and matrical atypia have been reported as malignant melanocytic matricoma {14631189; 21252635; 26730694; 30785652}, but their biological behaviour has not yet been well characterized.
