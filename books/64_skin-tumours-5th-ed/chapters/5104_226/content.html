5.1.0.4 Spindle cell / pleomorphic lipoma

Definition

Spindle cell / pleomorphic lipoma (SCL/PL) represents a morphological spectrum of the same benign adipocytic tumour. Spindle cell lipoma (SCL) is composed of varying proportions of mature adipocytes, bland spindle cells, and ropy collagen. Pleomorphic lipoma (PL) in addition contains pleomorphic and multinucleated floret-like giant cells.

ICD-O coding

8857/0 Spindle cell / pleomorphic lipoma

ICD-11 coding

2E80.0Z & XH30M7 Lipoma, unspecified & Pleomorphic lipoma

2E80.0Z & XH4AE98 Lipoma, unspecified & Spindle cell lipoma

Related terminology

Not recommended: dendritic fibromyxolipoma.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

In approximately 80% of cases, SCL/PLs arise within the subcutis of the posterior neck, back, and shoulders {1192370; 7459800}. The face, scalp, oral cavity, extremities, and trunk are involved in the remaining cases {7459800; 998247; 3623439; 25219904; 27686175}. Rarely, intramuscular and dermal-based lesions are also seen {27686175; 11190440}. Cases in women have a more varied distribution {27686175; 11190440; 28719462}. In women, the extremities are the most common site {28719462}.

Clinical features

Patients with SCL/PL mostly present with a painless, slowly growing, mobile subcutaneous mass. The symptoms are usually of several years’ duration. The majority of tumours measure < 50 mm. Multiple lesions and a familial predisposition have also been described {9422314; 12522375}.

Epidemiology

SCL/PL is most frequent among men aged between 45 and 60 years. Fewer than 10% of cases occur in women {1192370; 7459800; 998247; 3623439; 28719462}. SCL/PLs account for approximately 1.5% of adipocytic neoplasms {3623439}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Partial or whole chromosome 13 and/or 16 deletions are seen {9354891; 12494468; 8579124}. Breakpoints in 13q and/or deletions clustered around the region 13q14, where RB1 resides {12494468}, may be detected. Single-nucleotide polymorphism analyses suggest that other genes may be involved in SCL/PL tumorigenesis {34958506}. SCL may show deletions of chromosome 16 {7798294}. Two minimally deleted regions of interest in 13q14 have been described, the first of which involves RB1, RCBTB2, LPAR6, and CYSLTR2, and the second being a larger region encompassing MIR15A, MIR16-1, METTL1 (C12orf1), TRIM13, KPNA3, and DHRS12 {21563233}. Chromosome 12 polysomy within pleomorphic cells can result in focal MDM2 positivity by immunohistochemistry and may lead to misinterpretation as well-differentiated/dedifferentiated liposarcoma {18500263}. However, MDM2 amplification is absent {30857767}.

Macroscopic appearance

SCL/PL is grossly oval or discoid in shape. Depending on the proportion of adipocytes, spindle cells, and myxoid stroma, the cut surface may be yellow, grey-white, or myxoid. Extensively myxoid tumours can be gelatinous {1192370; 7459800; 3623439; 6884998; 30850230}.

Histopathology

SCL is well-circumscribed and usually encapsulated. Intramuscular and dermal tumours may have infiltrative margins. Mature adipocytes, bland spindle cells, and ropy collagen (thick refractile eosinophilic) bundles seen in variable combinations in a fibromyxoid stroma containing mast cells are characteristic features {1192370; 998247; 3623439}. The spindle cells are bland with uniform nuclei and are arranged randomly or in short fascicles in a “school-of-fish” pattern. Nuclear palisading, small cytoplasmic vacuoles, and focal lipoblasts may be seen {28546131}. Occasional mitoses can be seen, but necrosis is absent. A vascular network of small to medium-sized thick-walled vessels may be present. PL in addition shows pleomorphic spindle cells and multinucleated floret-like giant cells with a ring of peripheral, radially placed, hyperchromatic nuclei around a central deeply eosinophilic cytoplasm {7459800; 6884998}.

Morphological patterns include fat-poor SCL/PL, which is predominantly composed of spindle cells with little to no fat {17890910; 19542913}; myxoid SCL/PL, containing abundant myxoid stroma {25047505}; and pseudoangiomatous SCL/PL, which is composed of dilated, branching, vascular-like spaces and pseudopapillary projections {8063285; 17199739}. In addition, a plexiform architecture {8838333}, focal cartilaginous and osseous metaplasia {3623439}, and extramedullary haematopoiesis {11493343} have been reported.

Immunohistochemistry

The spindle and pleomorphic cells are positive for CD34, with loss of nuclear RB1 {9001985; 9042286; 22790852}. S100, SMA, desmin, and STAT6 are typically negative.

Differential diagnosis

SCL/PLs (particularly fat-poor patterns) must be distinguished from other CD34-positive superficial spindle cell lesions, including myxofibrosarcoma, dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans, solitary fibrous tumour, and neurofibroma {23600956}. PL may mimic atypical lipomatous tumour / well-differentiated liposarcoma and pleomorphic liposarcoma {22472959}. Cellular angiofibroma and mammary-type myofibroblastoma have features overlapping with those of SCL/PL and are related entities {11889594; 17497167; 11474286; 9669348}. Herniated orbital fat may contain multinucleated floret-like giant cells similar to those in PL and stromal cells with reactive atypia that may be confused with atypical lipomatous tumour {17255763}.

Cytology

Not relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Loss of the RB1 locus can be helpful in selected cases {21563233; 12494468}.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: bland spindle cells; ropy collagen with or without adipocytes, myxoid matrix; floret-like multinucleated giant cells in PL.

Staging

Not clinically relevant

Prognosis and prediction

SCL/PLs are benign tumours. Local recurrence is rare, even with incomplete resection {1192370; 7459800; 998247}.
