1.2.1.4 Infiltrating basal cell carcinoma

Definition

Infiltrating basal cell carcinoma (BCC) is characterized by elongated islands and strands of basaloid cells that are poorly circumscribed with spiking projections.

ICD-O coding

8092/3 Infiltrating basal cell carcinoma

ICD-11 coding

2C32.Y & XH5VK4 Other specified basal cell carcinoma of skin & Infiltrating basal cell carcinoma, NOS

Related terminology

Acceptable: infiltrative basal cell carcinoma.

Not recommended: basal cell carcinoma with aggressive growth pattern.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

See the family description (section Basal cell carcinomas).

Clinical features

These tumours are usually pink papules and plaques that are frequently ulcerated and may have a scar-like presentation. They are most frequent on the head and neck. On dermoscopy they are typified by thin serpentine vessels. Biopsy should include deep reticular dermis for most accurate diagnosis {16681663}.

Histopathology

Variably sized, sometimes jagged nests of basaloid tumour cells infiltrate the normal dermal collagen. The tumour has an irregular permeating pattern of invasion at the deep tumour edge {27311865}. Approximately one third of infiltrating BCCs are admixed with a nodular BCC component {16446711}. Perineural invasion is more frequent in this type of BCC {16112353}. There is often overlap with sclerosing/morphoeic and micronodular BCCs, which has led to a proposal to group these three tumour types into one group called “infiltrative BCC” {34931722}.

Differential diagnosis

Differentiation of infiltrative BCC from other types of BCC with infiltrative growth (sclerosing/morphoeic and micronodular BCCs) can be difficult. Infiltrating BCC can also be admixed with nodular and superficial BCCs, but the presence of infiltrative morphology defines the tumour type.

Cytology

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: variably sized, sometimes jagged nests of basaloid tumour cells infiltrating normal dermal collagen; an irregular permeating pattern of invasion at the deep tumour edge.

Desirable: perineural invasion may be present.

Staging

Staging is according to the eighth edition of the Union for International Cancer Control (UICC) / American Joint Committee on Cancer (AJCC) TNM system.

Prognosis and prediction

This type of BCC has a high risk of recurrence, requiring surgery with strict margin control.
