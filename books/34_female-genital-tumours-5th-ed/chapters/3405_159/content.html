3.4.0.5 Histiocytic nodule

Definition

Histiocytic nodule is an aggregate of benign histiocytes in the peritoneum.

ICD-O coding

None

ICD-11 coding

2B31.Y Other specified histiocytic or dendritic cell neoplasms

Related terminology

Acceptable: nodular histiocytic hyperplasia; nodular histiocytic aggregate

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Various locations in the peritoneum {27340746; 22271884; 15185256}

Clinical features

Histiocytic nodules are usually an incidental finding {27340746; 22271884; 15185256}.

Epidemiology

This lesion is seen in patients ranging in age from 6 to 70 years {27340746; 22271884}.

Etiology

Histiocytic nodules result from a nonspecific reaction to injury (including trauma), inflammation, or the presence of a tumour {27340746}.

Pathogenesis

Unknown

Macroscopic appearance

Histiocytic nodules present as small pinkish-yellow nodules measuring < 1 cm or as a solitary, filmy adhesion {22271884; 15185256}.

Histopathology

Histiocytic nodules are characterized by a compact and nodular aggregate of histiocytes. Mesothelial cells may be intermixed. The histiocytes have a moderate amount of amphophilic, eosinophilic, or basophilic cytoplasm and oval or kidney-shaped nuclei that can show grooves. Fibrinous material is sometimes seen. By immunohistochemistry, histiocytes are highlighted by CD68, CD16, CD163, lysozyme, CD4, and CD64 {27340746; 22271884}, but unlike mesothelial cells, they do not stain for calretinin and CK5/6.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: an aggregate of benign histiocytes in peritoneum.

Staging

Not clinically relevant

Prognosis and prediction

This is a benign lesion.
