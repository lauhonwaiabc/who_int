5.1.0.1 Leiomyoma of the broad ligament and other uterine ligaments

Definition

Leiomyomas at these sites are benign smooth muscle tumours arising from the uterine ligaments.

ICD-O coding

8890/0 Leiomyoma NOS

ICD-11 coding

2E86.1 Leiomyoma of other or unspecified sites

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Broad and other uterine ligaments

Clinical features

The presentation is similar to that of uterine leiomyoma (see section Uterine leiomyoma) and these tumours often coexist with uterine leiomyoma. Massive ovarian oedema and pseudo–Meigs syndrome have been described {24901402; 9704185}.

Epidemiology

The epidemiology is similar to that of uterine leiomyoma (see section Uterine leiomyoma).

Etiology

See section Uterine leiomyoma.

Pathogenesis

The tumours develop either from submesometrial smooth muscle layers, which run from the outer layer of the uterine myometrium to the pelvic floor, or from the round ligaments {17805565; 18661576; 21297441}.

Macroscopic appearance

Most are circumscribed, firm masses similar to their uterine counterparts. Some may exhibit prominent hydropic and degenerative cystic changes.

Histopathology

The spectrum of histological changes encountered is the same as seen in leiomyomas from the uterus, including histological patterns such as leiomyoma with bizarre nuclei, leiomyoma with epithelioid morphology, and leiomyoma with lipomatous differentiation {14714722; 24414189; 19716558}.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

See #10818Box #10818 in the section on section uterine leiomyoma.

Staging

Not clinically relevant

Prognosis and prediction

Most are benign. The uterine criteria for malignancy have been proposed for the classification of smooth muscle tumours at this site {30702465}.
