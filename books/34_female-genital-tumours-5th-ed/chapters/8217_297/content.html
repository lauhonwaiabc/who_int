8.2.1.7 Diffuse laminar endocervical hyperplasia

Definition

In diffuse laminar endocervical hyperplasia, there is laminar proliferation of endocervical glands.

ICD-O coding

None

ICD-11 coding

GA15.Y Other specified acquired abnormalities of cervix uteri

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Diffuse laminar endocervical hyperplasia typically involves the inner third of the cervical wall {1746679}.

Clinical features

Patients may present with mucoid watery discharge {7550997}.

Epidemiology

Patients present over a wide age range, with a mean patient age in the fourth decade of life {1746679; 7550997; 19820374; 9492737}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Unknown

Macroscopic appearance

Diffuse laminar endocervical hyperplasia is typically an incidental finding. An exophytic clinically visible lesion has been described {19820374}.

Histopathology

There is a well-demarcated diffuse proliferation of typically round to oval but often irregular, angulated, or star-shaped endocervical glands. A dense acute and chronic inflammatory infiltrate with associated periglandular oedema may occur.

Cytology

The findings on cervicovaginal smear are nonspecific {7550997}.

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: well-demarcated laminar proliferation of endocervical glands.

Staging

Not clinically relevant

Prognosis and prediction

Diffuse laminar endocervical hyperplasia is benign.
