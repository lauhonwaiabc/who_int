5.1.0.2 Adenomyoma of the broad ligament and other uterine ligaments

Definition

Adenomyoma is a mass formed by an admixture of endometrial-type glands (with or without endometrial stroma) and prominent smooth muscle.

ICD-O coding

8932/0 Adenomyoma NOS

ICD-11 coding

2F38 & XH4ZH4 Benign neoplasm of other or unspecified sites & Adenomyoma

Related terminology

Acceptable: extrauterine adenomyoma

Not recommended: uterus-like mass; endomyometriosis

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Broad and other uterine ligaments

Clinical features

Patients may present with abdominal pain, a pelvic mass, and/or vaginal bleeding {23532253; 16175086}. Rarely, the tumour is discovered many years after hysterectomy and bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy {16048397}.

Epidemiology

This is an uncommon tumour. The average patient age is about 50 years, but some cases occur in young women {22413810; 19047912; 23532253}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Extrauterine adenomyoma may be a result of failure of complete fusion of Müllerian ducts and is occasionally associated with other congenital urogenital abnormalities. It may also be due to coelomic metaplasia {22413810; 16048397}. Some lesions are considered smooth muscle metaplasia surrounding a focus of endometriosis {17592255; 3838644}.

Macroscopic appearance

Adenomyomas are circumscribed and well demarcated from the surrounding structures. The median size is 5.5 cm. Adenomyoma resembles leiomyoma with a whorled cut surface but also with scattered small cysts or foci of haemorrhage {22413810; 19047912; 10907166}.

Histopathology

The mass consists of endometrial-type glands and stroma scattered throughout a background of smooth muscle {12075412; 16048397}. Smooth muscle cells with bizarre nuclei have been described {19047912}. Cases in which the endometrial-type epithelium and stroma form a central cyst surrounded by smooth muscle may resemble a uterus (a so-called uterus-like mass).

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: endometrial-type glands and stroma within a mass of smooth muscle.

Staging

Not clinically relevant

Prognosis and prediction

Adenomyomas are benign, but rare cases of adenocarcinoma have been reported {1879815; 24942896}.
