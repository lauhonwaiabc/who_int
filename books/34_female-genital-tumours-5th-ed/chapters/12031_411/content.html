12.0.3.1 Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma

Definition

Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL) is a clonal neoplasm of large B lymphocytes.

ICD-O coding

9680/3 Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma NOS

ICD-11 coding

2A81 & XH3FE9 Diffuse large B-cell lymphomas & Mature B-cell lymphomas

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Female genital tract (FGT) DLBCL can present with involvement of one or (less often) both ovaries, cervix, corpus, vagina, or vulva (in decreasing order of frequency) {20509008; 28284518}. Ovarian lymphoma often involves the fallopian tubes as well; isolated tubal involvement is rare. DLBCL rarely involves the placenta, typically in cases with widespread disease {10761330; 26813900}.

Clinical features

Patients most commonly present with symptoms related to the presence of a mass or abnormal vaginal bleeding. Fewer patients have constitutional symptoms. Ovarian lymphomas may be associated with ascites {10658906; 10907172; 20509008}. Most patients presenting with ovarian DLBCL have extraovarian spread, involving lymph nodes, peritoneum, other genital organs, and/or distant sites {9062148; 25403088}. Most uterine DLBCLs are localized {16150291; 25075075}. DLBCL can involve the FGT secondarily, at relapse or in the setting of widespread disease {10658906; 10907172; 16224219}.

Epidemiology

Primary FGT DLBCL occurs in adults (over a broad age range) {10658906; 20509008; 28803429; 28284518}, with few cases in adolescents {16224219; 28803429; 29519565} and children {29519565}.

Etiology

Rare cases occur in immunocompromised patients {29086996; 26813900}, but otherwise the etiology is unknown.

Pathogenesis

IG genes are clonally rearranged {10658906}. Most cases show no rearrangement of MYC, BCL2, or BCL6 {25403088; 29519565}; 50% of cases have MYD88 p.Leu265Pro mutation and 25% have CD79B p.Tyr196 mutation (with or without MYD88 p.Leu265Pro) {28803429; 29519565}. Most MYD88-mutated cases have a non–germinal-centre B-cell (non-GCB) immunophenotype {28803429}. In the few cases of ovarian DLBCL studied, genomic analysis has revealed GCB-type and activated B-cell–type mutation profiles {29519565}.

Macroscopic appearance

Ovarian lymphomas usually form a mass (median size: 8–12 cm) {20509008; 25403088}. Endometrial lymphomas are polypoid or infiltrative. Cervical lymphomas produce bulky lesions, with circumferential enlargement or submucosal {16150291}, polypoid {15790461}, or fungating masses. Vaginal lymphomas cause ill-defined mural thickening or induration. Cervical and vaginal DLBCLs often invade adjacent structures {10800991}. Vulvar lymphomas form masses or indurated areas {10907172}.

Histopathology

Ovarian DLBCL invades diffusely, sparing the ovarian surface and (often) normal structures. Cervical and vaginal lymphomas show deep invasion, with a band of uninvolved tissue beneath the surface; the overlying epithelium is usually intact {1594503; 23645066}. DLBCL NOS is composed of centroblasts, immunoblasts, and/or multilobated cells. Neoplastic cells may grow in cords and nests associated with sclerosis, simulating carcinoma {10658906; 11706069; 15790461; 16306779}. DLBCL with spindle-shaped cells (called the spindle cell pattern {16400323} or the sarcomatoid pattern {12819398; 27167673}) mimics sarcoma.

Rare FGT intravascular large B-cell lymphomas {27803414; 29954821}, rare ovarian plasmablastic lymphoma {28228433}, ALK-positive DLBCL {23896707}, and teratoma with fibrin-associated DLBCL {25439346} are reported.

The neoplastic cells express pan–B-cell markers including CD20 {10658906; 11706069}, with a GCB immunophenotype (CD10+ or CD10−, BCL6+, IRF4 [MUM1]−) or a non-GCB immunophenotype (CD10−, BCL6+, IRF4 [MUM1]+) {11706069; 28803429}. Spindle cell DLBCLs have a GCB phenotype {16400323}. The Ki-67 proliferation index is usually > 50% {11706069; 25996637; 25403088}. EBV is typically absent {25403088}.

Cytology

Pap smears in cervical and vaginal DLBCLs may be abnormal {16150291} or negative, because surface epithelium often remains intact {1594503; 23645066}.

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Molecular studies for IG gene rearrangement or MYD88 mutation may be helpful in selected cases.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: a diffuse infiltrate of large atypical lymphoid cells; immunoreactivity for B-lineage markers.

Staging

The Lugano staging system is used.

Prognosis and prediction

The prognosis of ovarian DLBCL is similar to that of nodal lymphomas of comparable stage {9062148; 11706069}. Ovarian DLBCLs with bilateral involvement, widespread disease, high International Prognostic Index (IPI), and a large mass are associated with worse outcome {20509008}. Localized uterine and vaginal lymphomas have a favourable prognosis {10800991; 16150291; 25075075}. Cancer-directed surgery does not appear to improve prognosis {28284518}.
