12.0.1 Haematolymphoid proliferations and neoplasia: Introduction

This chapter discusses lymphomas and myeloid sarcoma involving the female genital tract (FGT), as well as the lymphoma-like lesion called florid reactive lymphoid hyperplasia. In cases in which the FGT is the only or the predominant site of involvement by a haematolymphoid neoplasm, that neoplasm can be considered to be primary in the FGT. Widespread disease at presentation suggests that FGT involvement is secondary.

Primary FGT lymphomas are uncommon; they account for approximately 1% of extranodal lymphomas {5007387}. FGT myeloid sarcoma is even less common than lymphoma. Haematolymphoid neoplasms occur in women and girls over a broad age range, mostly affecting middle-aged and older adults {28284518; 29984481}. The symptoms are nonspecific. Neoplasms usually present as mass lesions or with abnormal vaginal bleeding {29984481}. They are infrequently incidental findings {27340748; 11706069}. FGT lymphoma involves the ovaries most often, followed by the uterine cervix, uterine corpus, vagina, and vulva {28284518}. Primary fallopian tube lymphoma is very rare {15581994}, but ovarian lymphoma often shows tubal involvement {11073334}. FGT involvement is common in patients who develop lymphoma (especially Burkitt lymphoma) during pregnancy {23725710}.

Almost all primary FGT lymphomas are B-lineage non-Hodgkin lymphomas. Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma is most common, followed by follicular lymphoma, Burkitt lymphoma, and extranodal marginal zone lymphoma {28284518; 29984481; 11444509}. Other types, including B-lymphoblastic and T-lymphoblastic lymphomas {21193918; 16224219; 11706069; 20509008}, anaplastic large cell lymphoma and other peripheral T-cell lymphomas {11706069; 28284518}, and NK-cell lymphoma {11444509}, are rare. Almost all lymphomas in children and adolescents are diffuse, high-grade B-cell lymphomas involving the adnexa {16224219}. Lymphomas involving the FGT secondarily are of more-varied types (see #10294Table #10294) {16224219; 10658906; 10907172}.
