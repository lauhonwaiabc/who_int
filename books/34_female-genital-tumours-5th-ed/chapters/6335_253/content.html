6.3.3.5 Other mesenchymal tumours of the uterus

A variety of other benign and malignant mesenchymal tumours may also occur in the uterine corpus, including vascular tumours {28595254; 24257655; 9500227}, lipomatous tumours {21263242}, alveolar soft part sarcoma {28009610; 8598329}, solitary fibrous tumour (discussed in section Mesenchymal tumours of the lower genital tract) {29106748}, nerve sheath tumours – more commonly neurofibromas (associated with neurofibromatosis) {22487305}, NTRK sarcomas (discussed in section Mesenchymal tumours of the lower genital tract) {31107720; 30877273; 29553955}, and giant cell tumour {25851705}.
