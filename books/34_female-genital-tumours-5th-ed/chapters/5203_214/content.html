5.2.0.3 Ependymoma

Definition

Ependymoma is a neoplasm showing ependymal differentiation.

ICD-O coding

9391/3 Ependymoma NOS

ICD-11 coding

2F33.Y & XH1511 Benign neoplasm of other specified female genital organs & Ependymoma NOS

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

A few cases have been reported in the broad ligament {6703196; 16196528; 20403052}; ependymoma also occurs in the mesovarium, uterosacral ligament, and pelvis {26107559}.

Clinical features

Patients present with a pelvic mass and/or pain. Serum CA125 may be elevated {6703196; 16196528; 20403052}.

Epidemiology

The patient age range is 13–45 years (median: 24.5 years) {6703196; 16196528; 20403052}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Unknown

Macroscopic appearance

The tumours are cystic/multicystic or solid, and they range in size from 7 to 14 cm {6703196; 16196528; 20403052}.

Histopathology

The tumour cells are columnar/cuboidal, with eosinophilic cytoplasm. Atypia is mild/moderate and the mitotic count is usually low. There is a combination of architectural patterns – cystic, microcystic, cribriform, papillary, and solid. Ependymal pseudorosettes are seen. Spindle/flattened cells, cilia, ependymal rosettes/canals, cartilage, psammoma bodies, extracellular mucin, eosinophilic material, and myxoid areas may be present. This tumour is positive for GFAP, ER, PR, CK18, CK7, S100, and WT1 and variably positive for CD99 {18360284}.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: columnar/cuboidal tumour cells with eosinophilic cytoplasm; varying architectural pattern; ependymal pseudorosettes are seen.

Desirable: GFAP positivity.

Staging

Not clinically relevant

Prognosis and prediction

Multifocal involvement or recurrences several decades after diagnosis may occur {26107559}.
