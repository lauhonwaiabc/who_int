10.1.2.6 Squamous cell carcinoma NOS of the vulva

The percentages of HPV-associated and HPV-independent squamous cell carcinomas (SCCs) in the vulva are highly variable, depending on geographical area {23886586; 28590952; 23190170}; overall, approximately two thirds of vulvar SCCs arise via an HPV-independent pathway {23886586}. Although there is currently no difference in treatment between HPV-associated and HPV-independent tumours, it is recommended that the type of vulvar SCC (HPV-associated or HPV-independent) be documented in the pathology report. However, a morphological diagnosis of SCC NOS is an acceptable alternative where p16 immunohistochemistry or HPV testing is not available.
