5.1.0.4 Leiomyosarcoma of the broad ligament and other uterine ligaments

Definition

Leiomyosarcoma at this site is a malignant smooth muscle tumour arising from the uterine ligaments.

ICD-O coding

8890/3 Leiomyosarcoma NOS

ICD-11 coding

2B58.Y Leiomyosarcoma, other specified primary site

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Not applicable

Clinical features

Most patients are perimenopausal and present with pelvic pain.

Epidemiology

Although rare overall, leiomyosarcoma is the most common sarcoma of the broad ligament {27330834; 6837836; 14605822; 16343220}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Unknown

Macroscopic appearance

Not clinically relevant

Histopathology

The tumours resemble their counterparts in the uterus. Most are of spindle cell type and exhibit diffuse nuclear atypia, brisk mitotic activity, and tumour cell necrosis. Osteoclastic-like giant cells and rhabdoid cells have been reported {18777035; 20736768; 12893217}.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Classification using uterine-type smooth muscle criteria has been proposed for smooth muscle tumours of the broad ligament {30702465}.

Staging

Not clinically relevant

Prognosis and prediction

These are aggressive tumours; about half of patients die within 12 months of diagnosis. The presence of osteoclastic-like giant cells and rhabdoid cells is an adverse finding {20736768}.
