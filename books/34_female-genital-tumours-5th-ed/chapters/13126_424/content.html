13.1.2.6 Dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans

Definition

Dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans (DFSP) is a superficial, locally aggressive fibroblastic neoplasm, having a cellular storiform appearance and carrying a COL1A1-PDGFB or related fusion.

ICD-O coding

8832/1 Dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans NOS

ICD-11 coding

2B53.Y & XH4QZ8 Other specified fibroblastic or myofibroblastic tumour, primary site & Dermatofibrosarcoma NOS

Related terminology

Not recommended: Bednar tumour

Subtype(s)

Pigmented dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans; dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans, fibrosarcomatous

Localization

In descending order: the labia majora, mons pubis, and clitoral/paraclitoral area {28323651}

Clinical features

DFSP typically presents as a slow-growing mass, ranging in size from 0.5 to 15 cm (median: 4 cm) {20139758}.

Epidemiology

Patients range in age from 1 to 83 years (mean: 45.4 years) {10542946; 20139758; 28323651}.

Etiology

Most of these tumours occur sporadically.

Pathogenesis

Most DFSP lesions contain COL1A1-PDGFB fusions resulting from a t(17;22)(q21.3;q13.1) {29140881}.

Macroscopic appearance

DFSP usually has a fibrous appearance on cut section.

Histopathology

Conventional DFSP is a dermal-based infiltrative tumour of uniform, hyperchromatic spindled cells with a storiform growth pattern that infiltrates into the subcutis in a lace-like or honeycomb pattern. The mitotic count is low. Myxoid DFSP is composed of spindle to stellate cells uniformly distributed in myxoid stroma with a more apparent capillary vasculature {17890911; 17721193; 26355764}. Pigmented DFSP has scattered dendritic pigmented S100-positive cells. Myoid DFSP has myoid cells concentrically arranged around vessels {25943241}. Giant cell fibroblastoma has multinucleated cells, myxoid stroma, and pseudovascular spaces {21319518; 17415117}. Vulvar giant cell fibroblastoma often has a conventional DFSP component. Fibrosarcomatous DFSP shows an abrupt transition to a herringbone pattern of atypical spindled cells with increased mitotic activity {20139758}. CD34 is positive, but expression may be reduced or absent in fibrosarcomatous or myxoid DFSP {29140881; 26355764; 20139758}.

Lesions similar to DFSP with fibrosarcomatous change arising in the cervix have been described {30877273}. These show prominent storiform and herringbone architecture and no distinct vascular pattern. The spindle cells express CD34 and cyclin D1. There is little or no expression of S100, CD10, SMA, desmin, BCOR, ER, PR, or TRK {30877273; 21317712}.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

COL1A1-PDGFB fusions can be detected in selected cases.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: conventional DFSP: storiform pattern, spindled cells, and uniform CD34 positivity.

Staging

Union for International Cancer Control (UICC) staging is applicable for fibrosarcomatous DFSP.

Prognosis and prediction

There is a high rate of local recurrence. Fibrosarcomatous DFSP has metastatic potential.
