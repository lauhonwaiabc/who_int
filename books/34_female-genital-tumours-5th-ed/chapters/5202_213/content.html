5.2.0.2 Papillary cystadenoma

Definition

Papillary cystadenoma is a benign papillary cystic tumour of probable mesonephric origin, usually in patients with von Hippel–Lindau syndrome (VHL).

ICD-O coding

8450/0 Papillary cystadenoma NOS

ICD-11 coding

2F33.Y & XH0FM6 Benign neoplasm of other specified female genital organs & Papillary cystadenoma NOS

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Uterine ligaments

Clinical features

The tumour occurs in adults and may present with abdominal pain; some cases are incidental findings {22296276}.

Epidemiology

Most patients have VHL {22296276; 22317868; 3341511; 2386149}.

Etiology

The anatomical location and immunophenotype suggest a mesonephric origin {22296276}.

Pathogenesis

Allelic loss of VHL has been reported {11493991}.

Macroscopic appearance

The tumours are about 5 cm (range: < 1 to 8 cm), have a thick fibrotic capsule, and are traversed by fibrous bands creating a cystic-solid lobulated appearance {22296276; 9421096}.

Histopathology

There is complex tubulopapillary growth with varying broad-based and delicate branching and stromal hyalinization. Focal tubular or solid growth may be present. The cells are non-ciliated and cuboidal or low columnar, with minimal clear to eosinophilic cytoplasm. Prominent subepithelial blood vessels are common. The cells are positive for CK7, PAX8, PAX2, and CD10. WT1 and calretinin are variably positive; ER and PR are negative {22296276; 24441657; 22317868; 15767808}.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Demonstration of germline VHL mutation may be helpful.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: a cystic/lobulated mass; complex tubulopapillary growth of non-ciliated and cuboidal cells.

Desirable: VHL.

Staging

Not clinically relevant

Prognosis and prediction

These are benign, except for a single report of peritoneal metastases in a patient without VHL {22296276}.
