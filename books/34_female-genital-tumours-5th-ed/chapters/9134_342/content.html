9.1.3.4 Endocervicosis of the vagina

Definition

Endocervicosis of the vagina is benign endocervical-type mucinous glands that involve the deep vaginal stroma.

ICD-O coding

None

ICD-11 coding

GA14.Y Other specified acquired abnormalities of vagina

Related terminology

Not recommended: Müllerianosis

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Deep vaginal stroma

Clinical features

In one case, presentation was with a painful vaginal mass {12090597}.

Epidemiology

This is an extremely rare lesion, with only two reports in the literature {12090597; 11603227}.

Etiology

In one reported case, it was speculated that the endocervicosis was due to vaginal implantation of endocervical tissue at hysterectomy {12090597}.

Pathogenesis

Unknown

Macroscopic appearance

Not clinically relevant

Histopathology

Endocervicosis is composed of variably sized and shaped glands lined by bland mucinous endocervical-type epithelium and located deep within the vaginal stroma. Superficially located benign mucinous glands are designated as adenosis.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: benign endocervical-type mucinous glands deep within vaginal stroma.

Staging

Not clinically relevant

Prognosis and prediction

This is a benign lesion, although a single case of malignant transformation to adenocarcinoma has been reported {11603227}.
