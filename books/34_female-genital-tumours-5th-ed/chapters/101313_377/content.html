10.1.3.1.3 Chondroid syringoma

Definition

Chondroid syringoma is a benign vulvar tumour with an admixture of epithelial, myoepithelial, and stromal elements.

ICD-O coding

8940/0 Chondroid syringoma NOS

ICD-11 coding

GA13.Y & XH70N8 Other specified acquired abnormalities of vulva or perineum & Chondroid syringoma

Related terminology

Acceptable: pleomorphic adenoma; mixed tumour of the vulva

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

There is wide distribution on the vulva {16681787}. These tumours may arise from the Bartholin gland or other vestibular glands, or from anogenital mammary-like glands {22532914}.

Clinical features

Patients are usually postmenopausal and typically present with a slow-growing, solitary, painless nodule {22532914}.

Epidemiology

These are uncommon vulvar neoplasms.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

The PLAG1 gene on chromosome 8q12 is consistently rearranged in pleomorphic adenomas of the salivary glands {9525740} but has not been investigated in vulvar tumours.

Macroscopic appearance

Chondroid syringoma is typically a well-defined round to ovoid mass < 3 cm in diameter, with a solid or solid and cystic, white, glistening cut surface.

Histopathology

Histology typically shows a dermal-based, well-circumscribed lesion with epithelial, myoepithelial, and stromal elements. The epithelial cells form tubules or trabeculae with associated myoepithelial cells, which may have a clear cell, plasmacytoid, or spindled appearance. The stromal element is typically fibromyxoid but can exhibit adipocytic, chondroid, or osseous differentiation.

Cytology

The cytology is similar to that of the corresponding salivary gland tumours {26619021}.

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: admixture of epithelial, myoepithelial, and stromal elements.

Staging

Not clinically relevant

Prognosis and prediction

The tumour may recur if incompletely excised. Rare cases of adenocarcinoma arising in chondroid syringoma of the vulva have been reported {14501823}.
