5.1.0.5 Other mesenchymal and mixed tumours of the broad ligament and other uterine ligaments

Rare tumours in the broad and round ligaments include those proposed to be of Wolffian duct or supernumerary ovary origins, such as fibroma with heterotopic bone formation {8349152}, fibroma with minor sex cord elements {28351194}, and fibrosarcoma {25760903}. Miscellaneous benign mesenchymal tumours include lipoleiomyoma {16810060; 20379451; 19716558}, solitary fibrous tumour {17493768; 30278559}, and angiomyofibroblastoma {28800578}. Sarcomas include endometrial stromal sarcoma {843483; 17235569}, alveolar soft part sarcoma {8598329}, malignant fibrous histiocytoma {8088614}, myxoid liposarcoma {11576262}, Ewing sarcoma {16175085}, malignant perivascular epithelioid cell tumour (PEComa) {15361222}, and low-grade fibromyxoid sarcoma {27185563}. Several cases of rhabdomyosarcoma, including a case with an underlying DICER1 mutation, have also been reported {4016676; 7472949; 25006297; 28524158}.
