9.1.3.2 Mullerian papilloma of the vagina

Definition

Müllerian papilloma is a benign papillary Müllerian-type epithelial tumour.

ICD-O coding

None

ICD-11 coding

GA14.0 Polyp of vagina

Related terminology

Acceptable: Müllerian papilloma of infancy

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Müllerian papillomas may be mucosal-based or intramural, with a variable location in the vagina {26948653}.

Clinical features

Most cases occur in prepubertal girls, with a few cases reported in adolescents or young adults (age range: 2–24 years) {26948653; 25330952; 21073126; 11737317; 9891250}. Most patients present with vaginal bleeding {26948653}.

Epidemiology

These are extremely rare vaginal neoplasms.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

The pathogenesis is uncertain, but this neoplasm is believed to arise from Müllerian epithelium {9891250; 6170416}.

Macroscopic appearance

Mucosal-based papillomas are friable polypoid lesions {26948653; 25330952; 19616458}, whereas intramural examples are solid-cystic mural masses {9891250; 6170416}.

Histopathology

Histology shows an exophytic or intramural lesion with a papillary architecture and slender fibrovascular cores lined by cuboidal or columnar epithelium with little or no cytological atypia {9891250; 11737317; 19616458}. Nuclei are uniform, with fine chromatin. Mitoses are generally absent or sparse. Squamous metaplasia may be present.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: papillary architecture; cuboidal or columnar epithelium with no cytological atypia.

Staging

Not clinically relevant

Prognosis and prediction

The clinical outcome is favourable {26948653; 19616458}, but recurrence, including multiple recurrences, has been reported {30406704; 7979569; 10193336}. Malignant transformation to clear cell carcinoma after multiple recurrences was reported in one case {12713630}.
