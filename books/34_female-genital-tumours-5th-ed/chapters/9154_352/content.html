9.1.5.4 Adenoid basal carcinoma of the vagina

Adenoid basal carcinoma is extremely rare in the vagina; it shows histology identical to that of the more common cervical lesion (see section Adenoid basal carcinoma of the uterine cervix).

ICD-O coding

8098/3 Adenoid basal carcinoma
