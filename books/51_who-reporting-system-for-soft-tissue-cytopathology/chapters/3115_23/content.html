3.1.1.5 Sample reports: Insufficient/Inadequate/Non-diagnostic

The sample reports provided here are based on the standardized cytopathology report in the section Diagnostic categories and report structure section of the first chapter. See that section for a discussion on the structure and components.

These are sample reports that include the components required in one possible format; the final report format depends on local practice and local laboratory information system requirements. For brevity, demographics and ROSE are not presented here.

For consistency, the term “Insufficient” has been selected for these three sample reports. Cytopathologists and their institutions should decide whether their laboratory will use “Insufficient”, “Inadequate”, or “Non-diagnostic” for this category, and use it consistently and not in an ad hoc way. See the introduction at the beginning of this chapter for a discussion regarding the use of the term “Non-diagnostic”.

Each institution should select a term for the main heading of the diagnostic summary, such as “Diagnostic summary” (as used in the examples below), “Diagnosis”, or “Final diagnosis”, and use it consistently.

Sample report 1

Specimen type:

CT-guided FNAB

Clinical and imaging information:

14-year-old male, lump over his left sinonasal region. MRI: 90 mm, well-defined, lobulated right sphenopalatine region. PET-CT: avid soft tissue mass. ? juvenile nasal angiofibroma.

 

Diagnostic summary:

Site: Left sinonasal

Category: Insufficient

Diagnosis: Insufficient material for diagnostic assessment

 

Material received:

Five air-dried slides and two alcohol-fixed slides. 3 mL bloodstained fluid prepared for cell block.

 

Microscopic description:

Blood with very occasional spindle, possible endothelial, cells present. Only blood is seen in the cell block.

 

Comment: Repeat FNAB is recommended.

Sample report 2

Specimen type:

FNAB

Clinical and imaging information:

35-year-old female, 56 mm soft tissue mass right side of neck. MRI shows infiltrative 55 mm lesion within sternocleidomastoid muscle, isointense on T1-weighted and heterogeneously hyperintense on T2-weighted images.

 

Diagnostic summary:

Site: Right sternocleidomastoid muscle

Category: Insufficient

Diagnosis: Insufficient material for diagnostic assessment

 

Material received:

Four air-dried slides, two alcohol-fixed slides. 1 mL bloody fluid for cell block.

 

Microscopic description:

One paucicellular air-dried Giemsa-stained smear shows a minute stromal fragment consisting of several spindle cells with a few single spindle cells in a bloody background. The other smears show blood. The cell block shows blood only.

 

Comment: There is insufficient material for diagnostic assessment. Correlation with clinical and imaging findings is required, and repeat study is recommended.

Sample report 3

Specimen type:

CT-guided FNAB

Clinical and imaging information:

55-year-old male with a slow-growing left thigh mass. MRI shows a 50 × 45 × 60 mm heterogeneously enhancing solid mass in the vastus medialis muscle, suspicious for sarcoma.

 

Diagnostic summary:

Site: Left vastus medialis

Category: Insufficient

Diagnosis: Blood with fibrin clot (see comment below)

 

Microscopic description:

Blood is present with a fibrin clot.

Material received:

Four air-dried slides and a container that includes a blood clot

Comment: There is insufficient material for diagnostic assessment. Correlation with imaging findings and repeat FNAB with ROSE and/or CNB is recommended.
