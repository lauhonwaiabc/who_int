5.1.1.5 Sample reports: Atypical

The sample reports provided here are based on the standardized cytopathology report in the section Diagnostic categories and report structure section of the first chapter. See that section for a discussion on the structure and components.

These are sample reports that include the components required in one possible format; the final report format depends on local practice and local laboratory information system requirements. For brevity, demographics and ROSE are not presented here.

Each institution should select a term for the main heading of the diagnostic summary, such as “Diagnostic summary” (as used in the examples below), “Diagnosis”, or “Final diagnosis”, and use it consistently.

Sample report 1

Specimen type:

CT-guided FNAB

Clinical and imaging information:

62-year-old male. Past history of right upper arm soft tissue sarcoma, followed by radiation therapy. MRI reveals 10 mm subcutaneous lesion adjacent scar.

 

Diagnostic summary:

Site: Right upper anterior arm

Category: Atypical

Diagnosis: Spindle cell lesion with focal atypia (see comment below)

 

Material received:

Four air-dried Giemsa-stained smears from ROSE, two alcohol-fixed smears. 4 mL pink fluid.

 

Microscopic description:

One mildly cellular smear shows a few stromal fragments containing plump single cells along with scattered single spindle cells showing nuclear enlargement and focal atypia, against a background of inflammatory cells and red blood cells. There are no mitotic figures, markedly pleomorphic cells, or necrosis in the background. The other smears show blood only. Cell block contains limited material showing inflammatory cells and fibroblastic/myofibroblastic cells positive for SMA and negative for MDM2.

 

Comment: The features are considered atypical and raise a differential diagnosis of reactive changes following surgery and possibly residual sarcoma. CNB or excision biopsy is recommended.

 

(Editor’s note: In this case, there is limited material categorized as “Atypical”.)

Sample report 2

Specimen type:

FNAB

Clinical and imaging information:

28-year-old male. 30 mm soft tissue lump right forearm slow-growing over 4 years. MRI shows well-defined, lobulated 27 mm soft tissue lesion.

 

Diagnostic summary:

Site: FNAB right forearm

Category: Atypical

Diagnosis: Spindle cell tumour with atypia

 

Material received:

Two air-dried slides and one alcohol-fixed slide. 3 mL bloodstained fluid, which yielded no pellet for cell block.

 

Microscopic description:

Mildly cellular smears show syncytial fragments of spindle cells with occasional poorly formed nuclear palisades. Spindle cells have elongated, occasionally angulated nuclei and are embedded in a fibrillary metachromatic stroma. Occasional larger cells show nuclear enlargement, hyperchromasia, and atypia. There is no diagnostic material in the cell block.

 

Comment: A spindle cell neoplasm is present, showing atypia, and the differential diagnosis includes ancient schwannoma. Repeat FNAB (to obtain material for ancillary studies) or CNB is recommended.

Sample report 3

Specimen type:

FNAB

Clinical and imaging information:

25-year-old female with a slowly growing hard 60 mm mass in left flank. Ultrasound shows an irregular ill-defined mass attached to muscle and in deep subcutis.

 

Diagnostic summary:

Site: Left flank

Category: Atypical

Diagnosis: Spindle cell neoplasm

 

Material received:

Six air-dried slides. 3 mL pink fluid.

 

Microscopic description:

One moderately cellular smear shows spindle cells in tissue fragments with pale cytoplasm separated by magenta stroma, with elongate tapering nuclei. Occasional similar spindle cells are present in the background. There are no mitotic figures or necrosis. The other slides show only blood. The cell block has no diagnostic material.

 

Comment: The features are atypical and raise broad differential diagnoses, including fibromatosis. Correlation with clinical findings and further imaging is recommended. FNAB (to obtain material for immunocytochemistry) or CNB is recommended.

Sample report 4

Specimen type:

FNAB

Clinical and imaging information:

75-year-old male. Recently noticed posterior lower neck soft mass.

 

Diagnostic summary:

Site: Posterior left-sided neck mass

Category: Atypical

Diagnosis: Adipocytic neoplasm

 

Material received:

Two air-dried smears and one alcohol-fixed smear. 2 mL pink fluid. Cell block prepared.

 

Microscopic description:

There is scant material, consisting of tissue fragments of fat cells separated by spindle cells and occasional atypical cells with hyperchromatic large nuclei. Occasional adipocytes are in the background. Nucleoli are inconspicuous. No definite lipoblasts are seen. There is no necrosis. There is no diagnostic material in the cell block.

 

Comment: The features are considered atypical. The features favour an adipocytic neoplasm rather than subcutaneous fat.
