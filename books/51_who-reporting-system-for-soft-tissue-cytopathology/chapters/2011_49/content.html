2.0.1.1 Introduction

Evaluation of soft tissue masses by FNAB, with or without concurrent CNB, has been increasingly used and has gained greater acceptance in recent years. This has occurred in concert with the rapid development in molecular genetic diagnostics, the massive expansion of sensitive and specific ICC biomarkers, and the maturation of various image-guided needle biopsy techniques, as well as the increasing use of a multidisciplinary approach in patient care. Enhanced by ROSE, which enables immediate triage for flow cytometry, microbiological culture, ICC, and cytogenetic and molecular studies, FNAB is a valuable, cost-effective, and time-sensitive diagnostic modality for soft tissue lesions and neoplasms, especially those with clinical or imaging features suspicious for malignancy. This chapter focuses on the practical approaches of FNAB sampling techniques, ROSE management, effective triage of biopsy material, and various cytopathological preparation methods, as well as the judicious use of ancillary studies {32298511; 32303388}.
