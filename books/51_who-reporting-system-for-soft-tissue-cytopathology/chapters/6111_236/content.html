6.1.1.1 Introduction

The category of “Soft tissue neoplasm of uncertain malignant potential” (“STNUMP”) represents a diverse group of neoplasms whose biological behaviour cannot be predicted on an FNAB or CNB, or in some cases on histopathology, even if a definitive diagnosis of a specific tumour type can be made by routine cytomorphological analysis with or without ancillary testing. This section includes several neoplasms that are classified by the WHO classification of soft tissue and bone tumours as having an “intermediate (locally aggressive)” biological risk or “intermediate (rarely metastasizing)” biological risk, as well as tumour types whose criteria for risk stratification or malignancy are based on histopathological parameters that can be applied only on resection specimens.
