7.1.1.5 Sample reports: Suspicious for malignancy

The sample reports provided here are based on the standardized cytopathology report in the section Diagnostic categories and report structure section of the first chapter. See that section for a discussion on the structure and components.

These are sample reports that include the components required in one possible format; the final report format depends on local practice and local laboratory information system requirements. For brevity, demographics and ROSE are not presented here.

Each institution should select a term for the main heading of the diagnostic summary, such as “Diagnostic summary” (as used in the examples below), “Diagnosis”, or “Final diagnosis”, and use it consistently.

Sample report 1

Specimen type:

FNAB

Clinical and imaging information:

74-year-old male, with rapidly increasing, firm, painful 40 mm lump in left frontoparietal scalp region of 3 months’ duration. Ultrasound shows lesion involving dermis and possibly bone.

 

Diagnostic summary:

Site: Left frontoparietal scalp

Category: Suspicious for malignancy

Diagnosis: Suspicious for sarcoma

 

Material received:

Three air-dried smears and one alcohol-fixed smear.

 

Microscopic description:

Predominantly haemorrhagic smears show scant material consisting of occasional minute tissue fragments of markedly pleomorphic spindle cells, along with a few scattered large polygonal cells with atypical nuclei. One tissue fragment shows a possible vasoformative architecture. Occasional haemosiderin-laden macrophages are also seen.

 

Comment: The features are suspicious for a sarcoma such as angiosarcoma. Correlation with clinical and imaging findings is required. Repeat FNAB (to obtain material for ancillary studies) or CNB is recommended.

Sample report 2

Specimen type:

FNAB

Clinical and imaging information:

55-year-old female with a rapidly growing 70 mm soft tissue mass fixed to skin in left thigh, 4 months’ duration. MRI reveals a well-defined 75 mm heterogeneous soft tissue mass involving the deep fascia and the surrounding muscles.

 

Diagnostic summary:

Site: Left mid-thigh vastus medialis muscle

Category: Suspicious for malignancy

Diagnosis: Suspicious for sarcoma with necrosis (see supplementary report below)

 

Material received:

Six air-dried slides, three alcohol-fixed slides. 3 mL pink fluid processed for cell block.

 

Microscopic description:

These paucicellular smears show several minute tissue fragments consisting of spindle and pleomorphic cells, as well as several similar single intact cells, in a background of limited necrosis. Several minute tissue fragments of fat are also present.

 

Comment: The features are suspicious for sarcoma with necrosis, suggesting a high-grade neoplasm. Immunocytochemistry will be performed on the limited material in the cell block.

 

Supplementary report 1:

There are a very limited number of tumours cells in the cell block, positive for MDM2 and CDK4 and negative for pankeratin AE1/AE3. The features are “Suspicious for malignancy” and raise the possibility of a dedifferentiated liposarcoma. Correlation with the clinical and imaging findings is recommended along with CNB or incisional biopsy.

 

(Editor’s note: There is limited material, and the cytopathologist has issued a “Suspicious for malignancy” categorization report and alerted the clinical team that ICC will be attempted on the cell block. The cell block ICC, again on limited material, supports the diagnosis of sarcoma and raises the possibility of a dedifferentiated liposarcoma.)

Sample report 3

Specimen type:

FNAB

Clinical and imaging information:

70-year-old male with a 45 mm firm mass over his left deltoid. MRI shows a T2-hyperintense, septate multilobulated mass involving the subcutaneous region.

 

Diagnostic summary:

Site: Left deltoid

Category: Suspicious for malignancy

Diagnosis: Suspicious for sarcoma, including possibility of myxofibrosarcoma

 

Material received:

Five air-dried Giemsa-stained smears from ROSE and two alcohol-fixed smears. 3 mL bloodstained fluid.

 

Microscopic description:

There is scant material, consisting of small tissue fragments of predominantly myxoid material containing scattered spindle and stellate cells (including occasional cells showing nuclear atypia), foamy macrophages (which may represent pseudolipoblasts), and lymphocytes. There are thin blood vessels within these myxoid fragments. There is no diagnostic material in the cell block.

 

Comment: The features are “Suspicious for malignancy” and raise the possibility of low-grade myxofibrosarcoma, although cellular myxoma cannot be completely excluded. Correlation with clinical and imaging findings and CNB or incisional biopsy is recommended.
