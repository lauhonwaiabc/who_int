4.5.2.5B Ganglion cyst

Definition

Ganglion cyst is a degenerative cystic lesion most often occurring in the periarticular fibrous tissue or synovium.

Clinical features and imaging

Ganglion cysts present as a palpable lesion, with or without associated pain, and occur most commonly in the hands, wrists, knees, ankles, and feet. They have a predilection for females and often occur between the ages of 20 and 40 years, but they have also been described in paediatric patients and in the elderly. On MRI, ganglion cysts appear as homogeneous, lobulated masses originating from tendons, ligaments, tendon sheaths, menisci, or joint capsules. Ultrasound shows a hypoechoic mass showing continuity with the adjacent joint capsule or adjacent soft tissue, without significant vascularity on colour Doppler {29310457; 17901135; 18716098}.

Histopathology

Ganglion cysts are characteristically unilocular or multilocular cysts or clefts within degenerative capsular or tendinous tissue. No cyst-lining cells are present. The cystic mucoid material is usually not apparent in postfixation, embedded, and sectioned slides {12115304; 26902711}.

Key diagnostic cytopathological features

Extremely low cellularity Abundant granular myxoid background material with variable consistency Isolated bland histiocytes with oval nuclei and granular to foamy cytoplasm Lack of any vasculature

Reference(s): {8989538; 12115304; 33168472; 35878421}

Discussion and differential diagnosis

FNAB produces viscous mucoid material, which is often difficult to expel from the FNAB needle.

Given their periarticular location, ganglion cysts closely resemble synovial cysts and juxta-articular myxoma on imaging. Synovial cysts represent true herniation of the synovial membrane through the joint capsule; they more commonly occur in association with cranial and lumbar spine joints, and smears may contain synovial cells. In addition to abundant myxoid material on smears, juxta-articular myxomas show scattered loose tissue fragments of bland spindle cells with long cytoplasmic processes, which are absent in ganglion cysts {26902711; 33168472}.

Ancillary testing

Synovial cells can be highlighted by D2-40 ICC; they are also positive for CD163, as are histiocytes.
