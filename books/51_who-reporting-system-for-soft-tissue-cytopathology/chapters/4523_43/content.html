4.5.2.3 Spindle cell / pleomorphic lipoma

Definition

Spindle cell and pleomorphic lipomas (SCL/PLs) represent the morphological spectrum of a single neoplasm sharing distinct clinical, ICC, molecular, and prognostic features. Spindle cell lipoma (SCL) is a benign adipocytic tumour composed of variable amounts of mature adipocytes, bland spindle cells, and ropy collagen. Pleomorphic lipoma (PL) in addition contains pleomorphic and multinucleated floret-like giant cells.

Clinical features and imaging

SCL/PLs account for 1.5% of adipocytic neoplasms {3623439}. Most occur in the subcutaneous fat in the neck, shoulder, and upper back of middle-aged to elderly patients, particularly men {1192370; 7459800; 998247; 3623439}. Less common locations include the face, scalp, orbit, oral cavity, tongue, limbs, and trunk {1192370; 998247; 3623439; 25219904; 27686175}. Rarely, dermal and intramuscular locations have been reported {27686175; 11190440}.

SCL/PLs present as a well-circumscribed, subcutaneous mass with variable imaging appearances depending on the proportions of adipose and non-adipose components {24554024}. Occasionally, the presence of minimal or no intralesional macroscopic fat may make them difficult to recognize on imaging {31932047; 24240205; 28823067}.

Histopathology

SCL/PLs are composed of variable amounts of bland spindle cells, mature adipose tissue, and ropy collagen bundles in a fibrotic or fibromyxoid stroma {1192370; 998247; 3623439}. PLs contain a component of pleomorphic spindle cells and multinucleated floret-like giant cells. Mast cells are common findings, and lipoblasts may be seen in a subset of cases, while mitoses are very rare and necrosis absent. Histopathological subtypes include fat-poor {17890910; 19542913}, myxoid {25047505; 31934089}, (pseudo)angiomatous {8063285; 31934089}, and plexiform {8838333} SCL/PLs. Focal cartilaginous and/or osseous metaplasia {3623439} and/or extramedullary haematopoiesis {11493343} can be present.

Discussion and differential diagnosis

The differential diagnosis of SCL/PL includes a variety of spindle cell, pleomorphic, and myxoid soft tissue tumours, because of the variable proportions of spindle cells, fat, and collagen; the presence of a myxoid background and floret cells; and the occasional presence of pleomorphic epithelioid or polygonal or spindle cells and lipoblasts. Atypical spindle cell / pleomorphic lipomatous tumours (ASCLT/APLTs) share most histopathological features with SCL and PL {32068239; 27879515; 20228779; 24659226; 29358476}, and they appear indistinguishable in cytopathological smears, but increased cytopathological atypia of nuclei on smears should prompt consideration of ASCLT/APLT. The differentiation of SCL/PL from cytopathological mimics, including ASCLT/APLT, myxoid liposarcoma, low-grade myxofibrosarcoma, dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans, schwannoma, and fat-forming solitary fibrous tumour, generally requires the use of ancillary studies and access to clinical and imaging findings. Smears from the myxoid variant of SCL/PL lack the thick-walled vessels of myxofibrosarcoma. The presence of hyaline collagen fibres, in variable amounts from case to case, is an important clue for the diagnosis of SCL/PL {11748578}. The clinical presentation of dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans differs from that of SCL/PL, and there is a lack of collagen fibres in smears.

Ancillary testing

By ICC, the spindle cells are positive for CD34 and often BCL2. Demonstration of loss of the RB1 locus and/or RB1 protein expression can support the diagnosis in selected cases {12494468; 21563233; 31934089; 33802620}. Detection of the DDIT3 rearrangement in myxoid liposarcoma and presence of cytopathological atypia in ASCLT/APLT help distinguish them from SCL/PL. Positivity for CD34 and negativity for S100 helps to exclude schwannoma, and negativity for STAT6 helps to exclude fat-forming solitary fibrous tumour.
