6.1.1.4 Risk of malignancy and diagnostic management recommendations

Currently, there is insufficient published data associated with the cytopathological category “STNUMP” to establish a ROM. However, estimates of ROM for each of the diverse tumour types have been developed based on histopathology, and accurate FNAB diagnosis with appropriate ancillary testing can establish appropriate management for the patients. A recent single-institution retrospective study of 2214 soft tissue cases showed a ROM of 51.43% {39212584}.

Subsequent management after FNAB biopsy requires clinical, imaging, and cytopathological correlation. If material for ancillary testing is not available, repeat FNAB with cell block and/or CNB (if available and if the lesion is accessible) is recommended. In most cases, complete resection is usually performed as the definitive treatment, and for some specific tumours, an appropriate risk stratification scheme can be applied to the histopathological specimen to determine the risk of metastasis.
