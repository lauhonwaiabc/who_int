6.1.1.2 Definition

Neoplasms in the “Soft tissue neoplasm of uncertain malignant potential” (“STNUMP”) category are mesenchymal neoplasms that cannot be unequivocally diagnosed as benign or malignant on cytopathological analysis and ancillary testing, including tumours with intermediate biological potential and tumours requiring the application of histopathological criteria to determine whether they are benign or malignant.
