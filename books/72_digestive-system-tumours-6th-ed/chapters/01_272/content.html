0.1 Website beta version

The beta version of this volume has been uploaded to allow early access for the WHO Classification of Tumours Online community. The content may change during further editing and the review of proofs of the book, but such changes are usually minor, and the taxonomy itself, as agreed by the editorial board, is unlikely to change.

Please note that we use WHO English, which is midway between US and UK usage, so you may see some linguistic differences. Please ignore these.

Comments can be added using the feedback tool within the individual sections. We hope you find it helpful to have this information early and enjoy using the website.
