13.1.6.8 NUT-rearranged sarcoma

Definition

NUT-rearranged sarcomas are aggressive neoplasms characterized by NUT gene rearrangement.

ICD-O coding

93690/3 NUT-rearranged sarcoma

ICD-11 coding

2B5Y & XA03U9 Other specified malignant mesenchymal neoplasms of colon

2B5Y & XA7MC7 Other specified malignant mesenchymal neoplasms of stomach

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Roughly 43 NUT-rearranged sarcomas have been reported, including 16 involving the GI tract {29356724;33714983;30723300;32873880;35791777;35441831;38946048;39921028}. These 16 tumours have involved the oesophagus (2), stomach (4), small intestine (1), and colon (9).

Clinical features

Almost all cases have been reported in women (15/16; 94%). Patients present with signs and symptoms of obstruction, blood loss or weight loss, or with a mass discovered on imaging studies. Metastatic disease at presentation to the liver, peritoneum, and lungs is frequently present {29356724;33714983;30723300;32873880;35791777;35441831;38946048;39921028}.

Epidemiology

NUT-rearranged sarcomas are extremely rare, with < 45 reported cases and no description of epidemiology {34898574}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Reported fusion events have included MXD4::NUTM1 (8 cases), MXI1::NUTM1 (6 cases), MXD1::NUTM1 (1 case), MXI1::NUTM2 (1 case); another was NUTM1-rearranged by FISH {29356724;33714983;30723300;32873880;35791777;35441831;38946048;39921028}. The NUTM1 gene, located on chromosome 15q, is normally expressed in the testis and participates in spermatogenesis by altering histone acetylation {30257209}. The MAX basic helix-loop-helix leucine zipper (bHLHZip) protein is a critical component of a transcription factor network governing various aspects of cell behaviour (e.g. cell proliferation and tumorigenesis). MAX serves as a cofactor for DNA binding by the MYC family of oncoproteins and a group of putative MYC antagonists that include max-binding protein MNT (MNT), max dimerization protein 1 (MXD1), max dimerization protein 3 (MXD3), max dimerization protein 4 (MXD4), max-interacting protein 1 (MXI1), and MAX gene–associated protein (MGA) {16908182}.

Macroscopic appearance

The tumours present as nonspecific, fleshy masses of variable size centred in the muscularis propria of the affected organ; ulceration and necrosis are common.

Histopathology

NUT-rearranged sarcomas are morphologically similar, typically consisting of an admixture of monomorphic spindled cells, uniform epithelioid to rhabdoid cells, and occasionally more primitive round cells in a hyalinized background, sometimes with the formation of amianthoid-like fibres. Mitotic figures are easily identified, and necrosis is often present. MDX4-rearranged tumours often have a larger percentage of cells with epithelioid or rhabdoid features. Expression of keratins is present in a minority of cases; all are NUT protein–positive, although typically less intensely than in NUT carcinomas. Despite rhabdoid morphology, SMARCB1 and SMARCA4 expression is retained. A wide variety of other tested markers have been consistently negative {29356724;33714983;30723300;32873880;35791777;35441831;38946048;39921028}.

Cytology

There is a single report of a morphologically and immunohistochemically typical MXI1::NUTM1-fused sarcoma identified in a pleural fluid cytological specimen {35441831}.

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Next-generation sequencing or FISH identifies NUTM1/NUTM2 rearrangements.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: monomorphic spindled, epithelioid, and rhabdoid cells in a partially hyalinized background; evidence of NUTM1 or NUTM2 rearrangement.

Staging

Not applicable

Prognosis and prediction

The prognosis for patients with NUT-rearranged gastrointestinal sarcomas is poor. Of 13 patients with available clinical follow-up, often of very short duration, 2 died of disease, 7 were alive with metastatic disease, and 4 were disease-free. Overall, metastases occurred in 11 patients, typically to the lungs, liver, lymph nodes, and peritoneal cavity {29356724;33714983;30723300;32873880;35791777;35441831;38946048;39921028}.
