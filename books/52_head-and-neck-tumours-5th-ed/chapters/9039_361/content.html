9.0.3.9 Large B-cell lymphoma with IRF4 rearrangement

Definition

Large B-cell lymphoma with IRF4 rearrangement (LBCL IRF4+) is a de novo large B-cell lymphoma with a follicular, follicular and diffuse, or purely diffuse growth pattern characterized by strong expression of IRF4 (MUM1) and, in most cases, associated with an IRF4 rearrangement to an IG gene partner (IGH, IGK, IGL).

ICD-O coding

9698/3 Large B-cell lymphoma with IRF4 rearrangement

ICD-11 coding

2A81.Y & XH6SU8 Other specified diffuse large B-cell lymphomas & Large B-cell lymphoma with IRF4 rearrangement

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Most cases of LBCL IRF4+ arise in the Waldeyer ring or in head and neck lymph nodes. Less commonly, the neoplasm can present in the gastrointestinal tract.

Clinical features

Most patients present with localized lymph node or tonsillar enlargement (stage I/II) in the head and neck region. Systemic symptoms (B symptoms) are present in a minority of patients {32239695}.

Epidemiology

LBCL IRF4+ is rare. Although there is a wide age range (4–79 years), the median age at presentation is in the second decade of life, and LBCL IRF4+ is more frequently encountered in patients aged < 18 years. There is an almost equal sex distribution {21487109; 32239695}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

A translocation involving IRF4, usually partnered with IGH, is the hallmark of LBCL IRF4+. LBCL IRF4+ has a gene expression profile distinct from diffuse large B-cell lymphoma of activated B-cell or germinal-centre B-cell types. There is overexpression of both IRF4 and BCL6, as well as of NF-κB target genes, indicating disruption of BCL6 / IRF4 / Blimp1 (PRDM1) pathways {21487109; 31738823}. Recurrent mutations of BCL6 (usually in the IRF4 binding site), IRF4 mutations, and mutations of NF-κB–related genes (e.g. CARD11, CD79B, and MYD88) are found {21487109; 31738823}. Copy-number alterations include loss of 17p13/TP53 and gains of chromosomes 7 and 11q {23073988}.

Macroscopic appearance

These lymphomas produce a firm, fleshy, homogeneous mass.

Histopathology

LBCL IRF4+ may present with a follicular, follicular and diffuse, or entirely diffuse growth pattern; most cases are at least partially diffuse. When follicular, the neoplastic follicles are large, expansile, devoid of mantle zones, and closely packed. The neoplastic lymphoid cells have a centroblastic morphology or, less frequently, are of intermediate size with blastoid morphology. Mitotic figures are often infrequent, and tingible-body macrophages are usually lacking {32452165}.

The neoplastic cells are mature B cells, positive for CD20, CD79a, and PAX5. BCL6 is positive, IRF4 (MUM1) is strongly expressed, and staining for Blimp1 (PRDM1) is negative {21487109}. Positivity for CD10 and BCL2 is variable but usually present in about half of all cases {21487109}. Approximately 30% of LBCL IRF4+ cases are positive for CD5 {31738823}. These neoplasms usually have a high proliferation index.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Conventional cytogenetic analysis shows t(6;14)(p25.3;q32) (IGH::IRF4) or possibly IGK (chromosome 2p12) or IGL (chromosome 22q11) partners. IRF4 rearrangements can be identified by FISH in most (but not all) cases. In 10% of cases, the rearrangement is cryptic and undetectable using commercially available break-apart FISH probes {31738823}. Rearrangements involving BCL6 may be identified, but not BCL2 or MYC rearrangements {21487109; 31738823}.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: a large B-cell lymphoma with a mature B-cell immunophenotype and strong, uniform expression of both BCL6 and IRF4 (MUM1); IRF4 rearrangement; if IRF4 rearrangement analysis cannot be performed, the appropriate clinical setting in combination with a typical immunophenotype allows the diagnosis, but as “not molecularly confirmed”.

Desirable: absence of BCL2 and MYC gene rearrangements.

Staging

Lymphomas are staged according to the Lugano classification, which has been adopted by the eighth edition of the Union for International Cancer Control (UICC) TNM classification {25113753}.

Prognosis and prediction

Patients are treated with combination immunochemotherapy plus or minus local radiotherapy, with a favourable outcome {21487109; 32239695}. A small number of reported patients had tonsillar LBCL IRF4+ with purely follicular disease, and tonsillectomy alone resulted in complete and sustained remission. Currently, however, there is insufficient evidence for the de-escalation of treatment for patients with this disease {23108024}.
