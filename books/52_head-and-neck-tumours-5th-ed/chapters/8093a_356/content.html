8.0.9.3A Myxoma

Definition

Myxoma is a benign myofibroblastic proliferation within an extensively myxoid ground substance without other epithelial or mesenchymal (nerve sheath) elements and not affecting gnathic bone.

ICD-O coding

8840/0 Myxoma, NOS

ICD-11 coding

XH6Q84 Myxoma, NOS

Related terminology

Not recommended: peripheral odontogenic myxoma.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Myxomas affect the periauricular soft tissues (external auditory canal), sinonasal tract, and larynx {8116795; 22306211; 8334970; 21938690; 25606843; 26915162; 29988276; 31032655}. They appear to arise in the soft tissue but may expand to involve adjacent bone. When multiple myxomas are present (skin of the external ear canal, eyelids), Carney complex may be present {7640202; 8116795; 16267413}. See also section Odontogenic myxoma .

Clinical features

Myxoma manifests as a slow-growing, site-specific swelling {8116795; 22306211}. Symptoms may be related to local tumour extension into adjacent structures (middle ear, palate, orbit, and/or nasal cavity) {8116795; 8334970; 16267413; 19221255; 22306211; 26915162; 32066550; 32525735; 33829881}. Endoscopic and radiographic images demonstrate a mass, often well delimited. Sinonasal tract tumours may show an intraosseous unilocular/multilocular radiolucency {6302014; 21168253; 21938690; 24429059; 15364495; 15701983; 27398121; 30484069; 31032655}.

Epidemiology

Myxomas are rare, reported most commonly in children, although all ages may be affected, with an equal sex distribution {8116795; 8334970; 6302014; 15364495; 21168253; 21880053; 22306211; 24429059; 21624835; 15701983; 26915162; 30484069; 32525735; 33829881}.

Etiology

Carney complex, if present, is commonly associated with germline inactivating mutations in PRKAR1A and shows myxomas, spotty pigmentation, endocrine overactivity, and schwannoma {7640202; 8116795; 16267413}.

Pathogenesis

The pathogenetic relationship between odontogenic myxoma and sinonasal myxoma is unclear {15364495; 21624835; 21168253; 30484069}.

Macroscopic appearance

Tumours show a grey-white, glistening surface with a gelatinous to firm consistency. They can be as large as 50 mm but are usually < 20 mm {8116795}.

Histopathology

Myxomas are circumscribed but unencapsulated and are composed of a loose hypocellular proliferation of spindled, stellate, or round cells within a myxoid stroma containing arciform thin blood vessels and scattered fine collagen bands. Pleomorphism, mitotic figures, and tumour necrosis are absent {8116795; 8334970; 6302014}. Epithelial basaloid buds from the surface epithelium may be seen in lesions of the external auditory canal in patients with Carney complex. Odontogenic epithelium is absent in sinonasal tract tumours {24429059; 30484069}. Immunohistochemistry is non-contributory, with variable SMA and nuclear β-catenin reactivity {30484069; 21624835}.

In the differential diagnosis, angiomyxoma is a unique entity (CD34-reactive), and odontogenic myxomas are separated on the basis of anatomical tumour centring. Aural polyp and myxoid laryngeal polyp may have some histological overlap, but they also have inflammatory components.

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Not clinically relevant

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: a circumscribed myxoid mass with scattered stellate and spindled cells in a capillary-rich myxoid matrix; not arising in gnathic bones.

Desirable: nuclear β-catenin staining (in selected cases).

Staging

Not clinically relevant

Prognosis and prediction

These lesions are benign, but recurrences are common {7640202; 8116795; 30484069; 21624835; 24429059; 6302014}. External auditory canal tumours should prompt screening for Carney complex {7640202; 16267413; 22306211}.
