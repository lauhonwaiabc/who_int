10.0.0.1 Melanocytic tumours: Introduction

Mucosal melanomas arising in the sinonasal region, oral cavity, and larynx are combined and presented in this chapter on melanocytic tumours, rather than being separated by anatomical site as they were in the previous edition.

Rare compared with cutaneous melanoma, head and neck mucosal melanomas are biologically distinct from both cutaneous and uveal melanomas. Mucosal melanomas of the head and neck harbour distinct genetic alterations characterized by numerous chromosomal structural aberrations and copy-number alterations. The common MAPK activating mutations seen in cutaneous melanoma (BRAF, NRAS, NF1) are much less common, with only 28% of head and neck mucosal melanomas showing any of these mutations {28325255; 31655118}. Knowledge of the mutation landscape of head and neck mucosal melanoma is evolving, and potential therapeutic targets are being identified.
