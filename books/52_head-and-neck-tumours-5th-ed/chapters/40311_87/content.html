4.0.3.11 Epithelial-myoepithelial carcinoma

Definition

Epithelial-myoepithelial carcinoma (EMC) is a salivary gland malignancy characterized by biphasic tubular structures, usually composed of tightly coupled inner ductal and prominent outer myoepithelial cells.

ICD-O coding

8562/3 Epithelial-myoepithelial carcinoma

ICD-11 coding

2B67.Y & XH9JP2 Other specified malignant neoplasms of parotid gland & Epithelial-myoepithelial carcinoma

2B68.2 & XH9JP2 Other specified malignant neoplasms of submandibular or sublingual glands & Epithelial-myoepithelial carcinoma

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

The majority of EMCs develop in the parotid gland; the remainder affect the other major or minor salivary glands, sinonasal tract, and bronchus {8322454; 17197918}.

Clinical features

EMC usually presents as a slow-growing, painless mass. Lymphadenopathy is rare.

Imaging

Features are nonspecific, but lesions are frequently slow-growing and fairly well defined on CT and MRI, with intermediate signal on T1- and T2-weighted MRI and with heterogeneous enhancement. Biopsy is required to differentiate them from other low-grade salivary gland masses {17939852}.

Epidemiology

EMC is an uncommon neoplasm, accounting for 1% of all salivary gland tumours {17197918}. It mostly occurs in the sixth and seventh decades of life, with a slight female predilection.

Etiology

A small subset arise in association with intercalated duct hyperplasia {19542868; 36480093}. EMC may also arise in a pre-existing pleomorphic adenoma {17197918; 29135520; 30994537}.

Pathogenesis

A significant number of EMCs harbour HRAS mutations, most commonly at codon 61 {24277618; 26053092; 29135520; 30994537}.

Macroscopic appearance

The mean tumour size is 30 mm {33526226}. The cut surface shows a white-tan, nodular, firm mass. Cystic changes may be noted {17197918; 33526226}.

Histopathology

EMC generally exhibits multinodular invasive growth. The histological hallmark is a biphasic arrangement of inner (luminal) eosinophilic ductal epithelial cells and outer (abluminal), often clear myoepithelial cells {4340536; 6950345}. The myoepithelial component varies from single-layered to multilayered or even solid. Nuclear atypia is mild to moderate. There are several histological patterns and morphologies, including cribriform, basaloid, sebaceous, apocrine/oncocytic, and double-clear appearances, as well as squamous differentiation {17197918; 18425042; 19492889; 22427262; 23821213; 29135517}. High-grade transformation rarely occurs {10335939; 20679885; 23821210; 33825717}.

Immunohistochemistry

The luminal cells are positive for CK7, whereas abluminal cells are usually positive for SMA, calponin, and p63/p40 {17197918; 33526226}. Diffuse and membranous/cytoplasmic RAS p.Q61R expression is observed in 65% of EMCs {33481388}.

Differential diagnosis

The differential diagnosis includes other salivary gland tumours with biphasic and/or clear cell morphology, such as adenoid cystic carcinoma, basal cell adenocarcinoma, pleomorphic adenoma, myoepithelial carcinoma, and clear cell carcinoma {33526226}.

Cytology

Aspirates classically show 3D clusters with a dual epithelial and myoepithelial cell population {9839128; 12619100}.

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Assessment of HRAS mutations is useful for discriminating EMC from mimics {30994537; 33526226}.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: a salivary gland malignancy, usually with multinodular invasive growth; at least partly with a dual arrangement of inner ductal cells and outer prominent, usually clear, myoepithelial cells.

Staging

Staging follows the eighth edition of the Union for International Cancer Control (UICC) / American Joint Committee on Cancer (AJCC) TNM staging system.

Prognosis and prediction

EMC has a favourable prognosis {26195572; 30127663; 33526226} but occasionally recurs locally; lymph node and distant metastases are rare. High-grade transformation is clearly a poor prognosticator {10335939; 20679885; 23821210; 33825717}. Other adverse features include size, necrosis, angiolymphatic invasion, and margin status {8322454; 17197918; 20679885}.
