1.1.0.3 Nasal chondromesenchymal hamartoma

Definition

Nasal chondromesenchymal hamartoma (NCMH) is a benign mesenchymal sinonasal tract tumour composed of cysts lined by respiratory epithelium associated with nodules of cartilage and a variably myxoid spindle cell stroma.

ICD-O coding

None

ICD-11 coding

2E90.6 & XH2P15 Benign neoplasm of nasopharynx & Mesenchymal hamartoma

Related terminology

Not recommended: chondroid hamartoma; nasal hamartoma; congenital mesenchymoma.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Frequently bilateral (~25%), tumours involve the paranasal sinuses (mostly ethmoid) and nasal cavity, sometimes with skull base extension {9537469; 20822816; 26138824}.

Clinical features

Symptoms are nonspecific, with a mass identified {9537469; 16253025; 20822816; 25118636; 26138824}. Patients may have a history of other neoplasms associated with DICER1 pathogenic variants {20822816; 24761742; 25118636; 25356068}.

Imaging

Imaging studies show a complex, solid and cystic, heterogeneous soft tissue mass, frequent calcification, and associated bone erosion {9537469; 25117604; 31620849; 31871336}.

Epidemiology

Fewer than 1% of patients with pleuropulmonary blastoma have NCMH {20822816}. There is a wide age range, but most patients are aged < 1 year (mean: 10 years). There is a male predominance.

Etiology

NCMH is associated with DICER1 pathogenic variants {20822816; 22356457}.

Pathogenesis

Like other neoplasms in DICER1 syndrome, NCMH shows biallelic, loss-of-function, and missense RNase IIIb mutations in DICER1. This combination of mutations is expected to alter expression of microRNAs important in regulating the cell cycle and limiting differentiation {20822816; 24909177; 29343557; 31620849}.

Macroscopic appearance

Polypoid, fleshy soft tissue masses

Histopathology

NCMH is composed of epithelial cysts lined by respiratory epithelium associated with nodules of immature to mature cartilage with a rim of spindled cells set in a loose, myxoid to spindled cell stroma. Focal osteoclast-like giant cells may be seen in the stroma near erythrocyte-filled spaces, resembling aneurysmal bone cyst {9537469; 15129948; 16253025; 20822816}. A fibro-osseous proliferation with ossicles or trabeculae of immature (woven) bone can be seen {11231491}. Concurrent sinonasal papilloma and chronic rhinosinusitis may be seen.

Immunohistochemistry

The cartilage is highlighted with S100 or SOX9 immunohistochemistry, and SMA highlights stromal myofibroblasts {16253025; 11231491}.

Differential diagnosis

The differential diagnosis includes sinonasal tract hamartomas, biphenotypic sinonasal sarcoma, and embryonal rhabdomyosarcoma (which can be seen in DICER1 syndrome).

Cytology

Not clinically relevant

Diagnostic molecular pathology

When NCMH is diagnosed, germline DICER1 genetic testing is suggested {29343557}.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: cysts lined by respiratory epithelium associated with nodules of immature to mature cartilage within a bland spindled cell stroma.

Desirable: biallelic DICER1 pathogenic variants in selected cases.

Staging

Not applicable

Prognosis and prediction

Local recurrences may occur with incomplete excision (~25%) {9537469; 26138824; 28385583}. Rare malignant transformation has been reported {23348387}.
