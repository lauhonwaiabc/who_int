3.1.1.2 Mature plasmacytoid dendritic cell proliferation associated with myeloid neoplasm

Definition

Mature plasmacytoid dendritic cell proliferation (MPDCP) associated with myeloid neoplasm is a clonal proliferation of plasmacytoid dendritic cells (pDCs) with low-grade morphology identified in the context of a defined myeloid neoplasm.

ICD-O coding

9727/1 Mature plasmacytoid dendritic cell proliferation associated with myeloid neoplasm

ICD-11 coding

2B31.Y Other specified histiocytic or dendritic cell neoplasms

Related terminology

Acceptable: mature plasmacytoid dendritic cell proliferation.

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Most cases have been described in the skin and bone marrow; very rarely, it occurs in lymph nodes {1845925; 2301697}.

Clinical features

MPDCP in the skin manifests as rash, macules, papules, and (rarely) nodular lesions {33152058; 32871587; 21883371; 15105645; 22895265}.

Epidemiology

Patients are predominantly male, with a median age of 69–72 years {26743477; 32871587; 33054115}. MPDCP has been described in association with chronic myelomonocytic leukaemia (CMML) most frequently, as well as with acute myeloid leukaemia (AML), myelodysplastic neoplasms, and myeloproliferative neoplasms. MPDCP is reported in 4.9% of cases of AML {32871587}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

Clonal MPDCP cells accumulate in the bone marrow of patients with myeloproliferative CMML harbouring activating RAS pathway mutations {30894665}.

Clonal MPDCP in patients with AML has the same mutation landscape as CD34+ blasts, and it frequently arises in association with RUNX1 mutations {32871587; 33054115}.

It is unknown whether the pathogenetic mechanisms leading to MPDCP in association with CMML and with AML are the same. Nevertheless, while the concept of MPDCP currently pertains to the detection of mature pDCs in these diseases, it is recognized that in some cases of AML there might be a continuous phenotypical spectrum between the mature pDCs present and the dominant AML clone. In contrast to CMML, in which pDCs in nodules are typically mature, clonal MPDCP in patients with AML shows a spectrum of pDC maturity, from early immature (CD34+, CD303 dim/−) to mature (CD34–, CD303+) pDCs. Additional studies are needed to further elucidate the spectrum of MPDCP and its relationship to blastic plasmacytoid dendritic cell neoplasm and/or associated myeloid neoplasms.

Macroscopic appearance

Not relevant

Histopathology

MPDCP consists of medium-sized cells with an eccentrically located round or oval nucleus showing clumped chromatin. These cells have a moderate amount of amphophilic cytoplasm, which may occasionally contain vacuoles {32871587}. Mitotic figures are typically absent. In bone marrow tissue samples, MPDCP can be nodular and may be admixed with or rimmed by small lymphocytes {30894665; 32871587}. In patients with AML, MPDCP may be interstitial in distribution, with limited formation of cellular aggregates.

Immunophenotype

On flow cytometry, MPDCP is defined as ≥ 2% of non-erythroid nucleated cells in bone marrow and/or peripheral blood {30894665; 32871587}. The immunophenotype of MPDCP recapitulates that of mature pDCs (e.g. positive for CD123, TCF4, CD2AP, SPIB, CD303, CD304, and MX1) {24441662; 18218851; 12576313; 23165482}. Aberrant loss of markers expressed on normal pDCs (e.g. CD303 and TCL1) may be detected, and there may be aberrant expression of CD34, CD56, and TdT {33054115; 32871587}. The Ki-67 proliferation index in MPDCP associated with CMML is usually low {22895265}.

Differential diagnosis

Reactive mature pDC proliferations are often seen in settings other than myeloid neoplasms, such as infections, systemic lupus erythematosus, Kikuchi–Fujimoto disease, and Castleman disease; such cases should be excluded for the diagnosis of MPDCP.

 

Cytology

See above.

Diagnostic molecular pathology

No specific molecular or cytogenetic tests are required for the diagnosis of MPDCP.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: an accumulation of mature cells with plasmacytoid morphology and expression of CD123 and/or other pDC markers in the context of a defined myeloid neoplasm.

Desirable: an aberrant pDC immunophenotype; absent or low/partial expression of CD56.

Staging

Not applicable

Prognosis and prediction

The presence of MPDCP has been shown to correlate with adverse outcomes of the associated myeloid neoplasm in most studies {33152058; 32871587; 21883371}. Its identification may have therapeutic implications {32871587}.
