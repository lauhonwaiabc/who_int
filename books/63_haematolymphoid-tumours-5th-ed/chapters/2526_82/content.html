2.5.2.6 Acute leukaemia of ambiguous lineage NOS

Definition

Acute leukaemia of ambiguous lineage (ALAL) NOS expresses combinations of immunophenotypic lineage markers that do not permit definitive lineage assignments.

ICD-O coding

9805/3 Acute leukaemia of ambiguous lineage, NOS

ICD-11 coding

2A61 Acute leukaemias of ambiguous lineage

Related terminology

None

Subtype(s)

None

Localization

Bone marrow and peripheral blood are always involved.

Clinical features

Patients present with clinical features similar to those of other acute leukaemias, typically secondary to cytopenias.

Epidemiology

There are too few reported cases to determine epidemiological patterns of this leukaemia. It has been reported in children and adults {34540694}.

Etiology

Unknown

Pathogenesis

The pathogenesis of ALAL-NOS is largely unknown. Emerging data suggest that mutations in epigenetic regulators, as well as in components of the RAS and NOTCH pathways, may play a role {34540694; 34934076}. A recent study identified the presence of double RUNX1 mutations in ALAL {34540694}.

Macroscopic appearance

Not relevant

Histopathology

Blasts in the blood and bone marrow are usually immature and have no morphological features of differentiation.

Immunophenotype

Blasts do not express lineage-specific markers but rather combinations of markers that do not allow for classification as acute undifferentiated leukaemia, mixed-phenotype acute leukaemia, or leukaemia of a specific lineage. ALAL-NOS includes cases that express T-lineage markers such as CD5 and CD7 but without surface or cytoplasmic CD3, along with myeloid-associated antigens such as CD33 and CD13 (but without myeloperoxidase), and/or CD19 as a B-lineage marker but not other B-lineage markers. Care should be taken not to assign a case as ALAL-NOS on the basis of the expression of certain antigens with limited lineage specificity when such antigens are expressed dimly and/or partially. For example, a leukaemia expressing CD13, CD33, and KIT (CD117) along with CD7 and dim CD19 is more properly regarded as acute myeloid leukaemia (AML) with aberrant antigen expression.

 

Cytology

See above.

Diagnostic molecular pathology

Full cytogenetic and molecular evaluation is generally necessary before a designation of ALAL-NOS is rendered. Specifically, exclusion of BCR::ABL1 and KMT2A rearrangement is necessary.

Essential and desirable diagnostic criteria

Essential: ≥ 20% blasts in bone marrow and/or blood expressing combinations of immunophenotypic lineage markers that do not permit definitive lineage assignments; not fulfilling diagnostic criteria for mixed-phenotype acute leukaemia with defined genetic alterations.

Staging

Not relevant

Prognosis and prediction

ALAL-NOS is generally considered to have a poor prognosis, although data on outcomes remain limited {30956966}. The mutation burden may influence outcome {34934076}. Patients are most often treated with AML induction regimens, but anecdotal data suggest response to acute lymphoblastic lymphoma/leukaemia–directed therapy {23412561; 30956966}.
